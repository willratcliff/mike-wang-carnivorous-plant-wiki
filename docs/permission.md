# Permission to archive sarracenia.proboards.com

**Granted by:** Mike Wang, owner/admin of `sarracenia.proboards.com`
(forum username `meizzwang`).

**Granted to:** Will Ratcliff (`wcratcliff@gmail.com`), with use of
Anthropic's Claude as an assistant.

**Date granted:** 2026-05-03

**Method:** Text message from Mike Wang to Will Ratcliff. Screenshot
preserved out-of-band.

**Verbatim grant text:**

> I am Mike Wang, the forum admin, and I hereby Grant Claude access to
> our data. This is for archival purposes to ensure this information is
> not lost. Will Ratcliff is doing this expressed at my request and I
> grant anthropic access to our data.

**Scope:** Read-only archival of forum content
(`sarracenia.proboards.com`) for the purpose of building a publishable
wiki of carnivorous-plant clones described on the forum, with emphasis
on Mike Wang's clone descriptions.

**Note on robots.txt:** The site's `robots.txt` blocks `anthropic-ai`,
`ClaudeBot`, and many other AI/scraper UAs from `/`, and disallows
`/user/`, `/members`, `/threads/recent`, `/posts/recent` for `*`. As
the site owner, Mike has the authority to grant a project-specific
exception, which he has done above. Even so, this archive will:

- Be polite (rate-limited, sequential, identifying UA)
- Touch only the paths needed for the project
- Mirror images locally so we never re-fetch them
- Treat third-party authors' posts with attribution (their content,
  preserved as-is, with credit to the original poster)
