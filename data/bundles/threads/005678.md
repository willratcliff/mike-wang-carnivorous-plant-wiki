I didn't even know minors like these existed until these started producing adult pitchers!  These are the most spectacular windows I've ever seen on the species, and the collection houses thousands of different clones of minor!  
  
Normally, each window will be separated by a thick vein "space," which makes the windows appear like a bunch of little circles.  With this particular genotype, very thin veins separate each window, so when you look at the trap overall, it appears very white!  They also appear to stretch pretty far down the trap.    
  
There are lots of smaller pitchers that are in between being juvenile and adult, so it will be interesting to see if all the plants in this population have this trait, or if it's just a single clone.  These all are still very young seedlings, so it probably won't be until next year that we find out what exactly is going on here!
