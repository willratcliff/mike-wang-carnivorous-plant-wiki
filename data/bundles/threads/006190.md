Here's a site that I didn't know existed and have "passed on by" many times over the years!  It was full of diversity and interesting forms, and the reason I didn't know about it is because it's a bit dicey getting to the site!  For one, you have to climb down a really steep cliff: there are many sites that have cliffs and whatnot but this one was exceptional in terms of crappy terrain!  Either that or you can bush whack it through some very dense vegetation for about a quarter to half a mile, I decided to risk it and got down safely.  
  
Most plants were regular forms but a few were pretty interesting!
