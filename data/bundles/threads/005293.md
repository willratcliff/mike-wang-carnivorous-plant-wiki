I'm not sure I believe in the varietal name, but it's published, so I guess we'll just go with the flow for now!  As many of you guessed, alata var. ornata is a fancy way of saying alatas that have some decent venation on the exterior of the trap as well as on the lid and probably in some other places.    
  
Whatever the case may be, this makes me wonder, is it possible to breed a heavily black veined alata like flava var. ornata 'improved black veins'?  [sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/840/flava-ornata-improved-black-veins#:~:text=The%20original%20S](https://sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/840/flava-ornata-improved-black-veins#:~:text=The%20original%20S).,they%20are%20extremely%20slow%20growers.  Equally interesting, is it possible to create alata hybrids that have extremely heavy veins?  I don't know, it hasn't been done yet as far as I know, maybe someone someday somewhere with something in their mind (ie. a light bulb) will come up with such a hybrid!  In case you were wondering, I ran out ways to incorporate words that start with "some" into the previous sentence.
