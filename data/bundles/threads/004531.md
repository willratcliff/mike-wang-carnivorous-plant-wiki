Maybe there is a thread on this plant already, but I couldn't find it.  As we talked about somewhere in the thousands of threads on this forum, what happens when you get a better clone than best clone?  Oops, didn't think that far ahead.  
  
This clone originates from California Carnivores, and they acquired it via Bob Hanrahan.  I'm guessing Bob dug it out of the wild back in the 80's and then naturalized it on his property in Alabama.  Peter informed me, when he saw the plant growing in the field, it was solid red from head to toe, and after breeding with this plant extensively, I don't doubt that!  In cultivation, it's merely been a cuprea for me, and I've grown this exact clone for almost 20 years now.
