HI Kiwiearl,  
  
The moment I tilted the tray so the rubras NEVER sat in water, they stopped rotting.  The tayis on a slope so the other end is flooded with water, but it doesn't matter because the purp. purps. there don't care.  It seems some populations are more of a pain than others...jonesii's were the worst here, but now they aren't ever allowed to sit in water for more than 2 days, and all the rot is gone as well.  I've also observed that soils with mineral and salt buildup lead to rubras getting that fusarium wilt, so I leach them every so often to make sure they don't get that toxic buildup.
