S. flava var. atropurpurea 'surprisingly dark' Liberty Co, FL!     All red flavas from this County are extremely rare, and aside from the few clones I've teased out of my regular rubricorpora mother plants, I'm not sure any other genuine, DARK  atropurpureas from Liberty Co, FL exist in cultivation..   
   
  This clone is the result of crossing two different rubricorpora Liberty Co, FL clones with each other!  The resulting offspring were all over the place, there were rugelii's, regular, good looking rubricorporas, and some faded, watered down looking rubricorporas.   Out of around several hundred seedlings, there were only two dark ones, including this clone.  
  
With the dark clones, they tend to have some unusual traits, such as darker red phyllodia or even darker red sepals.  This clone produces  somewhat regular colored sepals and regular colored  phyllodia, yet it has surprisingly dark traps!
