Here's another nice clone of ornata that was the result of a huge seedling grow out I did many years ago.  4 different standout ornatas resulted and this is one of them!  The Okaloosa ornatas, at least from the gene pool that are in the collection, have a very strong propensity to have dark, well "filled in" red veins on the lid that appear mostly "clean" like you see in the black veined ornata from Bulloch Co, GA.  In other words, there's very little red pigmentation between the veins, which muddles the look.    
  
Like most ornata clones in cultivation, give this one too much light with zero root disturbance and the body will redden up.  Interestingly, when you divide this clone, don't give it full sun and disturb it, the body is sometimes more heavily veined looking!
