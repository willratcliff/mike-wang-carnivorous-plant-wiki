Hey Mike: I just want to touch base on this subject a bit. Reptilian Rose is not a sterile hybrid where pollen production is concerned. I have produced pollen on multiple occasions and have used it in several crosses. It is, however, very difficult to get the production started. After years of experimenting with this hybrid, even before I had it registered, I have come up with a program that works for me.  
  
After the flowers have fully matured and are just at the point of being a day or two from turning their noses upwards, I clip the flowers off and put them in a clean flask of RO water and refrigerate them for 3 days. I usually have multiple flasks going because it does take a dozen or more flowers to get a sufficient amount of pollen to work with.  
  
After 3 days have elapsed I take the flasks out of the fridge (which is in my garage and used almost exclusively for plant projects and beer!!. At this point I placed the flasks under very warn and bright greenhouse conditions. A polycarbonate coldframe works great for this stage. Do not let air circulate around the flowers too strongly and this could displace the few pollen grains that are produced.  
  
After a day or 2 under these conditions I take the flasks into my garage and close the door so no air is allowed in. Cupping the flowers in my hand I gently tap the top of them with the eraser end of a pencil dislodging the pollen grains. Carefully removing the petals and cutting off the umbrella I tap the pollen onto a piece of clean wax paper and let dry for an hour or so. Then I store the pollen in small glassine envelopes and refrigerate.  
  
I know, I know...it's an awful lot of work. But.....it worked and I did get pollen.
