I've had this clone in the collection for many years, and it turned out to be a good one!  Over time, it has formed a nice clump, and every pitcher seems to be holding the coppery color. There's a defined red blotch on the "neck" of the trap as well.  
  
In previous years, I made the mistake of cutting off all the winter phyllodia, and it seems like this may have caused the plant to not produce the coppery lids.  This last winter, I did not repot the plant, and left on all the winter leaves, and the plant turned out to be as colorful as can be!
