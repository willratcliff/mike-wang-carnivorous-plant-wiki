Here's the famous, classic clone that I think originated from Adrian Slack (feel free to correct me if you know otherwise).  All I know is that I've had this clone for probably close to 2 decades now, and it's a PITA to grow: rots easily, doesn't readily create sideshoots, and rarely performs well for me.  However, it has been an outstanding breeder plant!  
  
Extremely rare in the US, I don't think this plant will become widely available around here anytime soon, but a lot of work is being done to breed with it and produce plants that look even better!

some updated pics of this clone, photos taken 5/17/24.  Despite being in cultivation for quite some time, it hasn't been readily obtainable  in the US until somewhat recently and is still quite rare in collections around here.  It's not a super fast growing clone, so it takes some time to propagate, and there are reportedly a lot of fake versions floating around in Europe.  I haven't heard of complaints of fakes being circulated in the US, but we are keeping an eye out.  
  
Even though there are literally hundreds of different rubricorpora clones from the same population, this one still has   
"Je ne sais quoi" that really makes it stick out from the rest.
