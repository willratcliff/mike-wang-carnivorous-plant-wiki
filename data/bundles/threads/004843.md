FYI: The word "PISTE" doesn't mean anything other than a very obscure way to designate a specific location in Liberty Co, FL.  
  
Here's another unique s. flava var. ornata clone with intense, intricate red veins!  Red lips are an added bonus!  I'm almost positive this originated from a rubricorpora a few generations ago, but that's just a gut guess and conjecture at this point.   
  
   This clone has medium vigor, and as a smaller plant, the veins aren't as impressive compared to when the plant gets bigger.  This clone really does need optimal conditions to color up properly, and hopefully I'll have more impressive traps to photograph next season.  For now, here are some pics for the library.
