Don't be fooled if someone tells you that a lidless S. leucophylla Hurricane creek white exists.  These are mal-formed pitchers which likely resulted from either mechanical damage to the new forming growth point, some sort of environmental condition that caused abnormal growth, or a random single mutation on this particular pitcher:  
[http://s1110.photobucket.com/user/meizzwang/media/Apr%202013/IMG_0245_zps7df5e547.jpg.html](http://s1110.photobucket.com/user/meizzwang/media/Apr%202013/IMG_0245_zps7df5e547.jpg.html)  
  
  
[http://s1110.photobucket.com/user/meizzwang/media/Apr%202013/IMG_0244_zpse2118573.jpg.html](http://s1110.photobucket.com/user/meizzwang/media/Apr%202013/IMG_0244_zpse2118573.jpg.html)  
  
  
The other newly developing pitchers on this exact plant seem to be normal so far, so my guess is this bizzare leaf whatever you want to call it is not a permanent genetic mutation.
