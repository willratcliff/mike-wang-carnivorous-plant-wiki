Who would have thought Sarracenias would use their colors to help insects figure out where to land?  Here's a really unique moorei hybrid that has a red stripe on the top of the lid!  It makes you wonder what other color combinations and color patterns are possible with these plants!  I think in the near future, now that we have so many people interested in creating hybrids, we're going to start seeing things that we didn't even think was possible.  
  
This plant is literally still a seedling, so it'll be interesting to see how these colors develop as the traps get bigger and closer to vegetative maturity.  As of now, it appears the body is reddish, and there's a nice red throat with some coloration that goes up the interior of the lid.  The top of the lid is perhaps the most stunning aspect of this plant.  
  
While the name seems pretty self explanatory, it is what you think it is, LOL  Photos taken 4/14/17:
