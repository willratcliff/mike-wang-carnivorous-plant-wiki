Here is a legit, proven giant clone (seen unadulterated pics of this plant producing gigantic traps) that has so many different names that it's confusing!  HWG (true giant), Harald Weiner giant form,   C. follicularis ex. Harald Weiner, and probably a few other synonyms.    
  
So what's so special about this clone anyways?  Well, so far it does have this je ne sais quois look to it that seems distinct, but I can't quite describe it in words.  Maybe you'll see what I'm saying by looking at the pics below, or maybe you won't since many ceph. clones look almost identical until the trap sizes up or some environmental condition entices the traps to look different.  There's the whole debate as to whether or not clones even differ but I'd go on a limb and say ALL experienced growers who have grown out many different clones know there is without a doubt a difference!  It just takes a lot of experience and observation to see the differences.  
  
I haven't had this clone for more than a few months, so I don't really know much about it yet.  So far, even though this plant appeared to have just rooted when it was first acquired, it's already pushing out large traps on a relatively small plant.  The stem tuber was medium in diameter but very  short. Not all giant clones push out huge traps on such relatively small plants but if a clone does do that, it's a promising sign.  Bubble giant can also produce pretty large traps on plants that have just become established.
