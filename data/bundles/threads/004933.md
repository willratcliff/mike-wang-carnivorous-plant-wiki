In a few years, once people start copying MW 

![:)](//storage.proboards.com/forum/images/smiley/smiley.png) [not mirrored]

, they'll soon realize just how difficult it is to produce a good quality "atropurpurea" using 100% rubricorpora genetics.    I've tried so many different crosses, and only a few yielded anything worth while.  
  
I crossed this back grow giant pictured above with flava rubricorpora 'Burgundy' and found a stunning seedling!  Within the seed batch, most of the seedlings that appear to be solid red still have a little bit of yellow in the lid, like this one.  On a side note, notice the black, necrotic marks on the lid, that's from using toxic peat, which was around 200ppm.  As soon as I saw these burns, the peat was immediately leached:  

![](../../images/1f/1feea937ae266f9c10adf2a85f3d13a428d9cc60e564e03a8793ea1f22f74340.jpg)

  
  
Anyways, there is one seedling in the batch that is solid red from head to toe!  What's even more interesting is that compared to all the other rubricorpora seed crosses sown the exact same day, many seedlings from the back row giant x burgundy cross are almost 2X the height!  My hope is that this "atropurpurea" is not only all red, but also a GIANT!!!!  
  

![](../../images/2b/2bf24e201d15bce1cb8688def3a9c6fcd5f6be4adb0eb9a756086ed1d12bd1b0.jpg)
