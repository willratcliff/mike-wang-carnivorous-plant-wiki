Here's a clone of S. jonesii that originally came from Phil Sheridan.  This is the more "typical" looking jonesii that you see in cultivation these days, and a neat aspect of this particular individual is that it colors up nicely in the fall.  The plant is rather vigorous, and regularly produces offshoots:
