Hats off, this is the darkest red leuco that I have ever seen....so far!  There's a few other clones in the collection that might be darker, but they have solid white tops...oh wait, that actually sounds more interesting, so I'll leave it at that for another post once those plants start performing.  
  
Like every last single red variant of leucophylla in existence, I suspect the dark red coloration from this clone is of hybrid origin.  How "leuco dominant" a clone looks depends on how many generations away you step from that original hybrid cross and how many times the individual has been back-crossed to leucophylla.    
  
As you can see, this clone produces amazing spring pitchers, but the fall pitchers can get more intense in color.  I have never seen any red leuco both in the wild or in cultivation that gets this dark!  This clone sticks out like sore thumb even amongst many other oustanding plants.
