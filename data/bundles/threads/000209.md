After many years, I finally got around to dividing and transplanting my mother plant, so there's a new update.  Here's a blast from the past, a clone that originated from Dr. Phil...Sheridan!  As to where this clone originated from, nobody besides Dr. Sheridan knows for sure, but it really does look like an Okaloosa Co, FL genotype to me.  that is just speculation of course.  
  
Fortunately, there's no other clone in circulation that has been named S. flava var. ornata gulf coast, FL, and the features are quite unique.  Interesting fact: this clone has been extensively bred with and is the parent of all improved black veined plants!  Well, excluding Phil's Faulisi's improved version, hehe  
  
Just because it has been extensively bred doesn't mean we have taken these genetics to their fullest potential.  Given all the segregation that occurs with the offspring, I'm pretty certain this clone is the result of complex hybridization.  Given that fact, if you cross this plant with say this puppy: [sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/4503/flava-var-atropurpurea-okaloosa-yellow](http://sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/4503/flava-var-atropurpurea-okaloosa-yellow)  You'll probably have to run very large numbers before finding the gem we're seeking.  Have I made this cross yet?  Foolishly, no, just thought it up.  I bet somewhere in that combination, a ridiculously veined ornata is just itching to be found.  Maybe Leo will get there before I do!
