Yet another clone of S. minor var. okefenokeensis from the extirpated population in Ware Co, GA.  Clone Q generally produces coppery heads, and one thing I noticed is the traps look more shapely when they are giant in comparison to when they are smaller.  This is a relatively fast growing plant, and it consistently is giant every year there are favorable growing conditions.  The rhizome on the mother plant is ridiculously big-same can be said for all okee giant plants with giant traps.
