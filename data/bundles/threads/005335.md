Occasionally, when you make enough crosses and grow out enough seedlings, one will pop up that looks very different from the rest!  Here's a new clone of hurricane creek white that is the result of crossing two very genetically different clones with each other!  This particular cross yielded hundreds of plants, but this was the only one that had such "stretchy" white coloration on the trap!  It's still a seedling, but there's definitely some dividable material, so I'm going to get this into circulation hopefully by next summer or fall of 2022.
