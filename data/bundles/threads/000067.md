Here's a clone I originally got from meadowview perhaps a decade ago.  Unlike many other leucophylla clones, it produces a decent set of pitchers during the summer, and then a huge flush of pitchers in the fall.  
  
During the fall, when the pitchers are first formed, the color isn't all that great, but a few weeks of sunny days and cold nights produces pitchers that look like this:
