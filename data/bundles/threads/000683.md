S. minor var. okefenokeensis Ware Co, GA clone E, in my opinion, produces some of the best-shaped traps!  It has great symmetry throughout the entire leaf structure.  The bronzy-red tinge to the "heads" also adds a unique character to it.  This clone generally stays small for an okee giant (bigger than a regular minor), but it has historically produce 2ft tall traps!     Its medium-vigor growth habit means producing clones takes some time.  This clone hasn't been widely distributed as a result, but hopefully, we'll see it in more collections in the near future.
