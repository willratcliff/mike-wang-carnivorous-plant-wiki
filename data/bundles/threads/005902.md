Turns out this clone needs its own thread!  I've never had great success taking pictures of this clone and have had it in the collection for more than a decade!  Last winter, the mother plant was divided and here's a picture of one of the divisions!  
  
When it comes to these neon pink mitchellianas, the first thing that comes to mind is pink leucophyllas?    Can we make neon pink leucophyllas using neon pink mitchellianas?  Well, get ready for a 3-15 year project if you want to try it out, it'll likely take at least 2-3 generations to complete and a lot of good guess work as to what "pathway" to take.   Of course, there are many other potential hybrids that can be made with this clone.  This neon pink trait is very desirable and probably dominant in many different crosses.  
  
Anyways, these are the best pics I was able to get of this clone so far, and it's not exactly what you see in person (they're a bit more neon) but it's pretty darn close.  Pics taken 10/3/23:
