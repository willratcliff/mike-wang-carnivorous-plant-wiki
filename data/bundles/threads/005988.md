The stone Co, MS population is filled with giant forms, but in cultivation so far, it seems as though there are only a few individuals that are deemed worthy of being labeled a giant.  While the traps do get big, what is unusual about this population are the plants' propensity to produce massive CLUMPS of huge traps!  That said, this one is probably not one of them, but it has some great looking traps with white spots on it, so this was selected out and propagated!   
  
The traps are bright red, shapely, and overall really nice looking!  There's also a bit of white below the mouth, which much like leucophylla, varies year to year and environment to environment.  S. psittacina 'white spots' Stone Co, MS, pics taken 4/3/24:
