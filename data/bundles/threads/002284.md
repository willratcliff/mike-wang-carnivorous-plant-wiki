Here's an outstanding S. x moorei originally from our buddy Jeremiah Harris.  This particular clone can produce bright reddish pitchers under optimal conditions, and even after being recently transplanted, it still looks decent!  The traps take a little bit of time to open: they're about 2-3 weeks behind the flavas.  This is my first season of being able to see this plant produce acclimated traps, so it'll be interesting to see what this thing can do outdoors this fall!
