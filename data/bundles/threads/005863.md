These traps are pretty much at the end of their "life," most of the pitchers have turned brown and of course the rhizome is now completely dormant.  These will all be chopped off pretty soon all the way to the ground to make sure the new pitchers for next season have the maximum amount of light they can get.  Rubras in general benefit from "deadheading" all the way to the ground: not all species appreciate it.  
  
Photos taken 11/19/24.  Some luscious black lips!

Rubras in general benefit from "deadheading" all the way to the ground: not all species appreciate it.  

Any advice on which species don't appreciate it?

A few off the top of my head...  Purps (including Roseas), Minors, Psittacina all do much better usually if you allow them to hold onto their late summer and fall pitchers until after their spring and summer traps have come out.  
  
Some of their hybrids as well (Mitchelliana, Catesbaei, Excellens, Wrigeleyii, etc.) do better with this strategy.
