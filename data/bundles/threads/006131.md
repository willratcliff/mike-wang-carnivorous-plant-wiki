I made a ton of flava x leucophylla crosses and for the sake of saving space, all of the seeds were pooled together and then sown together.  AFter growing out a massive bunch of seedlings, the first thought was "huh": it's surprisingly not easy to land a good looking one!  Most were mutt looking aka not colorful, dull, and otherwise a waste of time.  A few were colorful but not strongly colored.    
  
In the end, well at least so far, this is all I have to show from that project: it was almost a waste of time!  That said, this one is a pretty average, standard looking moorei and it might color up better as it ages so for now it's going to be kept and grown for Jesus to see if it's anything special.  The lid is pretty darn white so that's a good start!
