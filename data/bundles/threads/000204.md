As many of you know, S. flava var. rubricorpora clone L Liberty Co, FL is a proven breeder clone that is responsible for countless choice species and hybrid clones in cultivation.  I feel there is still some amazing potential that hasn't yet been realized!  As a little history, this clone entered cultivation in 1998 and I acquired it in the early 2000's from Art Junier!  During the 20 or so years that I've been growing this clone, it has only divided a few times and is VERY slow growing.  If you got your hands on this clone, consider yourself lucky, since there's probably only 4-5 divisions floating around in circulation!    Anyways, this clone can really get dark, but hybrids made from this plant have been even darker!  I have since acquired several other dark rubricorporas which are a bit darker than this classic clone.
