Here's another "cross" that's been performing year after year, yet a homie never got around to photo-documenting it until this morning!  So what do you get when you mix up two really nice cupreas together?  You get:  
1) extreme vigor  
2) very well defined coppery lids (with the help of selective pressure of course.  Not all seedlings were great)  
3) disease resistance.  This cross is tough to kill and doesn't want to rot easily.  It's also quite freeze tolerant, taking down to 21F without any damage whatsoever.  It can probably take even cooler temperatures.
