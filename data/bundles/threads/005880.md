It looks like Mikes clone has done a little shape shifting, below is an image of the same plant from 2021. The site this specific plant came from was severely damaged several years ago but has been relatively undisturbed since then and has become somewhat stable. However, the number of Alata there now is less than half of what it used to be and all Psittacina are gone. The large clone that you have, Dan, is actually from a different bog that is actively being destroyed as I write this. Half of it is completely gone and destruction of the other half is well underway. Both sites are part of the same "bog complex" I guess, but they are right at a mile away from each other. These areas really had some interesting forms of Alata and though I'm sure there are more Psittacina somewhere in Lamar Co MS, I have been unable to find any more after the ones at the "POKR" bog (Source of Mikes Alata in this post) were extirpated.
