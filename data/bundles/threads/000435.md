The old Dock purps. have an incredible amount of diversity to them.  Some plants look like the bulbous Brunswick Co, NC plants, while others are very colorful and have nice contrasting colors.  
  
Clone H is one of the most unique looking plants from this population in the collection.  It has a slightly bulbous yet slim-looking trap, and almost reminds me of S. purpurea ssp. purpurea.  On the other hand, the  dark red veins in the "lid" with the nice contrasting green background seem very "montana"-like.  The colors are becoming more pronounced now that we have very cool nights and sunny days.  While there are many amazing clones from this population, this individual sticks out to me:
