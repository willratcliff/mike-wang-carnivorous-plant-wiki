Here's a single red individual out of hundreds of leucophylla seedlings that is currently sticking out of the crowd for its stretchy red coloration.  What's especially notable is the fact that the red coloration is well defined and well pronounced even on spring traps, which is not common with leucophylla!  
  
A lot of the times, you can't tell if a seedling this size will be something note worthy as it grows up, but from deep past experience breeding for red leucophyllas, this one has a very high chance of becoming one of the best red wild-type leucophylla clones out there.    
  
No name has been given to this clone yet and it's still under observation, will keep this thread updated as it grows bigger and shows its true colors.  For now, it's called S. leucophylla RED 'new clone' Covington Co, AL, pics taken 6/22/23:
