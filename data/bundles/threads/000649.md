Here's an interesting S. flava from Lexington Co, SC.  The population here is supposedly S. flava var. rugelii, but perhaps the red throat isn't as pronounced on some clones such as the one below.  Unfortunately, despite coming from a histically strong and decently big population,  I've heard the population in the wild was recently damaged by beavers in the past few years.  The status of these plants in the wild is unknown.  
  
This is a relic population of S. flavas since it's pretty isolated and far away from "the main" flava populations in South Carolina.  No doubt, these genetics are unique.
