That is absolutely peculiar Eric, thanks for sharing your results with us!  Only thing I can think of is that your grow season weather outdoors is pretty close to what it's like in the wild.  Plants in the wild get as colorful as can be.  On the pacific Northwest, plants grown in greenhouses compared to outdoors are much warmer on average (both day and night), more humid, and some light rays that otherwise bleach out anthocyanins  are filtered out.
