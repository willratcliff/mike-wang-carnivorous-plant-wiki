If some of you remember the story, there are 2 origins of S. leucophylla Hurricane creek white: Barry Rice's seed grown individuals that were directly from the site before it was destroyed (and to clarify things, John Hummer collected seeds) and then plants were collected and grown in John Hummer's collection.  Fast forward almost a decade later, I was able to acquire some open pollinated seeds from John Hummer and grew them out.    
  
These open pollinated Hummer Hurricane creek white seedlings have since matured and they've been around for I don't know, maybe 6 years now.  Last year, I saw a few individuals that looked decent, but nothing comparable to the original seed grown individuals from Barry...well, that all changed this year when I found this stunning winner!  You can tell this clone is unique with how far the white goes down the petiole, and the trap is both symmetrical and shapely.  Another great aspect is that this clone is not a direct descendant of Barry's seeds, but perhaps this one is a close cousin, so we can conclude this individual will add some diversity to the gene pool.  All of the other open pollinated individuals from John Hummer have yet to shine, but knowing it  has taken about 6 years to find the winner below, I'll still keep an eye on them.
