here's a clone that was selected out of the population years ago, but it was grown surrounded by taller Sarracenia and always stayed green for probably a good 10 years.  About 2 years ago, it was divided, given some fresh soil, and placed into blasting full sun, so now it's finally starting to color up again!    
  
This clone is extremely vigorous and forms enormous clumps over time.  It's super easy to grow for a rosea.
