Here's a clone that has been in the collection for decades, but not much has been done in terms of propagating it until recently.  The traps look strikingly similar to S. rubra wherryi Chatom Giant" [sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/241/rubra-wherryi-chatom-giant-washington](https://sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/241/rubra-wherryi-chatom-giant-washington)  , except they don't get as large, and maybe they get a bit darker as they age.  In fact, it looks so similar to chatom giant that Some of you might be thinkin that MW is trippin and mis-labeled this plant, but 4 show, he ain't trippin, ID is guaranteed!  Quite confident that MW ain't slippin either  (that's "rapper diction" incorporated into nerdy plant talk).      
  
From this population, there are tons of hybrids, and I have a hunch (back of Notre Dame) that this particular red individual is the result of introgression, but because it has been sufficiently back-crossed, the only difference you can see is the color.  Most of the regular forms from this site are greenish!  Pics taken mostly on  9/18/22:
