Here's yet another clone of S. minor var. okefenokeensis from Ware Co, GA that produces exceptional traps and grows very strong for me year after year.  I find this plant has more phenotypic variance than other clones: the shape of the traps seem more symmetrical one year compared to the next.  Also, sometimes, this plant produces hoods that dip downwards and on the same plant, another trap will have less of a downward angled lid.  Are these differences in physical appearance due to  difference in size of the trap, is it the environment, or both?   
  
  
  
I dunno, but in either case, this plant looks pretty neat!  Check out the copper lid with the slightly bronzy body, the color is also something to talk about.
