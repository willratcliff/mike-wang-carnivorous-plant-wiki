Here's a really interesting clone that I acquired from Noah Juve, the original source traces back to the late Allen Lowrie, who was one of maybe 3 consistent sources of the past that introduced most of what we have in cultivation.   It's one of the few that no matter what you do, the traps stay small!  It's a true dwarf clone!  Nevertheless, the plant is vigorous as heck and produced an explosion of growth last season!  While I haven't had this plant for very long, it also appears to be a darker clone than the average, more observation is needed to confirm this.  
  
This one is an absolute joy to cultivate, it's not as finicky as some other clones like big boy.  My guess is if there's some sort of environmental stress in the future, this will be one of the few to survive it:
