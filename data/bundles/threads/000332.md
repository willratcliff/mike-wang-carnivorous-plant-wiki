I get what you're saying, but from this distance and given where it comes from, I'd possibly call it a flava var. flava, albeit one toward the extreme end of the veining range even though it does have branching veins. There is a lot of enthusiasm out there for var. ornata these days. But var. flava has plenty of veins too.  
  
My hunch is you got this from within seed supplied as flava var. flava. Correct?  
  
I have plants from the same local that are flava var. flava with mucho veins too, but I still think of them as var. flava. Having said that my ones don't have branching veins. Maybe that makes yours a none too common Carolina var. ornata?  
  
Well, that's probably the best non-answer I've ever given!!!!  
  
In the end, I think there are so many blends within the demarcation between today's varieties that the naming could go on forever.
