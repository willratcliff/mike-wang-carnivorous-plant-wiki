Here are some fall pitchers from S. leucophylla Hurricane creek white clone E.  Just like all the hurricane creek whites in the collection, this particular clone grows very fast and produces nice specimen sized plants. Also, it sometimes produces a solid white interior, while at other times, it has green veins on the interior, depending on the environmental factors:
