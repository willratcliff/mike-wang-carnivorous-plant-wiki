I'm surprised that I haven't made a thread on this yet, so here goes nothing!  purpurea ssp. purpurea, for those of you who love this species, is unfortunately a PITA to keep alive long term (eg. past 10 years).  Maybe that's just my personal experience, and I know some other growers out there have had great success with plants that I've completely killed over time.  
  
 That said, if I'm able to keep them alive for a very long time, that means they're pretty bullet proof, and this clone from Huron Co, ON is one of those bullet proof genotypes!  I've had this clone for almost 20 years now and it's still kickin!
