Space is usually a limiting factor in the collection, and having everything packed in like sardines forces them to stay a certain size. That may be true in a community tray or community pot, but when you pot plants up individually and give them room, they can get pretty big.  
  
Here's an un-named S. rosea clone from Liberty Co, FL busting out of a 3.5" square pot!  This plant will easily fill a 6" pot, and it's not uncommon  for S. rosea to get this big (this isn't a giant clone):
