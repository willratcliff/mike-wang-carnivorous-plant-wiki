Okay fellow Sarracenia junkies, I've taken a long break from photo-documenting the collection, but now after resting for a good 2 months, I'm ready to share some more pics with you!   Here's another new and never seen before black lipped S. alata!  So far, we've seen black lips in S. jonesii, rubra ancestral, flava ornata, and now in alata.  Perhaps these genes are much like anthocyanin free variants in the sense that they're rare but found in many species.     Aside from the black lips, the shape on this clone is pretty neat!
