Here's an interesting clone that reminds me a little bit of hurricane creek white, except not quite.  This plant is probably an alba, but I won't call it such until that's proven.  The most unique feature of this clone is the fact that the green body of the pitcher turned almost neon yellow last fall!  We'll see if that was more environmental or genetic, but I haven't ever seen any other clone "behave" that way before.   Other plants in the same exact tray, with the same water, soil, weather, etc. didn't display the neon yellow coloration.
