Here's anew selection of pink leucophylla that has a little bit more white on it than most other clones!  Maybe this clone will be a bit more impressive once it reaches vegetative maturity, it's not quite there yet but still, the traits are very distinguishable at this stage. S. leucophylla 'pink and white' Baldwin Co, AL, pics taken 11/14/23:
