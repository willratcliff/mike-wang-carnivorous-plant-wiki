Overall, this is  the best I've ever been able to do with these plants!  I have a moderate amount of clones, they're quite challenging to come by, people don't want to cough them up LOL  I get it though, these aren't the easiest plants to propagate, and just when you thought you figured them out, many plants rot out.  You generally want to have at least one backup plant.  2 years ago, I lost almost all of my mother plants but was able to keep the genetics going because I had at least one backup plant.    Fingers crossed these don't crash!
