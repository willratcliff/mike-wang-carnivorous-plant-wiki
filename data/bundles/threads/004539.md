Here's an unusual clone that I've extensively bred with but it's been a bit of a challenge to get it to color up consistently.  Why breed with a plant that is difficult to color up?  Because this plant has unique genes that haven't otherwise been worked with, and those traits are useful for creating unique hybrids.  The color up difficult thing can be corrected in future generations.  
  
  The shape of this clone is very different from other flavas, and the lid can get really big relative to the rest of the trap!  Under optimal conditions, the throat can become solid red, but it's a bit different than extreme red throat....maybe more like flava rubricorpora solid red throat.
