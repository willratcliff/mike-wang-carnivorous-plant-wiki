Here's a "newly discovered" clone that I just noticed despite having in the collection for more than a decade!  Truth be told, there are probably hundreds of others in the collection just waiting to be discovered: the challenge is, they're all crammed into community trays so they don't have space to really put on their best show.  Going to make more space in the future and hopefully will find more interesting clones like this one to select out and propagate.  
  
This red lipped trait isn't uncommon, it's been found in Walton Co, FL, probably Santa Rosa Co, FL, Baldwin Co, AL...it's likely present from any location where red leucos occur.  That said, the red coloration on this clone really pops, it's pretty "loud" so to speak.
