Some of you were probably thinking, "dude Mike, how did you come up with such an interesting Italian name?"  Real Italians (assuming they don't speak english and are google translating this thread) are probably scratching their heads thinking "huh?  Effini?  Is Mike Wang an imposter trying to pose as someone who knows our language?."  
  
Sorry to get your hopes up high ladies and gentlment: I'm not the artsy, creative genius-type that can come up with crafty names like Phil Faulisi and other talented growers do, I'm just a simple man with a potty mouth!  Effini is just a ghetto-rigged acronym that means EFFIN Incredible!  LOL    
  
This is a complex moorei x mooorei hybrid, both parents from  Okaloosa Co, FL, so it's true to location.  Much like Sarracenia 'okaloosa', most of the offspring were trash, but this one indivdiual stood out of the crowd because:  
1) it has incredible vigor and is easy to grow  
2) great, big, colorful spring traps   
3) nice, well defined red throat under optimal conditions.  
  
If you like Leah Wilkerson, you'll like Effini!  
  
I admit, the pictures below don't do this plant justice, but people want to get an idea of what this plant looks like, so here it is!  Again, the plant is not at its prime in terms of appearance, that red throat is more well defined under optimal conditions, maybe it'll color up more as the traps age.  These just opened up a week or so ago.  
  
  
Photos taken 4/20/19, check out the details of that lid!  Red veins stenciled in and whatnot, didn't notice that until I looked at the photo:
