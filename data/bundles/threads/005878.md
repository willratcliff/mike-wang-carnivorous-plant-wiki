here's a cross between two genetically different clones from the same population!  I made this cross more than 10 years ago, kept the seeds in cold storage, and popped them maybe 5 or 6 years ago because most of the mother plants died out when I moved unfortunately.  In any case, these are starting to look pretty nice this season: they were freshly transplanted and divided last winter, but appear as though they haven't skipped a beat!  In a lot of cases, when you selectively breed plants, they're a bit more vigorous and grower friendly compared to the original wild types.
