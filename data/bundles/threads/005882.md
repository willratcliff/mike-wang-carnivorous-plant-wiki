Here's a new confirmed BLACK  alata that was recently  "found" after growing out literally thousands of plants from multiple different poplations!  There probably are more hiding in the collection just waiting to be found.    Interestingly enough, this plant is absolutely shaded by surrounding traps and is still coloring up, which is unusual!  It's not yet at its fullest color potential, but what you see below is sufficient enough to know that it is a black clone.  It will be isolated and propagated in the near future, this is growing in a community tray.  
  
Black clones of alata are significant: they don't often breed true and there are only a handful of genetically different clones of black alatas in cultivation.  These are critical building blocks for future Sarracenia hybrids, especially those trying to breed BLACK hybrids!
