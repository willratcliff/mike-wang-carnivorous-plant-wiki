here's a "newer" version of the moorei extreme red throat improved called "2.0" just to make the name sound nerdy and official.  This is a different direction than the moorei extreme red throat improved, which took a more alata-esque turn with more white-ish coloration on the outside.  This one sticks to the classic moorei shape and color overall but has a very well defined red throat and a little more color on the outside compared to the original moorei extreme red throat.  
  
For reference, here's moorei extreme red throat: [sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/4163/sarracenia-moorei-extreme-red-throat](https://sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/4163/sarracenia-moorei-extreme-red-throat)  
and here's moorei extreme red throat improved, which sometimes can be a bit challenging to color up: [sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/5254/moorei-extreme-red-throat-improved](https://sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/5254/moorei-extreme-red-throat-improved)
