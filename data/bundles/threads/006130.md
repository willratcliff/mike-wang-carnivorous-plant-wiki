I've been growing out this seed batch for a while, walking by it year after year, noticing a very yellow individual but not thinking anything of it.  When my wife walked up to the collection the other day (she only goes out there if there's an emergency or if something's wrong) so I asked her what sort of natural disaster did she find out about and she said nothing, if we want to make it to the bakery in time to get some really nice local bread, we had to leave now!  I guess this was the one off situation where it wasn't an emergency, phew!  
  
As she was saying this, we were standing over the collection and she said  "wow, these plants actually look pretty nice, they really filled in since I last was up here [over a month ago]!  BTW this yellow one is beautiful!"  When those last words came out of her mouth, this is when I realized this clone might be something special: my wife is super critical, so if she says something looks nice in her eyes, then there is a chance that others might think the same thing.  I personally have little to no eye as to what people think looks nice.  
  
Below are pictures of the most yellow individual from this particular seed batch, it's a cross between a very yellow flava and a leucophylla var. alba!  I haven't propagated this plant yet but if there is interest I will make some divisions this coming winter.    
  
S. Golden Moorei, photos taken 6/17/25.  These are crammed in a community tray: if the traps are allowed to get light all the way to the soil line they turn solid yellow from head to toe:
