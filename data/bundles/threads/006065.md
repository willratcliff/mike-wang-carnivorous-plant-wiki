Everyone has been chasing the famous brunswick beauty clone, and for this very reason, I bred the EF out of it, trying to see if something equally nice or better could be made.  Many of the crosses are still tiny seedlings or are still being evaluated, but one of the earliest crosses I did was Brunswick Beauty 2.0.  This involved the old, extinct 1990's brunswick beauty clone, which didn't have very frilly lids like the "new" clone that is now circulating.  I acquired the original Brunswick Beauty clone from Art Junier, but around 2014, lost it from a virulent fungal pathogen that killed off around 70% of my purpurea collection!  The original brunswick beauty clone was notoriously slow growing and very rot prone, and it didn't have as frilly of a lid, while the "new" clone circulating today is incredibly vigorous and seemingly resistant to rot.  The main thing that the two clones share in common is that incredible red color!    
  
Brunswick Beauty 2.0 is the result of crossing the old extinct 1990's clone with S. purpurea ssp. venosa and then crossing that to the new brunswick Beauty plant!  It's essentially 2/3 brunswick beauty and 1/3 other venosa.  Only a few of the offspring looked like brunswick beauty, so I selected those out, propagated them and here you have brunswick beauty 2.0!  These are super vigorous, easy to grow, and look almost identical to the new brunswick beauty.
