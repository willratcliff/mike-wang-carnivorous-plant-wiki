Completely forgot to put together a thread for this population, so here it is!  I absolutely love these genotypes, there's an enormous amount of diversity, and if I had to sum up the unique features of the population as a whole, I'd say they look like Okaloosa Co, FL leucos except these have a lot of red forms in the mix, quite a few albas (which are super hard to color up), and have quite a few individuals that have some serious girth to them, even with short traps!
