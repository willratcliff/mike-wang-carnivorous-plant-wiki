So, apparently, there's 3 different atropurpurea clones that all look almost exactly the same: MK F27A, MK F27B, and MK F27C.  Mike King informed me that  he suspects "the original" clone is MK F27A.  The others might be the same clone, or they could be seedlings of the original clone.    I think this confusion stemmed from vendors distributing multiple seedling look-alikes under the same name.  
   
For those of you who have Waccamaw, burgundy, and other highly sought after Sarracenia, I encourage you all to continue with  your breeding experiments, but when it comes to distribution, you'll save a lot of anger, frustration, and bad ju-ju by giving a seedling that looks almost identical to the parent a different name....and as an added incentive, the nerdy Sarracenia community is getting very well educated and they're pretty ruthless when they confirm imposters: [sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/3961/leah-wilkerson](https://sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/3961/leah-wilkerson)  
  
While I respect others who disagree with this, if people really want those seedlings that look very similar to the highly sought after clone , I don't think there's anything wrong with distributing them, so long as they're given the appropriate name (ie. waccamaw F2, burgundy F2, rubricorpora Burgundy x best clone, rubricorpora 'ultimate dark x burgundy, etc).  that's legit because you're being very transparent on the genetics of what you're distributing and you're not cheating the buyer by using the cultivar name for something that is clearly a seedling of a sought after cultivar.

While I agree that the 27A-C clones have a definite look that could be from Okaloosa county, neither Mike King nor Phil Wilson (where F27A originated from) recorded the county designation as far as I know. I communicated with Phil Wilson a while back, and he stated that he originally obtained two blackwater clones (PW F2 and PW F3) a very long time ago from Alan Hindle who was the one who had them exported to europe/UK. Mike King has MK F27A listed as PW F3 in his growlist. Here's a May and July shot of MKF27A and PWF3 side by side.
