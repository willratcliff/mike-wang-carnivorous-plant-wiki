I don't remember if the name on this thread is correct, so I may  have to amend the title  once I read the tag.  The unique feature of this plant is the red interior of the trap!  Out of literally hundreds of plants, this was the only to have this specific unique characteristic from this population.  are there any other red leucophyllas out there with this characteristic?  Maybe, but I can't think of any off the top of my head.    
  
The traps pictured aren't yet at it's fullest color potential, which is interesting because the traps last year were much more darker red in the interior than they are now!  Maybe this clone will color up as these traps age..  Usually it's the other way around: larger traps color up easier than smaller traps!  
  
Photos taken 10/25/18:  
Check out how dark the splotches of pigments are on the left and right hand side of the mouth.  With good breeding work, I bet you can amplify these characteristics.  Building block genetics are typically not very showy compared to the hybrids you can make from them:
