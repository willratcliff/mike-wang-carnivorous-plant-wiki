Couldn't fit the whole name in the title, so here it is: S. flava var. rubricorpora ( back row dark x newest dark) Liberty Co, FL!  Here's another story of crossing two very dark clones with each other with the hopes of getting darker plants or atropurpureas, but so far none of the offspring turned out to be as dark as either of the parents.  There still could be some really dark ones in there, we'll have to wait and see after the traps age for a bit.   I think I got one or two atropurpureas isolated out from this batch.  Out of the remaining seedlings, after very strict culling, there's nothing but really high quality plants that color up relatively easily!      Yes, it took a very long time to get to this point (5 years) and yes, these plants are  smaller than they should be by now because they were never given the space they need to size up, but at least this goes to show that breeding with good parents, along with very strict selective pressure, can lead to some excellent results!
