I believe Vintner's Treasure is from the amazing moorei cross seed pod Phil made between leuco purple lips and his breeder ornata. The single seed pod I think is responsible for VT, Saurus, Hot Lips, Super Heavy Veins and many more amazing Moorei! Just goes to show what knowing the genetics of your parent plants can do for producing amazing offspring. Hopefully one day I'll get to that level! Still a lot left to learn  
  
I'm not fortunate enough to grow VT yet, but I do grow Super Heavy Veins, Hot Lips and Phil Clone 7 and they are all among the nicest plants in my collection. To answer [hungry_plants](/user/331) 's question - it looks like VT is named after Phil's favorite Paph. orchid hybrid. Taken from the cultivar description here:  
  
[legacy.carnivorousplants.org/cpn/Species/v42n4p145_152.html#vitner](https://legacy.carnivorousplants.org/cpn/Species/v42n4p145_152.html#vitner)
