figured it's time to make a separate thread for this standout individual!  In case you want to know the history of how this clone got into cultivation, you can read up on the thread here, there's also some pics of this exact clone from previous years: [sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/4711/leucos-bay-calhoun-county](https://sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/4711/leucos-bay-calhoun-county)  
  
  
Given that this individual produces a lot of white coloration on the traps (it stretches pretty far down) and its relative ease of growth, it has been isolated and propagated!   One unusual trait is that even the smaller divisions have exceptional looking traps: normally, they don't look great until the pitchers size up on mature specimens during the fall.  I personally haven't seen any other individual clone like this, so it may be a one of a kind type deal.
