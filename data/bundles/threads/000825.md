Here's a selection that I originally got from Brooks Garcia of Sarracenia obsessed []()[www.sarraceniaobsessed.com/](http://www.sarraceniaobsessed.com/)    This clone has a very strange arching lid, and can turn dark purple to almost black under optimal conditions.  The traps can grow extremely tall, perhaps more than 3 ft!  When I first received the plant, the trap was jaw-dropping in terms of height...almost unbelievable.  It is now producing relatively tall pitchers, although not as tall as when I originally received it.  Perhaps it needs more time to acclimate.
