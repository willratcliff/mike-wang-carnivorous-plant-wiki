I was gonna call this clone S. flava var. rugelii 'Buck Toothed Beauty' but I had buck teeth as a child before having braces, so I didn't want anyone to feel self conscious when checking this clone out.  But in all honesty, I don't negatively judge based on the buck teeth (mike wang slicks back his hair, or whatever is left growing on his bald head), I think it's an asset and a unique,  nice look for rugelii's!  It also has a unique shape to the lips/mouth.  
  
Another nice feature of this clone is the fact that it gets GIIIIAAAAANT, but obviously it's not producing giantic traps this season.  Hopefully it will next season, it was shaded by some bigger surrounding plants, got them all crammed in this section.  It has moderately high vigor, but I'd say it's something between a hybrid vigor giant and genetic giant.
