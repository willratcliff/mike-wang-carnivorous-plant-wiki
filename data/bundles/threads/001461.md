This is one  of my favorite hybrids so far from The Lily Garden! It has a standard oriental lily smell to it, although it's light and not too heady. Plant is susceptible to burning in full sun, and should be in part shade during heat waves. Staking definitely required.  You'd think I'd already have this figured out since I grew oriental lilies commercially almost a decade ago, but nope, had to reinvent the wheel since every lily hybrid has it's own unique requirements.  
  
These flowers are really big, although it's hard to tell from the photo:
