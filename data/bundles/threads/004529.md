This was definitely a surprise.  In the section of plants that were neglected and not trimmed on time, I ended up hitting the whole section up with a weed wacker, which made an enormous mess that was hard to clean up because my setup isn't set up for that.   It had to be done though, and there was no time to trim for Jesus, Matthew, Mark, Luke, or John.    It was a thick mess of dead leaves, and if it wasn't cleared up, many pots would either have etiolated pitchers or serious trouble emerging.  
  
  Two months later, I noticed a stand out cuprea in this mix of random plants, but I  wasn't sure what the heck it was.  The pot was inspected, and in there were probably 40 runt-looking seedlings all crammed together, with one giant individual!  I'm going to cull all those little seedlings and keep just this one plant below!  
  
This plant looks like an improved version of S. flava var. cuprea 'Chocolate top': it has a stronger colored copperty top (presumably from Botanique), but maintained great shape from Chocolate top, and probably inherited that striking neon yellowish-green interior from Botanique!
