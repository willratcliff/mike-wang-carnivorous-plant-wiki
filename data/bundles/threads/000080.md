Here's another picture of a very well managed site, filled with S. leucophylla and S. flava.  These pictures are from several years back, and it's hard to say how the populations look today.  Certainly, when I visited some sites in the wild recently, most looked very stressed out from the drought and overgrown.  Perhaps the down economy has taken a harsh toll on our ability to manage the lands down there.   
  
This site looks amazing because it was being burned and maintained-when fire doesn't come through for many years, many bogs become overgrown and the pitcher plants get squeezed out by competition.  Anyways, enjoy this photo taken by Brooks Garcia!
