Here's another giant clone of atropurpurea from Okaloosa Co, FL that hasn't yet been photodocumented.  Like the name implies, this clone can get absolutely, cartoon-like GIGANTIC under optimal conditons.  It's also quite shapely and solid red from head to toe.  Like most atropurpureas from this location, the traps tend to color up all the way once they age a bit.  
  
S. flava var. atropurpurea Giant Red Okaloosa Co, FL, pics taken 6/19/24.  This is actually a medium sized division and isn't full sized, but is still worthy of pics:
