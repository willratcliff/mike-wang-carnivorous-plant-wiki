IT wouldn't be surprising if the throat turned solid red on this one.  For years, this plant didn't look all that interesting because it was crowded by mature plants and didn't get full sun.  Recently, I moved it to a sunnier, uncrowded position and now it's looking really nice. The shape is also something worth noting.
