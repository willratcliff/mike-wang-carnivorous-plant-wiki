Yeah, yeah, you don't think this is an alba because the mouth isn't solid white.  well, give me a few months and I'll hopefully have pictures of the fall traps, it has been solid white in the past.  
  
There isn't really much to say about this clone just yet, but I'm starting to think there might be some decent stretch on it!  This plant has been in a pot that was surrounded by taller plants for many years, so it never really got the chance to shine.  This year, I made divisions and they're getting some blasting, unobstructed full sun.  This is the first year the plant has entered circulation.
