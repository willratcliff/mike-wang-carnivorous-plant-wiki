California Carnivores is home to an incredible diversity of CP's, and one plant that's not always so obviously present in their collection is S. leucophylla.  For most of the year, this species does almost nothing more than produce phyllodia, but then in the fall, there's a nice bloom of pitchers.   A large number of the clones I grow originated from here.    
  
The collection at California Carnivores has been around since at least the early 80's, if not earlier, and many of these clones have been in cultivation since then.  I bet many of the original sites they came from are now shopping malls, parking lots, residential homes, or farmland.   
  
  
Note: in some cases, there are multiple shots of the same plant...anyhow, enough ranting-enjoy the show!
