From this seedbatch, there were 2 individuals that stood out, and below is the most vigorous one of the two.  They both have excellent shape, and the veins are very detailed.  This clone doesn't divide very often, nor does it grow fast, but it does form nice specimen plants over time.  
  
This plant inherited the great shape from the Bulloch Co, GA ornata, and the the veins seem to be mixed between both parents.
