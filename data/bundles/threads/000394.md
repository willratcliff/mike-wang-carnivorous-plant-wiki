After seeing the pictures below, some of you will be wondering why is this clone called dark pink?    Well, these are freshly open pitchers, so they haven't been exposed to the elements to get that nice dark color.  You will see in the near future, these same pitchers will turn darker.  Maybe for now, it should be called S. leucophylla 'temporarily light pink"   

![:D](//storage.proboards.com/forum/images/smiley/grin.png) [not mirrored]

  
  
One interesting thing I observed about this clone is that the pitchers on one of the divisions was extremely tall.  Maybe it was from being overcrowded in the tubs...who knows.  
  
Please note this is genetically different from the S. leucophylla 'Burgundy' clone that I got originally from Phil Sheridan:
