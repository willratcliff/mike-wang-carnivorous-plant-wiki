Years back, I grew out hundreds of roseas from liberty Co, FL, and a few were selected out to keep.  This was one of them, and while I didn't capture a pic of this plant when it was much larger, it still does look pretty nice with relatively small pitchers.   The traps can get very large due to its vigor (ie. it's not a slow growing genetic giant), and it has that classic rosea look to it!
