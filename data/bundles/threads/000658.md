S. flava var. maxima is a variant that is devoid of red pigmentation in the body of the trap, especially in the throat.  They are typically uncommon in populations, but show up here and there.  These are not anthocyanin free plants; they sometimes do produce red pigments when damaged or under other environmental conditions.  
  
Most of the maximas come from S. flava var. flava populations in the Carolinas, but occasionally, you'll find some maximas from Georgia and Florida.  In those areas, the maximas are coming from mutated S. flava var. rugelii plants (the ones with the red throat).
