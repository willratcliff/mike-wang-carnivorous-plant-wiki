These purple throated S. oreophilas are just spectacular!  I'm finding that many of the "boring" or standard S. oreophila clones in cultivation can actually color up when given the right environment.  However, in comparison, you don't need to do much to coax this purple throated clone to produce nice pitchers!  Granted, spring pitchers are pretty dull and green, but it's the summer and early fall pitchers that really produce the best show.
