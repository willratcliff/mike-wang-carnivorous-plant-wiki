This year was brutal for Sarracenias in the wild.  The drought extended for an unbearable number of months, followed by many weeks of blistering heat in the summer.   The medium at many sites was really dry on the surface, and only slightly moist underneath.  Sarracenias respond by throwing out phyllodia and not producing any pitchers.  
  
If the drought wasn't bad enough, a tropical storm and a few hurricanes whistled by.   Clearly,  this wasn't the best time  to see flavas, but nevertheless, we saw them, and a few of them were still looking good!  
  
  
  
  
S. flava var. rubricorpora Bay Co, FL  This was the healthiest population of red tubed plants we visited.  At this site, technically speaking, there are some S. flava var. atropurpureas, but I suspect they are just really red rubricorporas.
