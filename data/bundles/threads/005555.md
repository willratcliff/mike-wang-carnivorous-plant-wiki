Here's another clone of atropurpurea that I've had forever but haven't yet had a chance to fully document until now!  It stood out of the crowd a being an incredibly fast and vigorous individual, and it also readily divides easier and quicker than many of the other clones.  On the other hand, this clone can be a bit tricky to color up all the way: it absolutely doesn't want to be disturbed for a good year or two and at least in my climate, besides giving it blasting full sun, keeping the previous years' traps and phyllodia on(that stay alive during the winter) makes a huge difference.    
  
Interestingly, this year, the clone colored up rather quickly:normally, it won't color up until weeks after the traps have opened, but for whatever reason, this clone colored up very well and right away this year!  
  
Pics taken June 4, 2022.  Look at how colorful the trap is on this baby compared to other clones in the exact same soil (background):
