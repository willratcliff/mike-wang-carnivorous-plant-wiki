As always, this is the result of a large grow out of seedlings: the cross had all normal sized individuals except there was a single individual that stood out from the entire batch.   It was easily 3-4 times the size of all the other seedlings, which didn't have the same vigor as this one individual.  One of the hardest parts about breeding is figuring out which one(s) to keep, but in this batch, it was a no brainer: this one clone has a bulbous shape and can size up like no others!  To prevent confusion in the future, the rest of the batch was culled and only this one clone was kept!      
  
Every giant clone that has been bred in the past started off this way: the individual was bigger than the rest, and within a year, it filled up a pot despite starting off as a tiny plant in the beginning of the grow season.  This individual is no different....it did exactly just that!  It was a small seedling earlier this year, and after the end of the grow season, it has filled a 3.5' pot!
