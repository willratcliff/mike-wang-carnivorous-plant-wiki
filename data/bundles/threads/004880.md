You can't always judge a leucophylla by its summer traps: the best show is typically in the fall, when they produce their largest, whitest traps!  
  
well, thought I'd start photo-documenting this clone: it looks like a regular leucophylla in the pics below, but don't be fooled, this one can get very white under ideal conditions!  It's one of those chameleon clones but hey, the summer traps look like a decent regular leucophylla.
