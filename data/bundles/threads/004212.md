Here's a cool registered cultivar that I got from Pokie22 via Miroslav Srba, and at first, I wasn't so sure about it.  Now that it's fully established and shooting out colorful traps...well, I'm definitely sure about it now LOL  It has  grown on me (and grown well for me): the plant is crazy vigorous and produces somewhat compact plants with an abundance of traps.  
  
I'm sure this plant is all over Europe by now, but it has just started to trickle into the US.  It did take a lot of babying for Jesus to get this plant healthy and happy, but once it got acclimated to Northern Calfifornia sunlight, the plant took off and grew very fast!  these were pretty small, and I think I got it maybe a little over a year ago.    
  
My plant literally just started to produce colorful traps, so it has yet to reach its fullest potential. Thought I'd spill the beans early anyways, photos taken 7/2/18:
