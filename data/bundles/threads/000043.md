S. flava var. rubricorpora near Sumatra, Liberty Co, FL.  This is a seedling selected from about a hundred different individuals.   I crossed two different good looking red clones and got this.  Even though both of the parents are rubricorporas, this one looks like an S. flava var. atropurpurea with some imperfections in the color of the lid.  In my book, it's still a rubricorpora-just a really nice clone:
