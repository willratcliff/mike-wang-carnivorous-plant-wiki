So I had to come up with something and name it after Benny Goo-Goo, otherwise he'd be all like "Dude, Daddy, why did yall homies name a plant after Ellie Boo-Boo but not after me?"  Truth be told, it's because that plant was bred and named before we even knew Benny would exist!    My wife is still bent out of shape about Sarracenia Ellie Wang as well, so I guess I have to make something for her at some point as well.  
  
This new plant is just a "tentative place holder" while I evaluate the seed batch!   So far, it has that amazing red body and solid white top, but with a flava-esque shape to it!  YESSSSSS!!!!  That's what I was after!    It's way too early to tell if this will be a winner with 100% certainty, but I'm pretty sure it'll be!  It's only a few years old, and I have no clue whether it's just a spring/summer performer, or if it can also produce amazing fall traps.      
  
This is the result of crossing two different complex moorei hybrids with each other, and given that both parents are polyhybrids, the resulting offspring are all over the place!  There might even be other color combinations from this cross worth showcasing, but so far, this is the absolute winner as far as I can tell.   This was a relatively small grow out, I started with around 150 seedlings, culled all the uglies, and got them down to about 80 or so to evaluate.    There are a few  other red bodied individuals with white tops, but nothing comes close this one....so far!  We'll see if something else better shows up, but for now, I got my eyes on this one!  Hopefully it'll be an easy grower and have vigor, fingers crossed!
