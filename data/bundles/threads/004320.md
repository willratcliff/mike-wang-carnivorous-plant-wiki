This clone is one of the few that seems to be a regular leucophylla with a somewhat unusual characteristic: the white pigments stretch far down the petiole!  There are now several genetically different clones with this characteristic, and now I'm confident that we will lock this trait in when we breed with these individuals.  Just like many other species leucophlla clones that haven't ever been bred with, these sort of  backbone genetics offer the opportunity to create characteristics never seen in Sarracenia breeding!  Keep in mind, with the right crosses, the unique characteristics can become more well defined and easier to color up with proper breeding and selecting.
