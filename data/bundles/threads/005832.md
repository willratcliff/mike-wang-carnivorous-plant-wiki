I thought there was a thread on this, and if there is, a homie for sure can't find it, so here's a new one!    
  
This is an absolutely incredible, jaw dropping variant with the best of both worlds: crystal clear veining on the outside that turns dark as the traps age coupled with an extreme red throat!  Easy to grow, vigorous, productive, and overall a looker!
