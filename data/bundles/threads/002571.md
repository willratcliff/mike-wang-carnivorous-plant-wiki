Another interesting clone that's in the pipeline for distribution in the future.  KIWIEARL showed us a similar plant  where a copper bodied "flava" came from a S. flava var. rubricorpora plant (Liberty Co, FL)  Does anyone have the link, it's somewhere on this forum!  
  
 Similarly, this plant has "S. flava red form" genes as well as pure S. flava var. rugelii all mixed up.  Typically, you get either a solid red formed plant, or a rugelii, but less commonly do you get these rare mixes in between.  The entire body of the trap had a copper tinge to it earlier this year during the spring, but now it looks more like a S. flava var. cuprea.  With some of the traps, they look like pure rugelii, isn't that really weird?!  This clone seems to have a lot of phenotypic variances depending on environmental conditions.
