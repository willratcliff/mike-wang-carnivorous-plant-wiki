I saw this from the corner of my eye and thought "doo, that looks pretty neat, guys!"  Given the intricate veining and almost artsy fartsy nature of this clone, I'm calling it "red design" to sound more sophisticated....you know, like snobby LOL  "Hey Darling, what do you think of this DESIGN?"  or what about that DESIGN?    
  
  At first, I thought it might be from Frankie Co, FL (aka Franklin Co, FL) Upon looking at the tag, it said "probably a cross between two different red leuco clones" aka this is an unknown, but for sure, it's a seedling from one of the crosses I made in the past.  Maybe it has some frankie genes in it?  Who knows.....  Whatever it might be, the coloration and patterns on this clone is quite unique and good looking, and maybe even for those who have very strict taste when it comes to DESIGN, it might pass their seal of approval.    
  
Pics taken 9/15/22.  I guess we'll have to wait until next year or the year after that to see bigger traps.  When they get bigger, the details become even more remarkable:
