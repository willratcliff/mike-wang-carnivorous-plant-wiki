Here is an outstanding, select wild type clone that originated from Krishtian Kleiner....errr rather Klaus Keller!  There are perhaps one or two other coalmine beach clones floating around in the US, but this one for sure is a standout!  It really does look like one of the very best individuals from that famous site!    
  
To me, All cephalotus are challenging to grow, but once you grow several different clones, you'll realize some are less finicky than others.  Is the Klaus Keller Klone relatively easier than other clones to grow?  Yes and no.  Yes because I haven't killed it yet and it produced several side shoots from a tiny plant, but no because I almost did kill it during a heatwave last fall.  The plant sulked for months after that but eventually recovered and is looking well now!  
  
I've only had this plant for about a year now and when it arrived it settled in well right away but never produced anything worth photographing until now!  I still don't know a whole bunch about it yet but check out how colorful these traps are!
