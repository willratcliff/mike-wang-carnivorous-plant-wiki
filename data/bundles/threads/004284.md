This is technically an un-named clone, but it performs well year after year and sticks out of the crowd, so I'm tentatively using some generic Sarracenia grower terms as its clonal name.  
  
This clone has some red below the white pigments, but not quite enough in my book to call it a leuco red.  The intense network of veins seems to be more of a stand out feature for this clone, and it is indeed "vary niiice."  Unlike many of its Brethren- Sistren (since they're hermaphrodites, gotta include both genders by making up a word), this clone pushes out nice big fat traps year after year, it ain't shy to pitcher up!
