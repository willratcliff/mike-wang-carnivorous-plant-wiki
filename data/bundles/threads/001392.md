Sorry, the subject title is short hand since it won't let me write the whole thing.  S. (x Reptilian Rose) x (flava var. rubricorpora x leucophylla) clone #11 is one of those clones that I think is nameable.  Why is it called clone #11?  The number is to distinguish this individual from other individuals in the same seed batch.  There are at least 11 different seedlings from this cross, although I think there are more.    
  
  It's not every day that a choice hybrid like this shows up, and it's not every day that a single cross can produce several individuals worthy of being registered as a cultivar.  This cross was produced by Dr. Travis H. Wyman and grown out by my hybridizing Bromentor, Rob Co of the Pitcher Plant Project.  Just imagine a Reptilian Rose (photos: [sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/351/reptilian-rose-new-photos-10](http://sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/351/reptilian-rose-new-photos-10) )  that has even better shape and more dynamic colors...that's what this plant is!   
  
 In this seed batch, several color forms and shapes appeared, so while this plant is quite outstanding, there are other siblings from this seed batch that might also be worth naming.    While Rob's plant has deeper colors under his greenhouse conditions, this clone may be able to get just as dark outdoors.  For photos and more info, check out his blog here, and scroll down: [thepitcherplantproject.com/blog/tag/reptilian-rose/](http://thepitcherplantproject.com/blog/tag/reptilian-rose/)
