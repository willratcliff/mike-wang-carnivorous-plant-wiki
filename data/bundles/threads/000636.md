Here's yet another clone of S. flava var. rubricorpora from Liberty Co, FL.  This plant is very similar to its relatives, clones A, B, G, T, and so on.  The body on this one turns solid red once fully developed, and it has that typical contrasting greenish yellow lid.  Shape on this one is pretty decent too.  It's hard to come by a solid red bodied rubricorpora that doesn't look good!
