Sarracenia alabamensis (Dan Winter) is a clone that I don't know much about, except it produces some good looking traps!  Does anyone know who Dan Winter is?  The only thing I know about him is that he has an amazing moorei named after him...  Anyways, this is the second year I've had this plant, and now it's fully acclimated and growing full force.  Perhaps it can produce some even bigger traps next year now that momentum has picked up.  
  
Like all other alabamensis plants, this clone produces skinny spring/summer traps, followed by fat, sturdy, and tall fall traps (very similar to S. leucophylla).
