Sarracenia leucophylla RED 'DARK STRETCH.'    Normally, leucophyllas don't produce impressive spring traps: in fact, the majority of the leucophylla collection right now is lackluster with really tiny, unremarkable looking traps overall.  However, there are a few populations and individuals that do produce great spring traps, and they have been the focus of a long term breeding project.  
After many failures and  several generations of selective breeding, here's a clone that has some pretty amazing spring traps!  The fall traps are even more colorful and jaw dropping!    
It hasn't yet been propagated, but for sure I will be dividing it this coming winter dormancy period!
