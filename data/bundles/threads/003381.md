This clone tends to produce bright white traps with somewhat circular mouths.  The white gets so intense that this clone can be mistaken for a var. alba, which as a matter of fact, this may very well be.  Only after a few more grow seasons will we know how this plant performs and whether or not it can produce a solid white interior.  
  
  This year the traps look "normal" but in previous years, the traps were outstandingly white and stood out of the crowd.  The circular mouth suggests back-crossing with flava many generations ago, and it's not uncommon to find such shapes in leucophylla populations that also have flava mixed in.
