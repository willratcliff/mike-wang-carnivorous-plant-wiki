Here's a clone that was getting choked out in a community tray for a good decade before finally a homie got to it and repotted the dang thing!  Now that the plants have full sun and aren't being shaded by other clones, they're producing the most yellow traps I've ever seen from this exact clone!  They'll probably color up even better next year, since they tend to not reach their fullest color potential after being torn to pieces  (ie. after being divided).
