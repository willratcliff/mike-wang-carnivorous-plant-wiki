Oh my freakin GOSH!  I can't tell you how frustrating it was to acquire this clone, I had been searching for it for a few years and after no luck, I decided to contact the grower who named/registered the plant, Christina Toole.  Fortunately, she responded quite quickly and was extremely pleasant to interact with.   I thought maybe....just maybe there's a chance I'll get my grabby hands on this dang thing.....but nope!  Christina's explanation was completely valid: she was battling a powdery mildew outbreak!  I definitely didn't want an infected plant, and we discussed ways to battle the outbreak, but that was the end of the best lead I had to get this clone.  Dude, did you see them upward lids bro?!!!  GOOOOSH I wanted this thing so bad, LOL  [cpn.carnivorousplants.org/articles/CPNv47n3p125_129.pdf](https://cpn.carnivorousplants.org/articles/CPNv47n3p125_129.pdf)  
  
I was bent out of shape for maybe a few minutes until I hit up my home Slizzle in the hizzle-nowse, Rob Chiggity-Chocolately-COco-moe aka Rob Co of the pitcher plant project: [thepitcherplantproject.com/](https://thepitcherplantproject.com/)   and he just HAPPENED to have a division available for trade, so we worked it out!   He sent me a really nice division that had a decent size tuber and two growth points, but man, it was SOOOOOOO slow at first!  I kept babying it and babying it and babying it and the dang thing just didn't do much the first year.  For those of you who have grown cephalotus, you're probably nodding your heads, thinking "yup, been there done that."  They're just like that, but once they start growing strong, as long as you keep them happy, they keep growing strong.  
  
Fast forward to a year later, it has finally started to size up and WOW, it's a really beauty!  I'm not getting the upward lids just yet....I mean they're upwards-ish but not at a 90 degree angle...nevertheless, the plant now has incredible growth momentum, is pushing out traps left and right, they are decent sized, and colorful!  I'll take that as a win!  It just started getting cold outside, so we'll see if this thing colors up even more as time progresses into the winter months
