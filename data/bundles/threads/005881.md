I had to take a second look at this clone: it's for sure very yellow!  This the result of crossing two genetic giant flava var. rugelii clones together: both parents are slow growing, super hard to perform outdoors here in NOrthern California, but they are both proven giant clones with absolutely massive traps!  
  
Out of a very large seed batch (yes, the usual MW method of breeding), all of the smallest plants were culled, only the biggest, most vigorous individuals were kept in the collection.  About 3 years after sowing,  out of the biggest ones, I selected out the 10 largest plants, but they're all crammed in community tray, so they haven't been given sufficient space to size up properly.  
  
Nevertheless, this one clone, despite producing traps only a foot tall, has incredible girth and is no doubt a giant just waiting for some space to size up!  The girth didn't catch my attention though: look at that YELLOW color!  Holy moley!  
  
These are pretty old traps past their prime, wish I took some photos earlier, but now that this clone is on my radar, it'll get more room to spread its roots and size up in the future.
