This clone originates from Charles Brewer, who is a well known Cephalotus grower in the US.  He has since "retired" from distributing plants but his legacy continues with the genetics he's brought into circulation.  One of those clones that is quite impressive is C. follicularis 'Brewer's Beauty,' another "glorified typical" that colors up relatively easier than other clones and has a very well defined red lid under ideal conditions.  It's quite vigorous and can form very impressive clumps relatively quickly once it gains good growth momentum.  
  
Cephalotus in general are challenging to grow, in my opinion.  Those who haven't ever struggled with them will struggle if they ever move or have to change their growing environments, while those who have killed many will know how to adapt to any growing condition. After growing many different clones, there are definitely some that are way more sensitive to environmental changes than others.  Brewer's Beauty is perhaps on the easy side or in the middle: I can't say it's super easy as I've killed every impressive, specimen sized mother plant that I've grown from it, but the clone still lives on in the collection from the side shoots that were secured before the big plants croaked.
