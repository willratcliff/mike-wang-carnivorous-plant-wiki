Okay, first question non super hardcore Sarracenia nerds will ask is "what's S. flava var. ornata 'improved'?  Well, take a really good looking red veined flava var. ornata, make it easier to grow and produce more traps throughout the year, and then make it more veiny (note:this is very environmentally induced), and that's S. flava var. ornata improved.  Out of the improved "series," I found one individual with a solid red throat, hence this thread!  
  
This solid red throat phenotype is pretty amazing, and what you learn from breeding is that what you think you'll get oftentimes isn't even close to what you end up with.  S. flava var. ornata improved 'solid red throat' is no exception.  
  
Photos taken 4/16/18, literally just discovered this plant this morning!
