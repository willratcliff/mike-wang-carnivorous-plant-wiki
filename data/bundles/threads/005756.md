Here's a super rare genotype of purpurea ssp. venosa from Duplin Co, NC.  I don't remember what the big fuss is about this particular county, but what I can say is that this clone is quite bulbous and good looking!  It didn't color up all the way this season, but it can get dark red when left undisturbed.  
  
This division had a super rough start when I first acquired it about a year ago.  It was loaded with root knot nematode, so it has been isolated from other plants.  It also didn't acclimate well and got completely messed up for most of the season until finally, around August, it started producing nice traps.  Purpureas don't seem to like my climate because it's about 7-15F cooler in my area compared to downtown (10 minutes away) where it's much warmer.   
  
That said, because it got warmer during the late summer,  it's been growing strong and is starting to look nice!  Almost all growth this year happened in the past 3 months!
