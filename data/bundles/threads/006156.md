Well, when you grow out a bunch of alba crosses, have tons of seedlings, and a few at a very young age look like they have potential, that's when you separate them and evaluate them, or at least that's what I do when there is space and time.  How do you distinguish one plant from the other?  You could use some complex number and lettering system that makes you look really smart, or you can do what I did and just called it "eval#1, Eval#2, etc).  Well, now you know how I evaluate these seedlings!  
  
This one has performed well for many years, but I haven't had a chance to take photos of it until now!  It's consistently super white, colors up relatively easily, and overall a great performer.  That said, it needs optimal conditions to get this white (eg. full sun, no distubance, low nutrient level in the soil, etc).
