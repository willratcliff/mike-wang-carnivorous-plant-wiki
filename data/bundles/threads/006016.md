Many of the clones from Horry Co, SC have a nice yellow tinge to them, but this clone, as well as maybe one or two others, really turns deep yellow under optimal conditions!  The way this one has been selected out is by comparing other clones growing in the exact same soil and exact same environment (they all share the same community tray).  This particular clone appears to get the yellowest, so I'm isolating it and will propagate it this coming fall/winter.    
  
S. flava var. flava 'standout yellow clone' Horry Co, SC, pics taken 6/20/24.  First off, take a look at that trap in the middle and compare it to the surrounding traps.  It's for sure special and on a genetic level: this clone colored up last year as well and it probably has been doing that for a while, didn't really start paying attention until the last few years:
