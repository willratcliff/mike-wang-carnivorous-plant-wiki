I'm surprised to see no threads or grow reports on rugelii's from this site, so here's one!  Just like the ornatas from this site, there's some really unique genetics on the rugelii side as well.    Check out how strange the red throat is on this particular clone, it's long!   Not something you see every day.  
  
This seedling isn't particularly vigorous, so I don't see it becoming the next S. flava var. rugelii clone A.  It's more of a genetic resource for those who want to push breeding to the next level.  
  
Some may deem rugeliis boring, common, and pointless, but I see this species as very intriguing due to its vast diversity.  They're no longer common in the wild, since most of the habitat has been destroyed.   Anyways, with this clone, it has a trait not often seen, and this is what you want to look out for if you're  trying to breed something original.        Pics taken 6/1/20:
