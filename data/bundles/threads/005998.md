It's actually S. flava var. rubricorpora GIANT (back row giant x Burgundy) Liberty Co, FL, but of course, there wasn't enough space on the title to write the whole thing out!  
  
I think many in the past have sort of raised an eyebrow, questioning whether this cross is actually worthy of being called a giant, since I had no proof of seedlings sizing up all the way at vegetative maturity.  All I was able to show were large traps on relatively small rhizomes, a tell tale sign that you have a giant!  Understandably, unless you have experience seeing this first hand with long term breeding projects, it's understandable that some may be skeptical that  such characteristics are what you're looking for in giants.  
  
With this particular cross, I grew out a large batch of seedlings and only selected out the biggest, most vigorous individuals for distribution.  The rest were culled, so only the best of the best exist.  If you have this cross, you have a giant!  
  
Well, finally. FINALLY!  Here's proof that this cross is GIGANTIC!  These aren't yet at their fullest potential size wise, but it's pretty crystal clear that they're giants!
