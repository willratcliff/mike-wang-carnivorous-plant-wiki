These are genetics originally from John Hummer's collection.  John collected divisions of the plants before the site was destroyed, and these are seedlings from his divisions.  They are certainly very similar to Barry's seed grown plants, but quite different in the sense that they tend to be taller and more slender.  I found one individual that had one trap with red veins, and on the same plant, another trap without any red veins.
