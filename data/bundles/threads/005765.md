The minors from Berkeley Co, SC  have that very classic, shapely look to it!  I only have a few clones that are mature, but there's a good sized population of seedlings in the pipeline, so I'll keep this thread updated as they grow up.  It'll be interesting to see the diversity, if any, from this area.  Surprisingly, minors from Berkeley Co, SC are still quite rare in cultivation (at least in the US), you don't see them in many collections.
