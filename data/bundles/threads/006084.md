Back when Charles was selling Cephs, he sent me this clone as a typical (he sent me 2 different clones, so I originally called this one typical #2), but it's clearly a giant!  It's a random seed grown plant from his collection of many different clones, quite a few of which I don't think have entered circulation.  That said, he did distribute a good handful of them and they are circulating in collections.  
  
How do I know this is a giant clone?  The only way I can tell is that on a relatively small root tuber, this plant produced a huge trap!  Only giant clones can do that!  Western Venom, as an example, isn't considered a giant but it can produce very large traps here and there when given the right care and when the plant reaches a certain size.  This clone, on the other hand, sized up when it really shouldn't have big enough to, hence the giant designation.

There is some(Sarracenia)'Suspicion'(yes, that's a valid cultivar) that this giant clone is the result of Hum-DAWG Giant (that's improper grammar for Hummer's Giant) crossed with maybe one of Master Brewer's "beauty" or red clone, but of course it's all speculation.  The wide midrib is for sure intriguing.
