Here's a really neat clone that is very popular and in crazy high demand.  Fortunately, it makes divisions readily, forms nice clumps, and is an overall good trap producer.  Although the intensity of the red body color isn't as dominant in this clone as in other clones from the same population, it still can produce some excellent colors under optimal conditions.  Another plus is unlike many of the other rubricorpora clones, the lid on this one doesn't seem to turn solid red as easily, so it maintains that nice yellow/red contrast, even as the pitcher ages.  
  
So here we are, S. flava var. rubricorpora Liberty Co, FL clone H, Photos taken 5/4/14.  Check out the lid on this plant!
