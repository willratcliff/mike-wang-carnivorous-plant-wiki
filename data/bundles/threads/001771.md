Here's another site that Damon and I had visited 3 years ago, and we were curious to see how it was doing.   We spoke with the land owners, and they were concerned about people from the floral industry coming to this site and harvesting the traps!  Sadly,  the land owners themselves implied they were still harvesting traps, which over the course of time will technically not likely kill them, but it will set back potential long run population expansion.   On the bright side, the landowners were clearly burning this site and maintaining it, so I guess it's still an overall win situation for the plants.  The site looked just as nice as it did when we first saw it, so we are optimistic it'll last in the long run.
