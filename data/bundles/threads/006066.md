Here's a clone that's still quite rare in the US but perhaps more common in Europe(?).  I had this one imported since it was nearly impossible to find a source here in the US that could confirm with 100% certainty the real deal.  
  
One distinguishing characteristic of this clone is that it has a very particular shape when it's producing regular sized traps.  I'm not sure I can describe it exactly but when you see a german giant, it's recognizable to the hardcore plant nerd eye.  This plant visially matches the "looks" of plants that other growers in Europe.    I've had it for a little more than a year now, there are pictures of other growers getting giant traps out of this plant, but it was done under lower light levels.  It'll be interesting to see if the clone can produce giant traps with high intensity light, just like hummer's giant and the  Giant allen lowrie clone can!  
  
  
Here's a picture of the plant about a month after I acquired it.   It spent many weeks in a package clearing customs, so when I got it the growth points were yellow/etiolated.  A lot of the old traps died off, but that's okay, it clearly survived and is now thriving!  My source took such good care of it prior to sending it and the plant was clearly very strong before it entered the mail: had that not been the case, it's hard to say if this one would have made it after being in the mail so long!
