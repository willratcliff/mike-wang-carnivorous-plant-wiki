One thing I've learned about breeding pure lines of  S. flava var. rubricorpora: you can't judge an individual plant very well until they're fully mature in size.  However, you can get an idea of how nice they will be, especially seedlings that display darker colors than others.  Many of you out there have received seedlings of L x best clone from me, and I wouldn't be surprised if you ended up with something amazing or similar to this plant. I ended up keeping a few "mediocre" looking seedlings, and now that they're fully mature, they're jaw-dropping adult plants!
