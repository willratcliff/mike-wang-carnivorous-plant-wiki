Here's a really beautiful primary hybrid that originates from our very own Brooks Garcia.   It's quite vigorous and produces 2 solid flushes of pitchers: spring/summer traps and fall traps.  Much like its alata and leucophylla parents, the fall pitchers are larger and more vibrant than the spring/summer traps.    
  
Notice how S. leucophylla is more dominant in this cross than S. alata.  Perhaps the "floppy top" is due to the alata genetics.   Interestingly, the traps are darker red when they first open, and they slowly turn whiter as they age.  This particular clone is rather vigorous, and produces an abundance of medium height pitchers.
