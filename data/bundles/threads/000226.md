Just like the other clones, this one is also mysterious!  The shape varies, and sometimes, the spring pitchers produce  pink pigments.  
  
All pictures below are S. leucophylla Hurricane Creek White clone E from Baldwin Co, AL-these are extinct in the wild:

#### **Mike Wang** (`@meizzwang`)
`post #8` · `id=7290` · `2014-05-05 23:47:10 UTC`

Thanks Calen!  Yes, don't be alarmed, all hurricane creek white plants, including the brighest white ones, can turn red under certain environmental conditions.  
  
Kimbruun, yes, these are all spring pitchers, and the fall pitchers  typically have brighter white colors.  I also had very tall fall pitchers on Clone A, but clone E is the tallest.  I think on the last photo, there are some flowers, but they're almost bloomed out.  The petals on hurricane creek white are bright red, and they seem to be slightly brigher red in color when compared to other regular leucophyllas.
