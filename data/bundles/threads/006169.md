Here's a small population of purple pitcher plants that reportedly come from a higher elevation site in PA!  They are for sure genetically unique and look quite different from the more readily available variant found in Centre Co, PA: [sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/5238/purpurea-ssp-centre-pa](https://sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/5238/purpurea-ssp-centre-pa)  I don't know much more about these genetics other than it's been somewhat challenging to grow them, although that could totally be due to my climate, which is sometimes borderline too cold for Sarracenia during the summer months (there are nights during the summer where it dips into the high 40's in my precise microclimate, and that can burn the leaves!).  Nevertheless, despite being somewhat hard to grow, it's still worth it in my opinion because look at those shapes and the color is quite dark when you see it in person!
