Figured it's time for this clone to have it's own thread, so here you go! Presumably, the yellow coloration probably comes from historic introgression with S. flava, but then this thang got back crossed so many times that you can't tell, based on the shape alone, that flava ever had anything to do with this plant!  
  
To make things even more complex, this clone appears to be solid pink to reddish at times, while other times, it yellows up!  No clue what environmental factors favor one color over the other because it's always growing in blasting full sun.

LOL that's a good question!  To the best of my knowledge, doing something for Jesus means you're pretty much doing the very best you can, as you are seeking divine approval of your work.  When a plant is grown for Jesus, even the LORD almighty will approve of how well it has been cared for.  Thus, growing for Jesus means you meet the highest horticultural standards, as though being judged by God, who will determine if you go to heaven or hell.  I suppose there's an implication of some level of fear of the wrath of God if you don't grow it well, so some may not take a claim of growing something for Jesus lightly.   For me, it's just a figure of speech and has no theological intentions.  Amen.
