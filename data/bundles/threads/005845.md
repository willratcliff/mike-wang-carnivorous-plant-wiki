These S. flava var. maximas originate from a hilly habitat, which is unusual for flavas since they normally are found in coastal, lowland savannah bogs.  These have also been consistently yellow, at least outdoors here in Northern California.  
  
Generally speaking, while flava var. flava is abundant in cultivation, flavas from this particular site are just starting to enter cultivation as far as I know: these genotypes are quite unique and so far, they're pretty easy to grow!  Other flava var. flavas like the ones from Brunswick Co, NC can be a bit more challenging to cultivate since they're so dang rot prone!
