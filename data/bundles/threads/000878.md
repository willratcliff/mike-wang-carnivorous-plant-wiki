#### **Mike Wang** (`@meizzwang`)
`post #8` · `id=3644` · `2013-09-16 21:39:20 UTC`

There's over a 1000 photos from this trip, so stay tuned, you haven't seen anything yet, haha  
  
Oh, missed your question about the temperature....it was 85F (29C) at around 8:30 in the morning, and it averaged about 92 F (33C) during the day.  Some days, it was in the 100's (38C)  Let me add that the humidity was probably 90+ percent the whole time, and you have to be very tough to stay out in the full sun under these conditions.  It's hard to picture just how savage it is out there, but heat exhaustion kicks in very quickly.  Our shirts were constantly soaked in sweat, and if you stay outside for more 6 hours straight without hydrating, you'll likely pass out.  I'm part Taiwanese, so my genes can handle this weather, but Axel and Damon couldn't stay out for as long as I could (or maybe I'm just crazier about the plants than they are
