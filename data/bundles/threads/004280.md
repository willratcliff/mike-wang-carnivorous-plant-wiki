Wow! meizzwang that is one amazing fly trap display! That's one of the healthiest and happiest pot-o-fly-trap ever! No wonder that guy took your photo to sell other VFTs.     
  
This photo stuff happens to us also. The most upsetting response was actually from Wes Buckner who was using our Sunbelle photos on ebay to sell plants. He told us our photos were "fair game" and he wasn't going to stop. We told him to use his own photos, we had to threaten to report him to ebay. Wes started to use his own photos after that.   
  
Just a few weeks ago, I noticed one of our leuco photos on the Carnivorous Plant Resource website. The site owner David was apologetic and took our photo down (but he had a list of reasons why he decided to use our photo). At least he understood and was very friendly.   
  
Honestly, it's the time it takes to grow the picture perfect plants that are in the photos. It's not just snapping a picture. The time and effort probably spans years before the photos are even taken. Michelle's photography is done properly with good lighting & composition, and she has her recognizable style. But, the real trick: those well-grown plants that are the subject of the pretty photos.   
This is why we get such a stupefied reply to "use your own photos" because it's not that easy. Using someone else's photo is way easier for bait-and-switch sales... or pretending to be a more experienced grower than they actually are.   
  
It's an economic factor. Better photos sell more plants. It is a facade and that's the only real reason.   
Let's be honest, if this guy used his own plant pics... they are probably much crappier and would not have the same impact as the amazing fly trap you have grown!     
  
Most of us would never use anyone else's photos without permission.    
  
What I don't understand is why people don't just ask to use other people's photos. 99% of the time, I do allow it as long as certain criteria are met. 

   
  
Several of these people have explained that if it shows up on a search engine, they believe they can use that image, which is wrong of course. They don't ask because they are misinformed about image ownership. And they know 99% of growers are not taking photos of their plants so other sellers can use the same photos to compete and sell the same plants...
