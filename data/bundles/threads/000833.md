Here's a really neat naturally occurring hybrid that was registered in 2011 by Jay Lechtman.  This plant originally came from a site near the yellow river that has since been destroyed (from what I've been told).  This site had S. rubra ssp. gulfensis, S. psittacina, and S. leucophyllas (and maybe flavas?)  S. x gorey is technically a S. x gilpini (S. psittacina x S. rubra ssp. gulfensis).    
  
Below are some new photos, taken 8/9/13 of S. x Gorey.  The new fall pitchers that haven't developed yet are probably going to be more spectacular than what you see right now.  Love the bright red color on this plant:
