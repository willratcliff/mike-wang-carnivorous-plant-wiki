I'm really running out of names for all of these clones, but everytime I see this one, the heavy venation reminds me of spider webs for some reason, so that's it's name for now!  
  
This clone has been crammed in a community tray for a good decade and years ago, it performed, but I've since neglected it and not allowed enough light to reach the grow point, so for years, it didn't produce fall traps.  The tray is Jam packed with growth points, so the plants essentially overcrowd each other year after year.  This past winter, I trimmed out some of the growth points so the "canopy" was less dense.  This allowed the entire tray to "bloom" once again with some exciting fall pitchers!
