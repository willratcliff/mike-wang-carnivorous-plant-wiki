Here's a clone of S. purpurea ssp. purpurea from Oakland Co, Michigan.  Even though recent studies have shown the genetic diversity in S. purupurea ssp. purpurea isn't as expansive as say S. leucophylla, there is still some distinct characteristics from one population to another.  You would think the most widespread species of Sarracenia would have the most genetic diversity, but I guess that's not the case, or at least that's what we believe at this time.  
  
Anyhow, here's the plant-this clone seems a bit more "upright" than others that I've seen.  For sure, the distinctions are a lot more subtle than in other species of Sarracenia:
