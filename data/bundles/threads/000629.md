#### **Mike Wang** (`@meizzwang`)
`post #3` · `id=2534` · `2013-04-22 06:03:37 UTC`

Yes, this clone typically has a very well defined red blotch in the "neck" and the red color sometimes creeps up into the lid a little bit.  I've found that pretty much all of the lids from my rubricorporas will have more red in them as the pitcher ages.   
  
FYI: This one was transplanted this year, and the trap, when developing, wasn't solid red until about a week after it opened.  The red coloration in the throat will become more intense as the trap ages.
