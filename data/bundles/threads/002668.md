The red throat is a DEAD GIVEAWAY that this isn't a pure leucophylla.  This particular clone of S. x moorei is pretty darn amazing in the sense that to an untrained eye (ie. anyone who doesn't regularly post or lurk on this forum), it looks like a pure S. leucophylla, but we all know because of that red throat and the slight difference in pitcher shape that it's a leuco x flava rugelii cross.    
  
Photos taken 9/25/15.  From this first shot, even a trained eye can mistake this for a pure S. leucophylla:  

![](../../images/55/55a4c5e7b2a5e3ae0f921d5e96a16afd4f56af5bc78a8dc2cf6e1bc228469a92.jpg)

  
  
But as soon as you see this, you know there's flava rugelii up in the mix:  

![](../../images/02/021a9851af74a55f9f9c9c500bb47dc1fc17515267ed94012f33c96a6994b94b.jpg)

  
  
Can't wait for this thing to get bigger!  The summer traps were still juvenile-ish in size so there wasn't anything to photograph until now.  It'll be interesting to see if this clone can produce some stunning summer traps next year:  

![](../../images/24/24792da24a5b9203e60ec45b7991fff45d2f2693309cc255c4e99102a0f69cf6.jpg)
