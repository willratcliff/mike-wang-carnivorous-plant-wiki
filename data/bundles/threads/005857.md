[Jun 24, 2025 5:03:40 GMT -5](/post/51422/thread) [alexis](/user/558) said:

Nice. I crossed F21 rubricorpora with F18 rugelii and got.... 50% rubricorporas and 50% rugeliis.  
  
Anyone know why I didn't get any intermediates from this, as you would with a hybrid cross?

If its dominant red coming from the Rubricorpora, and all offspring are not red, then we can assume the parent is Rr or heterozygous for dominant red.    
  
If the Rugelii is rr, then we are proposing a cross of Rr x rr, which will give you 50% Rr (red) and 50% rr (not red).  
  
Of course this is theoretical and a best guesstimate, but it does fairly easily explain the results you are seeing from F21 x F18.  
  
edit to add:  regarding intermediates... a red rugelii would just be a rubricorpora with a throat blotch, and a rubricorpora without the red is no longer a rubricorpora... so I expect you are seeing intermediates but phenotype wise they are segregating into red and not red.
