I've had this clone for many years and finally got a chance to photograph it!  Montanas in general are quite rare, but acquiring this clone is probably a once in a lifetime sort of deal: very few people have it and most don't want to let it go because look at that darn thing!  
  
These are brand new spring traps that aren't fully colored, but you can already tell this clone is quite bulbous and just an absolute stunner!  It's not super difficult to keep alive long term like some montanas (eg. the ones from Samuel Jackson Co, NC), but it certain does take some experience to keep these alive and happy.    
  
AS the traps age, the lids fold over, giving this clone a very classic "montana'ey" appearance!
