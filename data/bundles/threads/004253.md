It was the best of batch....it was the worst of batch!  Well, actually, it's definitely not the worst of the batch, just sounds catchy to remix a tale of two cities (by Charles Dickens) Sarracenia style!  
  
Yes, this was the best plant out of maybe 100+ in the batch, hence the name.  It was the only one that was blindingly white, which made it stand out of the crowd.  Given its chameleon genetics, it doesn't always appear alba-ey, but this year(so far) it's looking quite white.  
  
So the pitchers aren't very showy right now being that these are late summer traps, but you can definitely tell, this thang is very alba-esque!  It feels like November here on the San Francisco Bay Area, CA Peninsula, with highs in the 60's and lows in the low to mid 50's, so most of the growth that we normally get right now is on hold.
