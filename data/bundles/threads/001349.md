HEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEERE's JOHNNY! I had been eye-balling this plant for years, and finally got my grabby hands on one of them!  This fascinating clone hails from the wilkerson's Bog, and has been introduced into cultivation via our very own Brooks Garcia of Sarracenia Obsessed.  The color on my plant isn't even close to the fullest potential, but even as a new transplant, it's already quite beautiful.  I think this thing can turn solid bright red under optimal conditions.  
  
What's the parentage?  Well, leucophylla is definitely in it, and there's probably flava.  It's most likely a moorei of some sort, but it doesn't look like your every day moorei.  Those genetics at the Wilkerson's property are very unique, and it wouldn't surprise me if this is in fact a complex hybrid.
