All photos below were taken by Brooks Garcia:  
  
  
Here are some pictures of S. leucophylla taken in September 2007  in Washington County, Alabama.  In this area, S. alata and S. leucophylla grow together, and the mixing of genes is quite frequent.  The pink on the inside of the S. leucophylla pictured below, for example, is likely to be the result of crossing S. leucophylla and alata.  Perhaps this one was back-crossed into S. leucophylla, making it look more like a pure species:
