some updates on the heritage of this clone.  I spoke with Phil, and he informed me it either originally came from Santa Rosa/okaloosa county area, or from west of Liberty Co, FL.  My suspicion, based on the shape and color of the plant, is that it's more likely to have come from the santa rosa/okaloosa county area. There's quite a few atropurpureas over there, and they look somewhat similar to this plant.  the Gulf county and Bay County red tubes, on the other hand, have a completely different look to them.  However, you never know...

Looks like this plant hasn't gotten much love on the Forum lately - time to fix that! Really happy with the way Bronze is looking this season - color is bright, lids are yuuuge. A bit of a ponderous grower and seems to prefer warmth to Cold Spring Oregon, but I'm happy with how it's doing. This clone has orange flowers and I believe originated in the now extirpated Cooks Bayou population in southern Bay County FL.
