K-Earl-now that you mention scent, I was doing a "sniff test" on the various flavas from different locations.  I compared flavas from Brunswick Co, NC, Sneads Ferry, Onslow Co, NC, bulloch Co, GA, Prince George Co, VA,  Thomas Co, GA, and found that they pretty much all smell exactly the same except for the Sneads Ferry plants.  Those consistently had less pungent fragrance-almost un-noticeable.  
  
I had a similar observation with S. rubra ssp. gufensis.  I have one clone from California Carnivores that I've never been able to detect any odor from after growing it for more than a decade.   However, two other populations of rubra gulfensis  in the collection consistently have sweet smelling flowers year after year.  Anyone else ever notice this?
