What are the parents?  Crap, my notes say "moorei Eastern AL," DANG!  Two possibilities, either it's a "regular looking"  moorei x a standard leucophylla, or a reddish flava (which probably originated from moorei) crossed with a "regular" leucophylla.  for sure, it's one or the other, but if I had to guess, I'd say it's the reddish moorei x regular leucophylla.  
  
What I do remember is that there were TONS of mutts from this cross: most of them didn't survive past the first adult pitcher stage because they were culled immediately.    Three individuals that looked similar to the one below were kept, and now it's down to two.  The other clone has a whiter top, we'll see how that turns out in the future.  
  
  Whatever the case may be, I think these individuals have excellent genetics for future breeding projects!  That red body is something else, and you can tell, this looks like backbone genetics that can be improved upon.  Photos taken 6/6/18:

Summit: missed your question, but thanks for bring this thread up, cuz now I do!  
  
Some of you guys out there got lucky getting this clone, pictures below will explain why!  
  
 This clone is called "S. moorei 'red body, white top" Eastern AL.     This clone looks almost like a red bodied Adrian Slack, but these genetics are VERY different and contain no direct slack genes (they're indirectly related since AS and this clone is a moorei).  Vigor-wise, it's slow for me like Adrian Slack, but not that slow...a little bit faster growing.  Other than that, it's pretty easy to grow, but somewhat mysterious when it comes to coloring up.      Photos taken 4/27/20:
