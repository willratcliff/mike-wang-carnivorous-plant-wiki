No, we're not talking about a neighborhood that lacks street lights at night, which increases the danger of nerdy plant folks getting shanked for a pitcher plant (don't ask me why anyone in their right mind would be carrying a pitcher plant in the middle of the hood at night) we're talking about the DARKNESS under the hood of this pitcher plant!  Ya, parts of that hood is pretty dang BLACK under that mix, guys!   Love that black color!

When that happens, one of two things are going on: 1) either the end of the world is near or 2) she genuinely thought it looked interesting.  I'm banking on the latter being the case, fingers crossed!
