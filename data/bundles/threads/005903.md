Clearly, I'm running out of names for super stretchy white albas, but this one looks like it worked out really hard to get as much white down the trap, hence the name.  Will it be the next best, most amazing alba out there?  Hard to say, you never know if the trait is stable and will show up on adults, although with albas, they typically become even more colorful as they produce vegetatively mature traps.  
  
For now, I have my eye on this baby!  For sure, it's the best (so far!)  out of a little less than a thousand seedlings, so there's some serious promise with this one.  You never know, a year or two down the road maybe a better one might show up.
