[banana](/user/75)  
Here in the US some sites are protected. If a site contains an endangered species such as S. jonesii or S. oreophila. Other government sites, such as those maintained by Meadowview Biological Research Center, or owned by Harvard Forest (Massachusetts) are also protected.  Some government sites have been destroyed in the past with the creation of roads, and other developments, but most sites on Federal land are protected.  
  
However many sites with Sarracenia are privately owned. The land owner can choose to develop his land as he chooses. If a person owns a large strip of land which contains a bog, and decides to build a shopping mall on the land, unless there is an endangered species on it, not much can be do to stop the development. Many carnivorous plant habitats have been destroyed due to development. I think most private property bogs are okay, but if a corporation owns a land, there might be a higher probability it would b developed.
