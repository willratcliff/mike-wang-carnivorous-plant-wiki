Sarracenia rubra gets very little attention from the CP world, and I sure hope people start seeing how amazing these plants are!  They put on their best show during the fall, when their largest and most colorful pitchers are formed.  They're a bit difficult to photograph because they form huge 3 dimensional clumps, which is hard to capture in 2D.  Anyways, hope you enjoy them!  
  
S. rubra ssp. gulfensis near crestview, okaloosa Co, FL The population these plants came from is now completely destroyed-the owner now sells prefabricated tool sheds on the property.   This original site is now in the middle of a busy city-really strange to think that the original site used to be out in the middle of nowhere.  

![](../../images/72/724d96b2e4e6fe732e65cba5badf30eddb71ccf00b6fcc8a348e8032ec963d04.jpg)
