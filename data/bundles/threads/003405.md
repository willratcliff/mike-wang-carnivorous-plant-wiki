Here's an old clone of S. leucophylla RED which was originally acquired by California Carnivores.  They acquired this plant from the late carnivorous plant legend Bob Hanrahan.  
  
Peter D'Amato made a really nice cross of S. leucophylla RED x minor okee giant, and this clone was the parent plant!  What was not known for all these years is how far the red can stretch down the petiole (yes hcarlton, I'm calling it the petiole, LOL)  Overall, this clone has medium vigor and needs careful attention to grow to it's fullest potential.  However, this gem has an incredible potential to create hybrids because of how much red is on the trap!  The finnicky-ness totally goes away if the other parent plant is vigorous.   I still have the leuco red x minor giant clone made by Peter, and it's really easy to grow, has lots of red that stretch down the petiole, is vigorous, and can produce huge traps (will take photos once I baby it back to health, plant was neglected for years), but it was only one random seedling from the entire batch.  Imagine if you could select for the darkest, reddest plant out of the entire batch....
