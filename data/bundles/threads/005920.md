Here is perhaps the blackest gulfensis clone in cultivation!  The traps can turn purple black under optimal conditions! I've grown a lot of gulfensis clones over the past 2 decades and maybe the only other clone that comes close to this one in coloration is the ABG clone: [sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/4966/rubra-ssp-gulfensis-red-abg](https://sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/4966/rubra-ssp-gulfensis-red-abg)  
  
I just acquired this division last winter and it was completely shaded by some flavas, so it had everything against it in terms of coloring up this season.  Hopefully next season, it will reach its fullest potential, or maybe even in a few months of full sun and cool temperatures, it might get a bit darker.  I do have pictures of this exact clone at its fullest color potential and I will be posting that soon, so stay tuned!
