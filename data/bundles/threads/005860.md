Here's a unique, giant genotype of S. flava var. flava that forms absolutely massive clumps over time, can produce a beautiful yellow tinge, and gets absolutely GIGANTIC year after year!    Traps consistently exceed 3' in height year after year when grown for the almighty, these aren't like genetic giants that only produce giant traps once every few years.  That said, this variant is only giant in the sense that it produces unusually tall traps, there isn't much girth going on compared to regular forms.  
  
    This is probably the most vigorous "strain" of flava in the collection: the roots are relatively bullet proof and can handle quite a bit of stress (eg. these were neglected years ago and the peat decomposed/disappeared completely, but the plants still survived growing as a tangled mess of roots!).  Even recent divisions start growing strong the first season if cared for properly.   Long description short, these are great for beginners and for collectors who like giant traps, they can take a lot of abuse and still muscle through it!  
  
Pics taken mostly on 6/5/23.  Not sure if you can tell, but these traps are easily over 3' in height!
