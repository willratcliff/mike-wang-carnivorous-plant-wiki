Certainly, the Wilkerson family owns property with some outstanding genetics.  Bud Wilkerson's bog has plants that are very similar to Leah's spot, but the leucos on his property, or at least the ones we were able to get into cultivation are just jaw dropping.  I suspect Wilkerson's red rocket is from Bud's property although I'm not positive.      
  
In any case, you can see that S. lecophylla clone A from Bud's property is very similar looking to Wilkerson's red rocket in terms of shape.  The trap below just opened, so it'll be interesting to see if this plant colors up even more over time.  
  
Just like wilkerson's red rocket, this clone A below produces nice spring pitchers and then doesn't do diddily squat all summer long.  Eventually, after so much waiting it'll send out these enormous, shapely fall pitchers, and there seems to be quite a few more pushing out of the growth points.
