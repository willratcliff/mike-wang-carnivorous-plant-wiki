A few weeks ago, I didn't think anything of this cross: it didn't appear to be any better than either of the two parents, but now that some of the traps have aged a little bit, their color and venation is becoming a lot more pronounced, so maybe this might be something worthy as the batch of seedlings gets bigger.   It was also pretty surprising to see one of the traps turn reddish on the outside, that wasn't expected!  
  
It's still too early to tell if any of these will be winners, but the thread has been made to document this particular cross and to see what happens over time!    
  
  
Didn't have enough room in the title to write down the whole name, it's S. oreophila var. ornata purple throat UCB x MK017 select x flava extreme red throat 'corner clone' Bay Co, FL.  This "corner clone" had an unusually well pronounced red throat compared to others in the population, which is why it was bred with.  Pics taken 6/7/23:
