As some of you may recall in a previous thread, S. leucophylla Hurricane Creek White clone A is a very mysterious plant (as are all the HCW clones in the collection).  For one, the pitchers on the same exact clone can look completely different from one plant to the next.  Sometimes, it produces pinkish pigments below the mouth of  in pitchers, and at other times, it's pure white.  The shape of the pitchers can vary as well.    
  
Here are some recent pictures of spring pitchers.  The fall pitchers will look almost completely different and are typically solid white.  
  
  
All pictures are from one clone, S. leucophylla Hurricane Creek White clone A, Baldwin Co, AL:
