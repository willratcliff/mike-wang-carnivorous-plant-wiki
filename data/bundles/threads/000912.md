#### *waxy*
`post #23` · `id=12231` · `2014-11-18 08:15:58 UTC`

Have "Green and white"s from a couple of different sources. They start with pink or tan flushes/veins and all fade to green and white like HCW. Also got some Jerry's seed - end of 2012 and sowed them February last year. One cross, leuco 07-1 x leuco AJ01, has some nice little 8 inch pitchers. Most come out pink veined and intensify to a nice red, one very much so with a red lip. However, two show the green and white trait and fade. Of course, not knowing what Jerry's leuco 07-1 is I can't say I was surprised, but I am very pleased at the color diversity. Can't wait till they get bigger. Also, Jerry's (leuco x purp 101)x leuco HCW #O has produced a plant with short stocky leuco pitchers that fade but so far don't all disappear, just a very few thin red thread veins on the tube.  
I have observed my two clones of flava maxima develop the same way. Young pitchers are very flushed red and fade as they grow, losing the last of the color right after they have opened to be an even all-over green.

They're almost done filling in!  I dunno, they look about the same as last year, pretty happy with the results.  Timing-wise, the traps seem to have filled in about the same time as last year.  
  
  For those of you new to S. leucophylla, it seems these plants don't like sitting in water for more than 2 days in a row (at least that's true outdoors in Northern California).  These plants are literally watered every day, and by the evening, the reservoir is dry but the soil is still fully hydrated.  In the winter, they NEVER sit in any water, that's a recipe for rot.  Give them good spacing, chop off all the leaves during the winter dormancy, and never let them give you a frown or droopy leaf!
