[https://flic.kr/p/25A3VJy](https://flic.kr/p/25A3VJy)

  

The shape of the flower is perfect!

It's of a huge size and the nice little yellowish trace is (a stable) add on!

It seems to me, to be a hybrid out of a flava and a leucophylla.
