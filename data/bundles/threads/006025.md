This clone is new to the collection, these divisions were recently acquired this past winter and they have settled in quite well!  I had no idea what sort of flava this was (well, it was either flava var. flava, maxima,  or flava ornata given the location) until it started trapping up, but so far, it's looking pretty beautiful!  This is an original clone that originated from the wild as far as I know, it's not seed grown from selfed plants which I do have in the collection, but they're in crammed community pots.  Anyways,  I think next year, once they've had time to fully settle in and get established, the traps may look even more impressive, but for now, they're for sure worth showing.
