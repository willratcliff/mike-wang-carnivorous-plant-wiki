When breeding these alba forms, you think you know what to expect based on experience.....but what long term experience teaches you is that a lot of the times, what you end up getting is way different than what you thought you'd get!  
Here's a great example of an individual that I'd never in a million years dream of making: a leucophylla with a very strange, curved back lid!  Never seen this  trait in the wild and never seen it this extreme in cultivation...until now!   None of the plants' parents exhibited such unusual "lid activity." Is the curved back lid an environmental factor or is it genetic?  I don't know.  What I do know is that the 80's/90's are making a big comeback, and now I have something to contribute to that trend!  
  
Aside from having a very uniquely shaped lid, this is also a pretty darn white clone!  S. leucophylla var. alba 'Mohawk' pics taken 10/3/20:
