`post #2` · `id=46400` · `2021-06-08 17:20:18 UTC`

Pure cutthroats (aka rugelii) aren't generally found any more north outside of the SC Sandhills. There are a few rugelii mixed in there with maxima and var flava, but it's definitely the outlier in coloration. Most SC/NC cupreas that i've seen don't have anywhere near the amount of throat blotch as this clone, with the exception of MKF216 (Greenswamp) and a few others where the color completely fills the throat and underside of the lid.
