Which clone is this?  I'll have to double check, but it's just one of the many select individuals from the population.  Like many of the other red variants of S. flava, these plants aren't the easiest to color up, but once they do, they're pretty darn unbeatable!  
  
Some clones seem to be easier to color up than others, but every last atropurpurea clone in the collection has been selected to produce solid red traps from head to toe under optimal conditions.
