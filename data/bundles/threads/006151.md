Technically speaking, this clone is  called S. leucophylla clone A Escambia Co, FL, but given that it's a proven giant, I think it should be call as such.    
  
This clone comes from my good homie and  flytrap legend Matt Miller of [www.flytrapcare.com](http://www.flytrapcare.com)  So here's the story: Eric Morrow, another homie from back in the day who was once very active on this forum, had a huge collection of mostly seed grown Sarracenia.  Regretfully, he got out of the hobby a few years ago for personal reasons and Matt was able to acquire his entire collection (thank Jesus!).  I remember Matt told me one winter he spent an enormous amount of time and energy (with some help) repotting/dividing Eric's entire collection.  The plants were then babied and well cared for as though Jesus was watching from afar...you don't want to slack and feel the wrath of the lord for not doing a good job at the Lord's work (aka growing crops for Jesus).  Anyways, fast forward to maybe two years ago, Matt and I were chatting and he brought up this freakin giant leuco that produced some absolutely jaw droppingly enormous traps.  I saw the first few pics below.....My eyes instantly bulged out with envy and somehow he ended up sending me a division, YESSSSSSSSSSSS!!!!!!  
  
  
So here are the pics that Matt took and sent me, these traps are GIANT maaan!
