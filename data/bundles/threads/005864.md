There's probably up to a hundred  different seedlings floating around in cultivation by now that look like Adrian Slack, and here's one of them!  Nice thing is, there are 3  distinctive characteristics that separate this clone from the real deal Adrian Slack:  
1) This clone has hybrid vigor, even in cooler climates.  The real deal Adrian Slack doesn't grow very well when it's cold and prefers warmer temperatures.  
2) This clone, so far, colors up much easier than Adrian Slack.  
3) Round Mouth slack has a very distinctive round mouth!  Duh?   
  
Pretty much everything else is the same!  I think it would be a bit tacky to call this S. Adrian Slack 'improved' so it's being called   S. 'Round Mouth Slack' for now, pics taken 6/22/23:
