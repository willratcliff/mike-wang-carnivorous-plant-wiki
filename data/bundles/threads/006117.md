I mean, I guess this is technically a rubricorpora due to the solid red body and yellow top?  I dunno, it's for sure mixed in with some ornata, so no clue if it really neatly fits into any specific category, but for horticultural purposes, I'm calling this one a rubricorpora for now.  This originates from a site that has mostly ornatas and rugeliis, but there are a few je ne sais quois plants in there that have reddish bodies, they're like watered down rubricorporas or rubricorporas in the process of evolving into something else....that said, they probably won't finish their original evolutionary path as the vast majority of the sites that used to exist in Walton Co are long gone.
