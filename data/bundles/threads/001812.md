`post #20` · `id=50379` · `2024-02-14 17:00:12 UTC`

some slightly grainy photos but this shows you how much this clone can color up!  This was a recent division that had finally become fully established about 9 months later, it sometimes takes longer depending on the health of the division and the environmental conditions.  I've had some greenhouse grown divisions (not fully established when acquired) take 2-3 years before they started growing like this and producing nice roots (eg. S. flava var. rugelii GIANT  'bob Hanrahan'), and the rhizome was very large!  It's all about growth momentum, size is less relevant to recovery and large pitcher production.  All else equal, Relatively smaller divisions that had outstanding growth momentum and are fully established can produce much larger traps quicker than a weak, large division.  Pictures below show just that!
