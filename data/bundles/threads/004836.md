But what about improving the solid red throat and upgrading it to an extreme red throat?!  Yup, that's what this thread is all about!  
  
Here's a pic of S. flava extreme red throat 'select':   

![](../../images/68/682a080abff15dcaa66656135b08048517c47c3506536612c7d26571cac47e66.jpg)

  
  
And here are the results of crossing the two together!   This cross wasn't easy to make, both parents are shy seed producers, so you really have to pollinate a lot of flowers to even get a few dozen seeds.  On top of that, the extreme red throat parent plant is heterozygous, so there's quite a bit of segregation in the offspring: only a few produced solid red throats!
