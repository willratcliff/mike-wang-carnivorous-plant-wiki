So, is this a pure leucophylla var. alba, or does it have something mixed in it from several generations back?  Well, if I had to guess, probability-wise, most Sarracenia in existence likely crossed with another species at one point and then back crossed so many times you can't tell.  This individual below is a great example: it totally looks like a pure leucophylla, but it has some alata or rubra wherryi look to it.  Hard to say what's going on here.
