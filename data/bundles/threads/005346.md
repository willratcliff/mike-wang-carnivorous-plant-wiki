Here's an alata clone from Harrison Co, MS that turns all red and colors up easier than many other clones from various different locations!  I think this clone can also get pretty dark, but it's still too early to tell if the trap will turn blackish this season or not.    
  
For those of you who grow the color forms of alatas (outdoors at least), you know how difficult it is to get some of these darn things to color up: some years, even the black clones can stay greenish!  Not this clone though, at least so far!  It's still a seedling, so more time is needed to assess if this is something outstanding or if I'm just getting lucky for the past 3 years or so.  What I'm after are clones that color up easily and readily, and clones that color up outdoors during the summer and produce red traps easily have potential to be the next alata holy grail!  We really need alatas that can color up for everyone, not just Mike Wang or someone with a greenhouse.  The good news is, this clone, along with a few others, are coloring up relatively easily, so I think they're going to be special!
