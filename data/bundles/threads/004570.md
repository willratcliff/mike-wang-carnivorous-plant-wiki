Horry Co. has/had some really big cupreas that were given to us by a home builder. He didn't want the plants to get trashed by the development and knew we grew Sarracenia; nice guy! Anyway, the shape of our giant cupreas  doesn't look at all like that maxima, but having not seen the populations there, who knows what variations existed?  
  
It's funny, right now Horry Co. pops up; we're currently auctioning a couple Horry Co. cuprous on ebay, something we've not done before. The Horry cupreas usually have a "spout" at the mouth, very much like, well, a pitcher.  

![Horry Co. copper top](../../images/03/039074fbc642f5c18d3633a629bda8223bc7d5891bffd8ef0b3b693fa12fc1d1.jpg)

  
  
Somewhere, in the dusty corner of the house, there's a picture of one of these giants next to a ruler/tape. I may be wrong, but I'm remembering one of the Horry copper tops being 42 inches high. In any case, Horry Co. flavas have some giant potential.  
  
By the way, I'm told the pronunciation is: or-RYE.
