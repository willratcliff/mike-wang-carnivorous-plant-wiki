Leucophyllas from Covington Co, AL are extremely rare in both cultivation and in the wild!   Just like the most jaw dropping albas of Baldwin Co, AL, the Covington Co, AL albas, from my experience so far, can be quite chameleon-like and difficult to color up.  Their trap shapes remind me of Okaloosa Co, FL leucos: stout, sturdy, and FAT!     
  
  
S. leucophylla var. alba 'vigorous,white,shapely' Covington Co, AL stuck out of the crowd early on by producing a stunningly white topped trap, even when it was really young!  That was several years ago and ever since it was spotted, I have been trying to get the damn thing to color up, but it kept looking like a regular leucophylla....until now!  Well, it's still not quite at its fullest potential color-wise, the top can get much more solid white, but I'll take this for now.  
  
When the trap first opened, there were many red veins in the throat, but as it aged, the throat became whiter and whiter!  Now imagine breeding this really white chameleon leuco with an alba from washington Co and getting that same sturdy, FATTIE, STOUT structure yet solid white spring and fall traps!  Yeah, I guess we could dream, right?  
  
S. leucophylla var. alba 'vigorous,white,shapely' Covington Co, AL, photos taken 9/9/17.  Again, not at its fullest potential, but so many of you have been asking to see what it looks like, so here you go!  
  

![](../../images/8f/8ffe484c95960612fc8780dc9b7b514c43938ee6a3015bc686c063e10bc818bc.jpg)
