Well, technically, this is S. oreophila var. ornata 'Sand Mountain' clone B, but there wasn't enough space to put that in the subject title.  
  
Here's a clone that colors up nicely during mid to late summer.  Like many other oreophilas from the sand Mountain region in Alabama, this clone can be heavily veined and beautifully colored in the throat.  I'm beginning to wonder how many of your "plain and ordinary" looking oreophilas can display this great color in some years and be a boring solid green in other years.  For certain, S. oreophila intrigues me because of it's "chameleon" like ability to have different phenotypes or physical appearances under various different environmental conditions.
