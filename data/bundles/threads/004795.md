Here's a neat clone of S. flava var. ornata that has been neglected for many year, but is pushing out a decent trap!  Great breeder so far, although not too many crosses have been made with this clone yet!  
  
The red throat becomes solid red once the plant produces bigger traps, but it's not very easy to get this one to color up all the way.  The veins on the exterior, however, are pretty easy to color up year after year.  This clone has excellent shape!  Pics taken 4/19/20:
