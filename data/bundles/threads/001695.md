When I think of the Wilkerson bogs (there's Bud Wilkerson's property and Leah Wilkerson's property), I think of a magician pulling a rabbit out of his hat!  It seems like these two bogs are responsible for more than a dozen different note-worthy clones/cultivars (ie. S. x Leah Wilkerson, leucophylla wilkerson's red rocket, bud wilkerson, johnny wilkerson, etc).  
  
Now that I've over-hyped these amazing places, here's a standard leucophylla from one of the Wilkerson's bog.  Nice thing is if these bogs go extinct for whatever reason (it's not uncommon for sites to be destroyed after they change ownership), at least we have relic divisions of some of the more "average" plants for future enthusiasts/scientists to study.  
  
What I like about this S. leucophylla clone is that it looks similar to the okaloosa Co, FL populations, but there's something unique about it that I can't quite pinpoint that makes it have that Wilkerson bog look.
