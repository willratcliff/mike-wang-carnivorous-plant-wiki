Here's an interesting clone of S. minor var. okefenokeensis from the Ware Co, GA population.  The windows on this clone are consistently either edged with red, or are completely red.  Other minor giant clones in the collection do get red windows as well, but they don't seem to have red pigments in the windows consistently.
