Yet another interesting clone of S. flava var. maxima from Berkeley Co, SC. One really neat feature of S. flava var. maxima is that under optimal conditions, the traps can appear to be almost neon yellow (although this one photographed below just has a yellow tinge to it).  This particular clone is shapely and symmetrical with a moderately thin "neck."    This is a new addition to the collection, so I don't know much about its growth habits other than it seems pretty easy and fast growing so far.
