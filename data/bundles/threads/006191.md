I started several 4" community pots of S. leucophylla Burgundy x dark pink way back in 2012, forgot about them, and they have been in the "neglected" section for 11 years now!  No transplanting, no spacing out, just a ton of crowded seedlings jam packed in some completely decomposed peat!  Well, this winter I went through the section, hoping to clean some of it up and make space for more plants (expanding is no longer an option, water is the limitation).  After going through tons of really old crosses, I found this one pot that had what looks like an exceptional individual in it!  I carefully separated it from the rest, brought it to my wife and asked "does this look cute?" and she said YES!  For this reason, I kept it, and now it's being propagated for distribution.  Hopefully I'll get divisions ready around September 2026.  
  
That said, this plant has not been taken to it's fullest potential, and now I'm pretty curious to see what it can do.  Pics taken December 2025:
