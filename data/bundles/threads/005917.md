What happens when you water down a reddish clone with some alba genes?  You get something like this!  Fortunately, we are not in ancient Egypt, where you get drowned in the Nile for watering down your beer....no, watering down your leucophyllas in this day and age results in maybe a tipping of the hat, a pat on the back, a thumbs up, or maybe even a heart emoji if you're lucky.  
  
Anyways, I just noticed this clone this season so it's been labeled and it'll be propagated this coming winter.  Since the trap isn't yet at vegetative maturity and we had suboptimal weather this season, my gut says it'll look even more amazing in the years to come.
