Here's a new select clone of atrorubra that came from a giant pheno hunt aka out of hundreds upon hundreds of different seedlings!  These color forms of alata are a bit tricky to find: if you stuff the plants into a community tray like I do and have them with pretty much no space, the plants shade each other, so even the best red forms don't color up since they need full sun to get to their fullest potential.  Fortunately, this individual was on the side of the tray and was getting blasting, unobstructed full sun!  
  
One of the unique characteristic of this clone is that it can turn solid red all the way down to the bottom of the petiole (leaf)!   The red coloration is also quite dark, this clone might arguably be an nigropurpurea, but only time will tell.  What I can say is that this clone colors up easier than the darker clones like MK A28 and MKA58, which says a lot because those are two proven, very dark clones!  Usually, the darker ones tend to color up relatively easier than the rest, but this clone is an exception.    
  
These were divisions from the mother plant, and these colored up the first season after being divided, which is unusual.  Typically, they need to get established for a year after being divided and then will color up the following year!
