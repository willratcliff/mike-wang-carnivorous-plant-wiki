As far as a homie knows,  I think there's 4 AF flava clones in cultivation: the AF clone from Colquitt Co, GA, Brunswick Co, NC, Liberty Co, FL, and S. flava 'suspicion.'     
  
Since there are only rugelii's in Liberty Co, FL, we can safely assume that this clone originated from a rugelii!  Zero red pigments show up, even when the plant is damaged by insects or has some form of physical injury!  All other normal rugeliis without the anthocyanin free mutation will show some red coloration under such circumstances.  
  
So far, this clone is quite vigorous and easy to grow!  I haven't had it for very long so I don't know much more about it, will post updated in the future as I learn more.  All I know is that this clone was dang hard to find, been chasing the AF flavas for many years now with no luck until recently.  For those of you just joining the Sarracenia community, you've entered the Sarracenia renaissance period: so much amazing material has become widely available in recent years, most of which even the nerdiest of nerds couldn't get back in the day!
