I totally missed the flowers last season and almost missed them this season and now the reason has become apparent: This particular genotype flowers many weeks later than the Southern genotypes!  It's also quite unusual in the sense that it appears to be a dwarf variant: the "roots" aka underground "junk" (perhaps this term is only known by MW at this point) is significantly smaller than all other genotypes grown under identical care/conditions.  
  
Like all other Pogonia clones in the collection, these grow in 100% peat and are pretty
