suppose it's time to make a thread on this clone, haven't done it yet.  One of the most popular montanas I have in the collection, this clone is highly sought after because of it's color, vigor, and overall looks.  That said, montanas can turn different colors based on environmental conditions (eg. solid red versus green lids/red bodies), so keep that in mind when growing this plant.  
  
I think the saddest truth to this clone is that the mother plant that it came from was absolutely stunning!  I'm still bent out of shape that I lost it probably a decade ago, but I think we have all settled for its offspring!  Here's a thread on clone AA: [sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/274/large-clone-montana-pic-update](https://sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/274/large-clone-montana-pic-update)  
  
It's vigor, color, and ability to form massive specimens over time has not been lost in the offspring!
