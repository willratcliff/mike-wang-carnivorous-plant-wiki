Here's a neat bladderwort from Mexico that's quite easy to grow and I've managed to overwinter it outdoors here in Northern Califoria where the night temperatures during the winter can get down into the low 20's!  That said, the plant is grown near the house, which radiates heat and probably keeps the temperatures above 30F.  For sure, I've seen the pot frozen and it didn't like that at all, but they survived!    
  
This particular clone is the "large leaved" (whatever those modified plant organs are called) clone that produces an abundance of flowers pretty much all season long.  The reason I ended up getting this clone a few years ago is because as a kid, Chuck Powell (Danny Powell's pops) hooked me up a clone that looked just like this!  AS a matter of fact, DAnny was super young at the time and I was visiting their house to buy carnivorous plants (that's how it was done in the 90's folks, no internet sales!)  Before I left, DAnny handed me this Utricularia and I kept it going for a good 10 years! Sadly, I ended up losing it when I went to college and that was a huge regret!  That's what happens when you leave your collection and have your parents care for it while you're gone....don't get me wrong, I'm super grateful for my parent's help, I just wished I took some over to UC Davis with me!    It was maybe 15 years later that I acquired a clone that looks just like it, blooms just like it, and man, it seems like the exact same thing I had during my childhood!   
  
This clone was surprisingly very challenging to acquire: I had been looking for it for years, but it was only available via private growers.  I had to "shoulder tap" quite a few times with no luck until eventually someone had it and was willing to sell me one!  The first plant arrived completely dead (It was shipped from AZ and probably fried on the way here).  The seller asked me to wait a few weeks to make sure it was dead, and after proving that it was for sure long gone, he sent me a replacement.  GOSH!  I guess waiting a few weeks wasn't bad compared to the many years it took to finally track down this thing again!
