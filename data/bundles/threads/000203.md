#### *bogman*
`post #14` · `id=31507` · `2017-03-19 15:43:05 UTC`

Gotta agree with Kiwi; I much prefer the look of lids. Plus, in our climate, plants without a good lid tend to fill up with rain water and break. We actually try to breed lid cover into some of our hybrids to keep rain out. There are some, though, that we stake because the lids are more vertical/fan shaped. Flava x purp. is a good example of a hybrid that often breaks or falls over without staking. Three inches of rain can down a lot of pitchers. Guess we could take a red-hot nail and burn in some small drain holes!
