Here's a really interesting natural hybrid of montana x jonesii.  I don't know for sure, but I'm pretty sure this originated from Larry Mellichamp.  Took some lousy photos, but at least you can get an idea of what this looks like!  
  
Very easy to grow, vigorous, and quite colorful,  this short and compact plant produces its best pitchers in the fall, which is only a few weeks away!  More pics in the near future, here are some late summer traps!  Pics taken 8/17/22:
