Here's another clone for the photo-library, had it for years but kept it in a small pot hidden amongst tons of other stuff that was shading it.  Moving things around and now this clone got a little more light, so there's some traps to snap pics of.  
  
S. flava var. ornata 'tall,vigorous,heavily veined' Okaloosa Co, Fl, pics taken 6/17/19.  Given the fact that the traps on this plant are pretty small, I'm pretty impressed with how heavily veined this clone is!  Many ornatas really need to produce big traps to look their best, but this one is already showing a lot of color!  In my mind, this is great breeding material if you want to focus on plants that don't need to be grown very well to be showy.  sorry about the blurry pics, will try to snap better shots of this plant:
