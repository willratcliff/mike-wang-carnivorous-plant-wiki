Here's another clone of rubricorpora that has been in the collection for decades, so it's probably a good time to get this up in the library!  It has that classic red body, yellow top!  This originated from Art Junier's collection, and I acquired it around 2000.    It's probably in circulation, but I don't think I've propagated it since maybe 2008 or so.  It definitely justifies the space it takes up in the collection!  This winter, it'll be divided so that others will have the chance to try it out!  unfortunately, there are probably hundreds of clones in the collection that have been neglected or not noticed, I suppose that's when you know you have a problem?   A good problem to solve!
