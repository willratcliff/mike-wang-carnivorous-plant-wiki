I mean, from a technical standpoint, this is a cuprea by definition, but I'm not sure I'd like to lump this together with the carolina cupreas, which are genetically different and of a different hybrid origin.  
  
This clone is likely a cross between an atropurpurea and flava rugelii, aka a watered down atropurpurea!  It could even be an atro x atro, since many atros are actually polyhybrids and recessive traits can show up in the F1 generation.  Thing is, when you water down atros, you don't often get this sort of  look, you usually get ornata or atros that don't quite fully color up even under optimal conditions.
