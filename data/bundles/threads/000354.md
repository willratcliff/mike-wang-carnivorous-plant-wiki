Here's a unique clone of S. rubra ssp. rubra 'long lid.'  For one, the plant is consistently red, and the pitchers are slightly taller in general compared to your typical S. rubra ssp. rubra.  I almost wonder if this is indeed an ancestral form, or perhaps an undescribed variant.  I also have a rubra rubra from Onslow Co, NC that also has a long lid, but it's green and short like your typical rubra ssp. rubras.
