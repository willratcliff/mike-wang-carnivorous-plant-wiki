Here's some genotypes from Calhoun Co, FL that have been in the collection for a while, but they never really thrived and were neglected.  Now that they've been repotted and given a good year to get established, they're starting to look like something!   
  
Just most other rosea clones I've grown from throughout their naturally occurring range, I think these individuals have some pretty extreme phenotypic variance, especially in the fall.  We'll see this year if some of these traps get pretty bulbous, I think some will!
