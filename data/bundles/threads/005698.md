I guess this thread will include multiple different attempts to improve the extreme red throat moorei's!  Feel free to add any of your crosses, would love to see them!  
  
My goal with improving the extreme red throat moorei is to get whiter bodies while still maintaining the extreme red throat.  These are just preliminary pictures: as the traps age, they can become snow white, like an alba!  I transplanted and divided everything last winter, so they're not coloring up as hoped just yet, maybe they will as the traps age, or maybe I have to wait another season, we'll see!
