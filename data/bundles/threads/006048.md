Thank you, subject line, for not letting me write out the entire long as Ef name of this clone, which is S. alata var. rubrioperculata 'blood red interior' Jackson Co, MS!  That red color under the lid is pretty dang special, it's such a vibrant red, and it contrasts well with the greenish color of the trap!  
  
This clone is relatively easy to grow, and since we're not talking about the body turning solid red or dark purple/black, it's also relatively easy to color up!  That red coloration under the lid is pretty dang dominant and not too picky, although it still needs a good 6+ hours of direct sunlight to get that coloration.  It'll probably still get red in part shade, but won't look as vibrant or healthy.
