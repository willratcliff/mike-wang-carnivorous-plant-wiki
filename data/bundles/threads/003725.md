One of the best "pure" rubricorpora crosses I've made was S. flava var. rubricorpora (Burgundy x clone L(dark)) Liberty Co, FL.  Out of that entire batch, there were quite a few individuals with really deep red  bodies/yellow top. The runts and other individuals that didn't color up very easily(which was probably 60% of the batch), were tossed.    However, a single plant turned out to be a stunning ornata!  
  
This trap just opened, and the "backside" was facing the sun, so the veins on the "front" aren't as pronounced yet.  These pics don't do this clone justice, but you can sorta get an idea of what it looks like.  Photos taken 4/21/17:
