I've had this clone for a while now but haven't gotten around to photo-documenting it until now!  The wilkerson's properties definitely have some rather unique genetics: the closest populations that come close to them appearance wise are from Okaloosa Co, FL.  Still, there's a unique look to them that you don't find anywhere else, it's one of those Je ne sais Quoi sorta situations aka hard to pinpoint the details.  
  
This clone produces avererage to large sized pitchers, and since I've sorta neglected them in the past, they tend to be nothing but a pile of phyllodia until the fall.  can they produce nice spring traps?  Yes, they can and they have.  Vigor-wise, it's pretty fast growing.  
  
Once the traps get really big (not pictured), you can tell the upper "head" section of this plant is relatively wide compared to other clones.  Other than that, it's a pretty and typical leucophylla!  
  
Pics taken 8/18/19.  These just opened, so I'll try to snap more photos of them as the traps age.  Bear in mind, young, just opened traps sometimes have duller, less intense colors compared to older traps:
