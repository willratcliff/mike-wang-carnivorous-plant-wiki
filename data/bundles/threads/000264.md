Yeah, I have a few different clones from Bob Hanrahan's property, and they tend to put up really boring, skinny, nothing-to talk about pitchers in the spring and summer.  At times, they only produce phyllodia for the summer.  But during the fall, they have the most spectacular show-some of the traps are gigantic too!  It's very odd because if you look at pictures of his plants growing on his property during the summer, it appears they're producing some decent sized pitchers.  Perhaps it's just an illusion though-there's 10's of 1000's of plants there, and I bet a ton of them are multi-crowned, so while it looks like there's a lot of pitchers, there's probably only 1 or 2 per growth point.
