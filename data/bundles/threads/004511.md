I guess this thread is to photo-document multiple giant clones of S. flava var. atropurpurea from Okaloosa Co, FL.  I had a hunch that one of the clones that I selected was going to be a giant, but turns out 3 of them so far are confirmed monsters!    
  
Before you ask the question, let me answer it: it seems the majority of atropurpurea clones, at least under my outdoor conditions, start off kinda red but mostly green, but as the traps age, they turn solid red from head to toe.  
  
How do I know these are giants?  
1) the "head" of the trap is enormous  
2) one of plants has nearly 3' tall traps in a 4" pot  
3) the lids are enormous!
