I guess you could call these extreme red throats, but since they're from Okaloosa territory and the famous Brooks Garcia named the first red throated flava clone killer, I figured it makes sense to carry on this name.   
  
As far as I know, besides flava killer, there are no other red throated clones like these from Okaloosa Co, FL in cultivation, nor have I seen such genetics in the wild, although some complex hybrids come close.  Speaking of hybrids, these are the result of flava color forms mixing up with each other, although I do not know the exact parents, since these came from a large seed batch from multiple clones being crossed with each other.  
  
So far, unlike tha original killer clone, these babies are vigorous and super easy to grow in my cooler climate, so I expect them to perform very well under a variety of conditions.
