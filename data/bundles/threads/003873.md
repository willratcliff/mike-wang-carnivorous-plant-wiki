Now, it's hard to complain about having a yellow flowered variant that also is one of the whitest clones out there!  This is one of the few individuals that rivals hurricane creek white clone F, and to make things even more interesting, these produce an abundance of very solid white summer traps!  however, the fall traps are much bigger and even whiter!   
  
My hope in the future is to breed for super solid white traps that have the pigments stretch far down the petiole.  Fortunately, the washington Co, Al population has sufficient genetic ingredients to potentially make that plant, but as with all crosses, you never know what will happen until you jump in and try!  I have yet to see, both in nature and in cultivation, a yellow flowered plant that has white stretch 2" or more down the petiole....wouldn't that be something?
