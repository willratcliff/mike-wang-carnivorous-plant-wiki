After 20+ years of having this clone in the collection, it's probably about time to write up a thread on it!  This clone was acquired at the very first ICPS meeting in 1997 in Atlanta, GA!  It's either a genotype from Ben Hill Co, GA or from Liberty Co, FL, but since my record organizational skills weren't developed at the time (I was a teen!), location data for this clone is uncertain.  
  
This individual has medium vigor but a nice, well defined red throat and shapely traps!  It's clearly very rot resistant, otherwise it wouldn't still exist in the collection, hah!  Probably the most note-worthy trait about this clone is it's genetics: for whatever reason, when outcrossed with other rugelii clones, the offspring turn out really well!    
  
S. flava var. rugelii clone B, pics taken 5/21/23.  This "mother plant" in the picture is actually not anywhere near as healthy as the divisions: it hasn't been repotted in over 6 years, yet somehow it's still pushing out traps and looking okay!
