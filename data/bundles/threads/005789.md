#### *kiwiearl*
`post #4` · `id=49412` · `2023-02-02 09:57:01 UTC`

Sometimes, yes. My advice is wait to see the clones at full maturity before finally selecting or understanding the characteristics of an individual clone. Especially when crosses or hybrids are involved.   
  
Also, bear in mind different growing conditions will cause a individual plant to express different colours. So, what may appear to not be an overly red plant may in fact just need the right conditions to display that. The differences in expression between growing under glass, full outdoor sun or - worst case scenario, but reality for some - the only marginal conditions one might be able to grow a plant in can be vast. North, south, east or west can, of course, define how a plant may express itself.   
  
Patience can deliver up great results, even if unexpected. Having been patient, realizing that playing the long game paid off is great fun.  
  
Hope you get what you are after.
