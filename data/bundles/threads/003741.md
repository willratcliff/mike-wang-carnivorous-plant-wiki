Here in the San Francisco Bay Area, people earning 6 figures are struggling to make end's meet. You might think "no way, they're exaggerating" but it's absolutely true.  My family has been facing the same challenge here.  To give you perspective:  
1) Gardener: $35/hr (that's considered on the cheaper side, the ones you don't have to manage are $50/hr)  
2) daycare: $1200-$2K per Month.  If you prefer the one where the kids bite, it's $800/month, but that was 2 years ago, I'm sure it's gone up since then.  
3) Preschool (private): $1,900-$2,500 per month, even for the crappy ones that feel like jail cells.  Average wait list is 1-2 years long.  My mom didn't even believe me when I told her the prices.  
3) High School(private): $47,000/year!!!  
4) 6,000 sq. foot vacant lot (residential zoning), no house on it sold for close to $800K earlier this year on the SF Peninsula.    
  
  
Here's an article that sums it all up:  [www.msn.com/en-us/money/realestate/twitter-employee-making-dollar160000-in-san-francisco-says-hes-scraping-by/ar-BBAgLrS?li=BBnb7Kz](http://www.msn.com/en-us/money/realestate/twitter-employee-making-dollar160000-in-san-francisco-says-hes-scraping-by/ar-BBAgLrS?li=BBnb7Kz)
