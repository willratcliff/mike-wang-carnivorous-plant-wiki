Now you might be thinking, what does making craft beer have to do with Cephalotus?  IF you answered "nothing," you're completely correct!  This clone originates from the legendary Ceph grower Charles Brewer: as an aside, he was one of the few consistent sources for Cephalotus in the US for the longest time.  He was consistently quick to respond to requests, even if he wasn't able to offer what you're after.    Anyways, I reached out to him this past fall and I wasn't able to acquire anything, he's essentially retired from selling Cephalotus.  
  
That said, this red clone is a select, "glorified" typical that is quite vigorous and good looking!  This clone can really produce some beautiful red traps with the right conditions, has great vigor, and the traps get large but not gigantic.   The patterns under the lid are very well defined with the right conditions as well!  Some typical clones have this Je ne sais quois look to them and lack of vigor that make them less desirable, IMO, compared to a clone like this!
