`post #1` · `id=20019` · `2015-07-22 22:28:25 UTC`

When I finally noticed this plant for the first time in 2 years, the tag had to be double checked....wow, this really looks like a jonesii, but it does look very  unique and not quite like the jonesiis I've grown.  If anyone else has a crawford Co, GA ancestral, I'd love to see it!  
  
Other ancestrals from nearby populations look very different.  For example, the marion Co ancestrals look more like gulfensis, whereas some of the taylor Co plants look more like rubra ssp. rubra.  The Peach Co, GA clone I have looks just like like a cross between a jonesii and a gulfensis, it's so weird!  I suspect the ancestral group is incredibly genetically diverse.  
  
One key trait about this clone: even on a relatively small rhizome, the pitchers are crazy tall!  This trap photographed is about 2'(0.6 meters) tall!  I couldn't get my darn camera to focus on the whole trap, but will try again later so you can see just how tall it looks.
