I’m a bit desensitized to moorei crosses at this point. A lot of similar hybrids, including this one in my opinion, look good and interesting, such that I am hard pressed to pull one out of the crowd as great unless it has something I’ve never seen before among moorei and related crosses. But I admit, soo many out there look good and have interesting qualities that I respect.  
  
For this plant, what draws me in is the white color and shape of ~whimsical~ lid.
