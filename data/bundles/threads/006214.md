While many of you may be familiar with S. flava var. ornata 'improved,' which is a great looking, excellent performing red veined flava that you can read up on here: [sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/4112/flava-var-ornata-improved](https://sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/4112/flava-var-ornata-improved)  over the years I wanted to create a taller plant with "cleaner" veins and I think S. flava var. ornata 2.0 might just be the one!   It also has great shaped traps, it's hard to imagine a red veined ornata variant looking better than this!  However, this version isn't perfect and there is definitely room for improvement.  
  
 I have no doubt something better will come about in the future, it's just a matter of time.  Maybe someone has already made something better and it's just not well known!  It only took around 20 years to come up with these original improved ornatas LOL  In my defense, it wasn't 20 years of breeding: a huge amount of time was observing large seedling batches over long periods of time to see which, if any, could withstand the test of time.  
  
Back when these original crosses were made, there was no  AI or a huge community of the brightest minds sharing their thoughts on breeding at the time, it was literally a shot in the dark and an enormous numbers game.  What you don't see are all the crosses that were made that failed, which were most of them!  That said,  the odds of making something better in less time today is higher, but breeding is part science and part art, so even though I have a lot of experience and knowledge, it doesn't make me any more likely to breed something better than anyone else out there!  Hopefully, this inspires you to get out there and make your own crosses to see what can be done!
