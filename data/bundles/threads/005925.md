Also known as Chipola Giant, this variant is composed of several different clones that can all size up under optimal conditions.  Still quite rare in the US, I don't really even know that many hardcore growers who have it, but it has been propagated and is slowly circulating into collections.  It appears to be more widespread in Europe, but even that, there aren't too many reports of it.  
  
All of the clones can size up and they're relatively fast growing, but they do prefer warm weather to grow well.  My climate here is colder than most areas during the summer but we do get consistent heat waves during the fall year after year, and during that short window of time, this clone puts on maybe 60% of its growth.  The most vigorous clone appears to be this wavy lidded one pictured below.  
  
Last year, they sized up very well but I failed to photograph them, so here are some pics from this season.  They were divided last winter, so they spent most of the season recovering from that!  They didn't size up all the way but produce decently large traps.
