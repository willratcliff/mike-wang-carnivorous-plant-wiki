Here's a spectacular S. x naczii (formerly known as S. x catesbaei) that is a cross between S. rosea x flava var. rugelii.  S. rosea  was selected as the mother plant in this case.  Both parents were from the same county and state, which makes this hybrid true to location.  
  
I've seen many of these hybrids before, but this one stands out because of the great color.  Photos taken 5/4/14:  

![](../../images/01/01b6effe58853094ebcfb3857f1fa1c3c2346ee869f7a5685e41e92b9e229f62.jpg)
