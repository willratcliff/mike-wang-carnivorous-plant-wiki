Here's another interesting clone, which is a bit similar to clone N in the sense that it has a green body, but it has a slightly different shape.  The green color can turn slightly yellow as the season progresses.  I believe warm days and cool nights really entice the plant to produce those yellow colors.  
  
Like most okee giants from this population, this plant is a fast and vigorous grower.  Last year, the plant grew a lot of traps, but they were all either a foot or under.  This year, every trap is moderately giant.  Like S. leucophylla, these plants tend to produce gigantic traps in the fall when you have both warm days and warm nights.
