S. leucophylla "neon pink" is looking amazing again this year. Interestingly, the spring traps have minimal areolation compared to the summer and fall traps, but the color and vigor are so phenomenal that I don't mind at all! These bad boys are nearly black in the mouth, and the color stretches almost all the way down the tube. While clones like "Black Sheer" can certainly get darker, most photos I've seen of it don't exhibit nearly this much "stretch" of pigment down the pitcher.
