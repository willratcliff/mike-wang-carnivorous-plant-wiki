I had no idea how amazing this clone is until this year!  There's soooo many plants in the collection that somehow, these went un-noticed for at least a month: I literally water them every day, but somehow this plant eluded me until last night.  
  
What makes 4520 clone#2 so impressive?  Probably the amount of stretch that goes on with this baby!  It could be an alba, but since it's never shown its "whiter side" (pardon the lack of PC), it's labeled as a regular form for now.  
  
Some people talk about how they sit their leucos in water all year around without any rot problems and it seems plausible, but over here, I've never been able to succeed with leucos doing just that.  This clone in particular is very sensitive to sitting in water for long periods of time, so keep that soil fully hydrated at all times, but not water logged for prolonged periods of time.
