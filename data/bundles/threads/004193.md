Here's a really interesting S. oreophila clone that has heavy veins, a purple (ish) throat, and most imporantly, gets an incredible orange tinge once the traps age!  Right now, the plant hasn't yet fully colored up, but it's looking pretty nice and just starting to get some interesting colors.  
  
In the past few year, as the traps age, they all turned a slight orange color, so I'm thinking this is likely a genetic trait.  Hopefully it will show its true colors in the next month or so, but for now, there's still a pretty nice show.  Photos taken 6/18/18:
