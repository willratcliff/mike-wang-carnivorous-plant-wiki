Holy(*bed*) **SHEET**!!!  That's what came to mind the second I saw this trap in the distance....well, it was more like a long laundry list of explicatives, but I'll try to keep this PG-13, where parental guidance is suggested but not necessary.  
  
This particular clone is called S. flava var. rugelii 'vigorous, well defined red throat' Covington Co, AL for a reason: the red throat is almost like the extreme red throat variant, and this particular individual grows much faster than its relatives from the same population.  The traps are pretty big considering how small the rhizome is, so I suspect it can get giant once it reaches vegetative maturity.  
  
The first trap to open this year on this clone had an okay red throat, but the lid is pretty darn gigantic relatively speaking!  The second trap to open, on the other hand, is something that caught my eye from a distance: this clone definitely sticks out and triggers those have a potty mouth to curse with joy....well, maybe it's not all that amazing to everyone, but I think our fellow Sarracenia OG-Nerdz know where I'm coming from....
