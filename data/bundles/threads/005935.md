Here's the beginning of a thread on this small minor population in the collection from Brantley Co, GA.  All I know is that it's extremely rare and I've heard of others growing similar clones but never seemed to be able to acquire them for whatever reason until about a decade ago (LOL)!  These plants were crammed in a community pot in the "weedy section" and hadn't been transplanted since I acquired them: they were near death and I lost a bunch of original clones due to the neglect. Last winter, I divided them and gave them new soil.   Here they are after getting new soil and being pampered a bit, I'll have more info on them this coming grow season!
