Here's a "new" population of Sarracenia psittacina from Santa Rosa Co, FL, lots of diversity and it's just an absolute joy growing these out and seeing what they end up looking like.  parrot pitcher plants have incredible phenotypic plasticity: in non-geek terms, this means that a single plant can look drastically different as it gets bigger or even as it's grown under different environmental conditions.  The color can be different, as well as the shape of the pitchers!  Earlier this year, I found a very round headed clone from Okaloosa Co, FL, but a few months later, the traps reverted back to a normal, no-so-round, parrot-head shape!  Weird.  
  
Anyways, here's the population of psittacinas from Santa Rosa Co, FL, pics taken 12/7/21.  These plants hate my weather, it's just too cold in the fall and they grow pretty darn slow, but they still look good!
