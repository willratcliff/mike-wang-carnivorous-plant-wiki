I'm running out of names, and technically speaking, I think this name is invalid should the word abaxial be latin.  I mean come on, MW isn't an academic yet he's trying to flex some "big words" to impress people?  Naw, homies ain't like that.  I already used "white back" on another clone, and at the time this one was named, it had a very white back!  Well, this year, the back of the pitcher is average for a leucophylla because only the side of the trap facing full sun will turn very white.    However, overall, the pitchers are turning quite white!  Is this an alba?  I'm leaning towards thinking that it probably is, but whatever the case may be, the traps overall are really white.  
  
This clone is incredibly vigorous, and when the fall traps first opened up, they weren't very white.  However, now that they've aged a bit, the color has become more intense.  Photos taken 10/29/18:
