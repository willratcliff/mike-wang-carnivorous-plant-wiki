Here's a cross between S. rosea(mother plant) and S. flava var. rugelii (pollen donor) from the same County.  Out of the batch, this was the only individual that had a solid green body and nice red throat.  It was pretty interesting to see seedlings from this batch varied in color, shape, and the presence/absence of a red throat...it was all over the place!  Perhaps one or more of the parents in the past was the result of complex hybridization.  
  
S. naczii 'red throat,green body' Okaloosa Co, FL, photos taken 5/1/18.  The red throat will probably get redder as the plant produces bigger traps:
