Here's another site of S. leucophylla that we had never seen before, and it was just spectacular!  We found more S. leucophylla var. albas, and the field was pristine.  This site didn't seem to be maintained like some of the other sites, but in any case, the population looked healthy, and it seems like this site will sustain itself in the long run as long as it doesn't get developed!  Very close by, there are (surprise, surprise!) slash pine plantations, and my fingers are crossed that this spot doesn't turn into one as well!  It's the very last  large standing population of S. leucophyllas in the immediate area...every other surrounding parcel of land has either been cleared or planted.   Interestingly enough, the biggest threat out here is agriculture, not residential, industrial, or commercial developments.
