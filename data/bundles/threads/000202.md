Kiwiearl, as you probably have experienced, the traps of S. flava var. rubricorpora in general  have the most striking/contrasting yellow lid/dark red body for about the first 2 weeks after it opens.  After that, the bright yellow lid turns dark red and eventually, some people would call it S. flava var. atropurpurea, since the entire trap can become solid red.
