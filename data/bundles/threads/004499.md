As gotsarrs mentioned, there are some dogs in the registered list, things we would throw out or test new chemicals on! Since this cross looks like many other, similar crosses, you could name it with the proviso that it CAN be seed-raised as long as offspring retain certain traits. I'm thinking of doing that with flava Cinnamon Sticks (cuprea x rubricorpora), but adding only plants with dark red-brown lids and tubes qualify. We raised a ton of seedlings, but only some gave us the color combination we were looking for.  
  
Now, those black plants you gots: after you sell one to me, so I can verify it's not strictly environmental (LOL), that'd be a no-brainer to register.  
  
It's good-looking enough, and as comments indicate, tastes are individual. Maybe the ones worth the **hassle** of registering are the ones you don't need to ask about.
