This clone is **S. flava var. ornata (Gulf Coast, FL x black veined Bulloch Co, GA) clone B**.  I have two clones from the seed batch that stood out, and this individual, clone B, is probably one of the most spectacular S. flava var. ornatas in the collection.  It has a bronzy overall color to it, and the veins on the lid are dark black.   This clone inherited the beautiful shape and dark black, detailed veins from the pollen donor (the black veined flava) and the bronzy background color was contributed by  the mother plant.  There doesn't seem to be anything else like it in cultivation or in the wild.  
  
Unfortunately, despite being an F-1 outcross, which generally results in hybrid vigor, this individual was skipped in that department.  I suppose that's the price it paid for its good looks!  Fortunately, it has medium vigor and isn't as bad as the original black veined father plant,.  AFter growing the original black veined flava for more than 15 years, I'm still having a hard time getting them to produce specimen sized plants!  
  
  
Anyways, here are the pics!  It was hard to capture the darkness of the veins because of the glare from the lighting-these look a bit blacker when you see it in person than the original bulloch Co, GA black veined parent plant:
