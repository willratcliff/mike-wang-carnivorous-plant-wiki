The famous plant that was originally collected in 1991 from Northern Florida and registered in 2008, S. x Royal Ruby is a moorei hybrid known as an outstanding parent plant of stunning hybrids (ie. Reptilian Rose and a ton of Phil's masterpieces).  Here's a link to the official cultivar description: [www.carnivorousplants.org/cpn/Species/v38n1p12_22.html](http://www.carnivorousplants.org/cpn/Species/v38n1p12_22.html)    Its fullest potential in breeding has perhaps yet to be seen, but from what's pretty much Phil has done single-handedly with it (not sure if he used 2 hands though, LOL), you can tell with the right "cook" in the kitchen, one can come up with hybrids beyond our imagination.  Even if you don't do any breeding, this plant is a keeper!   
  
Royal Ruby  produces excellent spring/summer traps, and if you happen to have the right environmental ingredients (or a controlled greenhouse), it'll also produce some stunning fall pitchers.  For me, I was probably missing a little bit of salt in my environment because my fall traps this year turned out colorful yet not as big as I was hoping.  Similarly, Phil Faulisi also had some smaller looking fall traps on his well established mother plants  this year, so perhaps our California weather is to blame.  Edit: Phil's spring/summer traps as seen below are outstanding!  
  
In any case, check out the color on this thing!  Yeah, it's pretty amazing.  Photos taken 9/27/15:
