Here's a very unique registered cultivar created by Travis Wyman and grown out/selected by Rob Co of the pitcher plant project!  It's  Reptilian Rose x flava var. rubricorpora x leucophylla!  It was registered very recently, here's a link to the description: [cpn.carnivorousplants.org/articles/CPNv51n2p109_123.pdf](https://cpn.carnivorousplants.org/articles/CPNv51n2p109_123.pdf)  
  
I've been eyeballing and trying to acquire this clone for so many years, but was always told that it wasn't available until almost 2 ago, I got a super tiny division!  It has since tripled in size, but is still very small LOL  Pics taken 7/12/23:
