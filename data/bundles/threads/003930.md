Another new S. leucophylla RED clone from Franklin Co, FL.  The red pigments are very well pronounced, even on pitchers that have just opened!  So far, the main difference between this clone and all the others?  It's pretty darn fast growing, and even with a relatively small rhizome, it's produce decently large traps!  The notes that I wrote on the tag indicate that the traps got reddish in the interior, but I'm not seeing that just yet this year.   There is another clone in the collection called 'RED Interior' that seems to have more dominant red interior genes, so for now this clone won't be noted for such characteristics.
