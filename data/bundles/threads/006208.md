I mean, I'm more of a fan of the OLD Hampshire, but since that only exists as a figment of my imagination, I suppose we have to settle for purpureas from New Hampshire.  Now, if you're scratching your head wondering WT EF is Mike Wang talking about, you're probably normal.  
  
I've had this small batch of purpurea ssp. purpurea from Strafford Co, NH for several years now and never thought much about it....UNNNNNTILLLLLLL!!!! (sorry, stole that from the youtuber guy) I saws this plant this winter and it produced some seriously near BLACK traps!  Holy Filoli  historic house and Gardens!!!!  Now, the sad thing about these genetics is that they're EXTREMELY difficult to keep alive long term.  I have maybe only 1/3 of what I started off with, and hopefully the survivors are the strong ones but I'm not counting on it.  Purpureas are weird: some populations I've had for almost 30 years now, others grow well for a solid 5 years and then CRASH!  This seems like one of those 5 year type populations, but hopefully I at least get a chance to cross this darn thing into some other dark purpurea ssp. purpurea and keep the genetics going that way.  I think that's the plan for now.  Needless to say, I haven't had any luck getting divisions from any of these mother plants....yet.
