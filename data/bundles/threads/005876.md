I didn't even know there were dark purple genotypes from Okaloosa Co, Fl until now!  This was an absolute surprise and I had to double check the surrounding plants to make sure the label was right, and it is!  These are "boring" summer traps that are far less showy compared to the fall show, it will be interesting to see what the fall traps look like, probably okaloosa-ey!  
  
This is a significant find: this clone is significantly genetically different from all the other dark clones "discovered" earlier.  Most of the darkest leucophylla clones come from Covington Co, AL.  I'm pretty excited to breed with this one and see what kind of offspring it can produce!
