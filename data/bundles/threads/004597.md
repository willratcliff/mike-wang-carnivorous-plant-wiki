Here's a somewhat old clone for the photo archives.  S. flava var. ornata 1AT Okaloosa Co, FL is probably in the collection of a few growers, but it's not widely in circulation.  It's essentially a "watered down" flava atropurpurea like all ornatas from this area, but as you can tell, watered down doesn't mean less impressive!  There's heavy venation on the lid, which is a dominant feature of this clone.  The veins on the body are also well defined under optimal conditions.  This clone almost reminds me of some of the ornata individuals from Walton Co, FL  and Bay Co, FL, but the biggest difference is that this clone can produce really giant traps once it gets big.  It's also quite vigorous!
