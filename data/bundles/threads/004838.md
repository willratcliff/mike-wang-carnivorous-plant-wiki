Here's an interesting moorei from Eastern that pushes out decent looking traps during the spring, but the most spectacular ones are produced during the fall!  It's really interesting how a lot of mooreis either perform best during the spring or the fall, but not both.  There are definitely a few clones that put on a show no matter the grow season and this is one of them.  
  
This is a primary hybrid resulting from crossing S. flava var. rugelii with S. leucophylla, both of which originated from the same population.    
  
This plant doesn't look as great as it did in the past, but this was the first time it was "noticed" in a while, so I figured it's time to get it photo-documented.  I'll keep this thread updated, hopefully there'll be some interesting fall traps this year.    Pics taken 5/11/20:
