I recently acquired this plant from my friend Rob of the the pitcher plant project, and what a beauty!  There were several seedlings from this seed batch, but this individual is one of the best.  It's fast growing, and produces both spring and fall pitchers.  Some of the most amazing red leucos I've ever seen come from this Franklin Co population.  The red color can become more intense, especially in the late fall, when the night temperatures become a lot cooler.  
  
My guess is these genetics (clone A and clone B) originally came from Sunbelle Exotics, and what you're seeing is the next generation.  Maybe Rob can confirm?  The original clone A and clone B are spectacular as well.

Land clearing or open burning can only be done by those who are able to control it and have gone through the proper channels. There are state as well as local laws for burning and the land owners must use a certified burner and have permission from the local fire departments.
