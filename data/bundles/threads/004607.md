Why does MW choose names for his plants that confuses himself?     Cuz sometimes he don't think things through!  I get this thing confused all the time with thick neck, but these are 2 very different clones.  
  
What's so special about S. leucophylla var. alba 'wide neck' and why was it selected?  Uhh, I dunno, it just looks pretty?  Traps have produced solid white interiors from this clone in the past, but at this point, I'm not sure that's the greatest feature of this plant.  It looks impressive as a "regular" leucophylla, and there's a decent amount of "stretch" going on with the white pigments.    These are late summer traps from recently divided divisions, and it's surprising to see how well the Washington Co leucos perform at this time of year.  Normally, you have to wait until the fall to see anything close to nice (at least in my Northern California climate), but probably due to the distant alata influence in this population, these leucos produce big late summer traps as well.  I'm really looking forward to seeing if this is it, or if there will be yet another flush of even more impressive fall traps.  We'll see.
