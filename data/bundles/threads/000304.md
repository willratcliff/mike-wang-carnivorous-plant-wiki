Been a while since I've updated this thread!  Pictured below is a very intriguing red throated variant with a nice contrasting green body!   The population this originated from is likely extinct in the wild.  Very few sites in Mobile Co, AL still exist.   I visited many sites in this county in 2014, 98% of which no longer contained plants that were seen a little more than a decade earlier (early 2000's).  This Mobile Co rosea population has some of the best genetic diversity that I've seen!
