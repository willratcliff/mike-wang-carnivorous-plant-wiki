For sure this clone is darker than the average clone when grown under identical conditions, but it's hard to tell from these pics because in person it's just dark red, it hasn't reached its fullest color potential yet.  Vigorous and relatively easy to grow compared to other cephalotus clones, although I haven't had it for very long so I'm still learning about it.   In many ways, this clone looks like its related to purple black or maybe from the same original location?  I don't know, just a shot in the dark guess.  I really wish the original sources of Cephalotus would give more general details about their origins, but most of them are just mystery plants and it'll probably stay that way for a long time.  Fortunately, in the Sarracenia world, we got our act together and so as history goes on beyond our time, growers will have the detailed history of many clones so that they can have a better understanding of the plants and breed/preserve them accordingly.
