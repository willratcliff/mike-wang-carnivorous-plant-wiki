I've been trying to propagate this plant for years, but every time I get divisions, the second someone finds out, they're gone, so I'm back to square one!    
  
Without a doubt, this plant is worthy of being registered as a cultivar!  It has that adrian slack look, but on a judith hindled sized plant.  It produces lots of traps throughout the grow season, and is easy to cultivate.  Certainly one of a kind, the offspring were all over the place in this cross, and no two looked exactly alike.  Maybe this is a plant worthy of naming after my newborn son Benny?  I don't know, still not sure...I have an uphill battle since it's going to be hard to create something as eye popping as Ellie Wang.  Can't play favorites.
