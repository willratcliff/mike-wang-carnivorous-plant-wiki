I've done a pretty poor job showing pictures of the quality of these crosses, so here's an attempt to get some photos out there!  Why has Mike WAng dropped the ball?  1) I haven't given these proper space to grow  2) that's what happens when you have too many plants.  Don't worry, you will get there if you continue at the pace you're going, LOL  
  
So what do you get when you cross two dark rubricorporas together?  The goal of this cross was to either get darker plants than either parent (which I didn't) or get some atropurpuras, which I did!  That said, these resulting offspring are very high quality rubricorporas: they color up easier than many of the unselected wild types.    
  
If any of you have done rubricorpora breeding (especially from Liberty Co, FL), you'll know you pretty much never get 100% rubricorporas: they're a mix of all sorts of other genes in that population.  What you see here is AFTER all the off types were culled and only the red tubed plants were kept!  If you got this cross in your collection, it came from my mother plants in the picture!
