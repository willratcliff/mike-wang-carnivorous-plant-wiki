S. montana Clone AA is genetically different from the transylvania Co, NC individuals in the collection, although I'm not sure what county it's originally from.  In any case, it produces very large pitchers, and looks more "purp-purp" to me than "venosa" like the transylvania Co plants.  Does the lid fold over on this clone?  Sometimes.  When?  Whenever it feels like, so it seems!  
  
The pitchers can get big with this variant, much like the larger S. purpurea ssp. purpureas.

These plants typically grow pretty fast, especially the outcrossed F-1 seedlings.    
  
However, when the plants flower, it sometimes zaps a huge amount of energy from them.   This is true for all the other purps-I have S. purpurea ssp. purpurea and S. purpurpurea venosa that flowered, but haven't produced a pitcher yet this year!
