Here's a beautiful clone created by Karen Oudean from Willow Creek Nursery up in Washington State.  One of the key features of this clone is the extremely dark purple-black color of the throat that contrasts with intricate background patterns and colors.   The cultivar name couldn't be more appropriate!  
  
For your convenience, here's the cultivar description: []()[www.carnivorousplants.org/cpn/Species/v38n2p56_58.html](http://www.carnivorousplants.org/cpn/Species/v38n2p56_58.html)   
  
Nice thing about this plant is that even under outdoor conditions, it's starting to color up.  As Kiwiearl once said, no need for those "fake suntans" from greenhouses to get the best colors from this plant.  The colors are still developing on these plants outdoors, but they're starting to look nice.   As the pitchers age, I expect they will become even more colorful.
