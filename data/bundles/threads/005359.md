Here's a very interesting population of flava var. flavas that I suspect has rubra introgressed in them a few generations ago!  Kiwiearl has plants from this population and hasn't seen similar traits, so this may be a localized thing rather than an entire county wide occurrence.    
  
  
As a side note, the maximas in the population I have look very similar to the flava var. flavas shape-wise!  Unlike a lot of maxima clones that naturally occur in the Carolinas, the Cartaret Co, NC ones I have are one  of the few maximas to NEVER produce red veins in the throat or body, even when under environmental stress.  
  
  
Anyways, this population has been in the collectin for a very long time and surprisingly there doesn't seem to be many pics of them online, so here they are!  Photos taken 10/26/21:
