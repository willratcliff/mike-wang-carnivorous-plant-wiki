I had no clue that individuals like this existed in Bay Co, FL but it's only logical that they do since there are red forms all over the place in surrounding counties.  That said, I've never seen a red clone with a red interior, lots of red coloration below the mouth,  and shape just like this one, it's for sure quite unique!  This has been in the collection for years and was "hiding" until literally today when I stumbled upon it (well, briskly walked by and noticed it).  This will for sure be selected out, propagated and put into circulation in the future!
