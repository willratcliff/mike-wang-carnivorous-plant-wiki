FINALLY got a chance to photograph this dang thing!  Here's S. flava var. atropurpurea 1AT 'red lid dominant' Okaloosa Co, FL.  So, you're probably wondering, why is it called red lid dominant when the whole dang thing is red anyways?  The answer is, these atropurpureas from Okaloosa Co, FL tend to color up as the traps age and are oftentimes greenish-red when they first open up.  Out of many different clones, this one, almost seemingly no matter what, will still open up with a red lid, even in the off years when the traps don't color up all the way, hence the name.    
  
S. flava var. atropurpurea 'red lid dominant' Okaloosa Co, FL, pics taken 5/26/22.  I lost count over the years how many people have asked to see pics of this clone and I haven't been able to deliver until now!
