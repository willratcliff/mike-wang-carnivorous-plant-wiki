This is one of those really white clones that I suspect is tough to entice to turn solid white.  When I first saw the fall traps open, even though they weren't solid white, it has the tell-tale signs of being capable of producing very bright white traps.  I've seen some HCW clones that produce red traps, so just because your plant isn't solid white on the first try, doesn't mean it can't change.
