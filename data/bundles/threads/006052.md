Cephalotus follicularis Denbarker 1. This clone originated from the late Phil Mann, seeds were sent over to the US and grown out, this was one of the few that was selected out and survived the test of time. There are 3 clones that are in cultivation from this same seed batch, Denbarker 1, Denbarker 2, and Denbarker 3.  
A few features of this clone:  
all else equal, it readily produces dark black lids under ideal conditions. All clones can probably do this if the conditions are right, but this clone in particular colors up relatively easily.  
the plant can attain exceptional sizes relatively quickly once it has good growth momentum and a good sized tuber to start with. Many clones can do this, but not all of them have vigor like this clone.  
I was told it may in fact be a giant.. I haven't had it for long enough to find out, maybe next year my mother plant will be big enough to reach it's fullest vegetative potential. What you see below are two growth spurts from this season, my hope is that the tuber sizes up so I can hopefully get some bigger traps next season.  
There are two main phenotypes with this clone. When it produces normal sized traps, they appear slightly longer than typical clones. Once they size up significantly (not shown in the pics), the length of the traps appear over exaggerated, reminds me of the shape of a shoe! I only know this because when the plants were received, they came with traps that were like that!  
In the photos below,  there are two different divisions (same clone, Denbarker 1) shown.
