So the name is a bit misleading, but I got tired of naming these things "super sonic Neon pink" so this time around, it's called something a bit different.  To be clear, the plant isn't very old, maybe a decade now, but this used to be my absolute best pink clone in the collection.  This clone became old news once S. leucophylla yellow flower 'super pink' was created (super pink pictured below):  

![](../../images/8b/8b1bf0109639eeb3bf8bcac9cd1010db7c25c512ccb45a1163438f0e59ac5527.jpg)

  
  
  
Nevertheless, if you've never seen super pink, you'd think old pink is the best thing since sliced bread...when it's fully mature and at vegetative maturity.  Trouble is, the demand for pink leucophyllas is so severe, I ended giving up my mother plant and keeping a tiny side shoot.  That side shoot stayed small for maybe 3 years and now it's finally producing traps worth photographing.  
  
Color-wise, it's Dana's Delight pink when it has just started to color up and has an outstanding leucophylla shape.  As the trap ages, the pink becomes a bit more intense and turns almost dark pink (but not quite) when fully colored up.   when the fall traps just open up, under my conditions, they're just barely pink.  
  
This clone isn't widely in circulation, and if it has made it to other collections besides the one collection that my mother plant was sent to, I'm not aware of it.  It's in my cross hairs for propagation, and I've been babying it for Corey aka Bog Jesus.   Here's S. leucophylla 'Old Pink' Baldwin Co, AL.   Photos taken 10/23/18:
