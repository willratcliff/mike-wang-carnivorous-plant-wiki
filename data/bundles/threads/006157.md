Here's a really standout clone that has some nice coloration going on!  When the traps first open up, the coloration below the white is reddish, but as it ages, it turns to dark purple!  The top contrasts very well with its bright white coloration.  Even though this isn't an alba, the white coloration is pretty darn white: the pics below don't quite showcase this, I'll have to get more photos once the traps age or maybe wait another season to show it.    
  
Growth wise, it's moderately vigorous.  For the most part, the best show is during the fall, especially outdoors.  During the spring and summer, traps outdoors are average looking, although if you live in the deep south or somewhere that has really warm summer temperatures, it's quite possible this clone could perform well there even earlier in the season.
