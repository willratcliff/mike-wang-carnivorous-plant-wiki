Here's a clone, originally from Mike King's collection, of S. flava var. rugelii that has an unusual copper tinge to the lid and parts of the "body" of the trap.  There's a lot of S. x moorei hybrids in the wild in that area, so I suspect it is likely that there are moorei genes that were historically mixed into this plant.
