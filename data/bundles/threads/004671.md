How can such a looker be hidden?  well, place this plant among hundreds of other leucophyllas and don't pay much attention to them, that's how!  It hasn't been repotted for maybe 8 years or so, and there's barely any soil left in the pot!      It went through a lot of neglect and the soil is almost completely depleted, so it's not exactly in the best of shape, but you can't tell by the pitchers!    
  
What's so great about this clone?  
1) the tubular, slightly bulbous shape  
2) the white color isn't pure because there are red veins mixed in it, but it's still appears pretty dang white!    
3) slightly red coloration below the white gives great contrast  
4) white color stretches down the back of the pitcher  
5) plant is vigorous with decent pitcher production  
  
It's definitely a "normal" leucophylla, but a standout, hidden beauty!  Working on propagating this plant, hoping to have some available around fall 2020 or so.    Pics taken 10/3/19:
