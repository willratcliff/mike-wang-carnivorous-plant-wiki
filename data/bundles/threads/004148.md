Here's a very interesting S. flava var. cuprea from Brunswick Co, NC, and check out that little spur thingi-majigger that's on the tip of the lid!    This plant has been in the collection for many years now, and I have it in an overgrown, neglected community pot, so I'll be separating and planting it into it's own pot so it can be propagated.    The shape of the lid, nice contrasting, lightly veined yet green colored body, deep coppery veins in the lid, and the fact that the coppery lid color stays vibrant even as the traps age  are the key features of this particular individual.  Since it's been ignored all these years, I'll pay closer attention to it to see what other characteristics, if any, are note-worthy.
