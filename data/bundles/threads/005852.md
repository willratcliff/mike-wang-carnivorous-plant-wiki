Here's an incredible hybrid that I've been chasing for years and finally.....FINALLY got a division of!  It was absolutely challenging getting this division: kept being told that someone else got it before me.  Seeing many others acquire this clone years ago didn't make me jealous.....okay, let's rephrase that in a more accurate way: I was definitely jealous, but it made me think that this clone is obtainable at some point, and that eventually became a reality!   
  
This somewhat newly registered cultivar has been in cultivation for quite some time, but as many of you know, it takes a long time to to get these plants into other collections.  Nothing else is quite like it, and when this plant reaches vegetative maturity, I'll hopefully have much more impressive photos to share.  Here's a link to the official photo for this cultivar: [legacy.carnivorousplants.org/cpn/Species/images/v51n2p115f7.jpg](https://legacy.carnivorousplants.org/cpn/Species/images/v51n2p115f7.jpg)    and here's a link to the official cultivar description: [cpn.carnivorousplants.org/articles/CPNv51n2p109_123.pdf](https://cpn.carnivorousplants.org/articles/CPNv51n2p109_123.pdf)   
  
The lid on this thing is absolutely enormous compared to the rest of the body of the trap!  It has some reptilian rose "kinks" and looks to it as the traps mature.  This is literally the very first trap that opened on a recent division, and homies ain't complainin, it's beautiful!  Pics taken 6/6/23:
