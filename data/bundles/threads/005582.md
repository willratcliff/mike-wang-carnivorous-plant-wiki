Here are some new clones of S. alata var. nigropurpurea Stone Co, MS, also known as S. alata 'black,' or at least that's the street name....like if you were in a dark alley and then some homeless looking half asian  guy in sweat pants rolls up to you with some alatas and tries to offer them to you, he would hypothetically call it alata black rather than alata nigropurpurea.  Just for the sake of transparency, that's all speculation, ladies and gentlemen.  
  
right now, at this time of year, most of my red, black, and copper top alatas are completely indistinguishable from the regular form, except some, like these, have almost pure black interiors!  I don't know of any regular forms that produce black interiors like these!
