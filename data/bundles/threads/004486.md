This was a really big suprise: a new yellow flowered S. leucophylla!  To recap, leucophylla flowers are normally red, and the chances of finding a yellow flowered individuals is very low, as they are extremely rare.  In cultivation, here's the clones I can think of off the top of my head that have yellow flowers:  
1) S. leucophylla anthocyanin free  
2) S. leucophylla 'Schnell's ghost'  
3) S. leucophylla yellow flower 'super pink' Baldwin Co, AL  
4) S. leucophylla yellow flower Washington Co, AL (there's maybe 4-5 clones from this population that are yellow flowered)  
  
yeah, maybe a dozen  yellow flowered leucophyllas exist in cultivation, they're pretty rare!  Aside from the anthocyanin free version, all the other yellow flowered cultivars are not anthocyanin free, meaning they produce red pigments.  
  
  
I'm not aware of any other leucophylla from Okaloosa Co, FL with a yellow flower, so this is probably a first for this locality!  Photos taken 4/20/19:
