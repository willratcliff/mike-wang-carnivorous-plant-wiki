some of you growers may be wondering, since when did they name a county after Brooks Garcia?!  Okay, Okay, buda boom Ch.....none of you guys were thinking that, it's just Mike Wang's crazy thoughts.  
  
Anyways, here's a very special flava var. rugelii variant that comes from a very large private parcel in Brooks Co, GA.  It is reportedly from the largest extant stand of flava rugelii's in GA! Sadly, while the fields are big compared to what's left (there are multiple different bogs on the property, all somewhat close to each other), historically, there used to be a bountiful number of bogs that span as far as the eye can see.    
  
Anyways, what's so special about these rugelii's?  They just somewhat recently entered cultivation a few years ago and these genotypes tend to have a very well defined red throat!  For those of you who have grown rugelii from seed, you'll know a tiny seedling that has a well defined red throat is a winner winner chicken lunch....errr, DINNER!
