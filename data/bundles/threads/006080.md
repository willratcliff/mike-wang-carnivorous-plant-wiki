Here's another clone that originates from the late and great Phil Mann and is extremely rare in cultivation.  This clone is quite possibly a giant from what I was told, and it seems plausible, but I won't label it as such until it has proven to produce traps at least 6cm in length, excluding the lid!   
  
For now, it's a pretty incredible, colorful clone that can produce dark blackish lids under ideal conditions.  The shape of this clone is very similar to Denbarker 1 and they're from  the exact same population, although this is a different individual.  I think denbarker 1, from our limited experience, is a bit more vigorous, but only time will tell because it takes a few years to really see all the characteristics of each one.  
  
Much like Denbarker 1, when C. follicularis Denbarker 2 clone produces traps on the larger size, the shape reminds me of a slipper or shoe!  It's slightly longer than many of the other ones in cultivation.
