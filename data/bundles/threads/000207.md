Some new photos of S. x Elaine, named after my fiance.  It will officially be published in the near future once most characteristics of this plant are observed.  
  
So far, it behaves like an S. flava early in the grow season by putting on a nice flush of traps.  However, it behaves like an S. leucophylla in late summer and fall.  After the spring pitchers, it produces a huge amount of phyllodia, and then has its fall pitcher production.  I bet once this plant is big enough, it'll make some nice specimens.

`post #33` · `id=3484` · `2013-08-25 17:00:58 UTC`

Interesting idea KE!  This hasn't yet been grown under greenhouse conditions,but I would expect the lid to turn whiter, and perhaps the body could turn a darker red.    
  
As for using outdoor conditions as metrics for cultivar descriptions, it seems the results can be equally variable and anomalous.  For example, If you look at S. x Adrian Slack when it was growing outdoors at Bob Hanrahan's property in Alabama, the colors were way more intense than anything I've ever seen elsewhere, outdoors or indoors.  Also, plants in nature produce some of the best colors you can find.      
  
I scratch my head on your question because under your and my outdoor, mediterranian  climate, the colors can become intense, but it's not as intense as under a greenhouse at the same latitude.  However, go to the SE US, and outdoor colors can be good as what you get in a greenhouse, if not better.

#### **Mike Wang** (`@meizzwang`)
`post #97` · `id=47622` · `2021-12-22 07:17:47 UTC`

Sure thing, I'll try to get a pic of them side by side next growing season!  
  
The main reason I never released Elaine Wang is because of it's growth habit: it produces nice spring and fall traps, but during the summer, it's very leucophylla-ey and produces only phyllodia.  Sometimes, it's hard to coax the clone to produce nice fall traps.  Ellie wang is far superior performance-wise, it just keeps producing traps all grow season long and is far easier to grow in general (eg. not as rot prone compared to Elaine Wang).   The color of the body of Ellie Wang is arguably a bit more refined...the rounder/more symmetrical shape of the mouth is also subjectively more desirable.
