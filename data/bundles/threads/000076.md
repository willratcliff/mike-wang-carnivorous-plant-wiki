Both those photos are from Stone County, MS in the DeSoto National Forest. These populations see regular burns from the Parks Service. The flowering photo was taken at sunrise. I was with Jim Miller who was shooting video of the last large native stands of these plants. We went all across the panhandle in 4 days shooting film. What a great adventure.
