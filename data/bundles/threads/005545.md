Maaaaaaan!  It's really hard to find good quality ornatas in these pheno hunts that I've been doing, but I think I found at least a few winners, this clone being one of them!  Even the baby divisions from this clone look incredible!    
  
This clone has outstanding venation on the top of the lid, which is actually quite common in the batches of plants that I've been searching through, but what's hard to find are individuals with heavy venation on the body.  Well, this clone has both and it has a really cool shaped trap with a nice dipped lip!    
  
  
I'm sure this plant will look even more interesting as it matures, but for now, here are some photos of this new clone!  S. flava var. ornata 1AT clone#1 Okaloosa Co, FL.  Pics taken  6/5/22:
