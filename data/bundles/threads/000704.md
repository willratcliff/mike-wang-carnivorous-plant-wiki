#### *alexis*
`post #13` · `id=46063` · `2021-04-09 18:01:14 UTC`

Cutting off decent old pitchers generally results in shorter growth. Leucophyllas prefer light to the rhizome in summer, but there's no advantage in getting light to the growth points in spring in my experience.
