I guess it would be appropriate to call this S. moorei Escambia Co, FL, but hey, the tag don't say that (maybe it sounds a little less grammatically incorrect to say "them tags ain't sayin that"), so I'll leave the original name.    
  
Got this clone from a homie Bristol a few years back, and while it has raised a few eye brows for producing some pretty nice sized traps in previous grow seasons (while being under full neglect), it hasn't really colored up until now!  What's interesting is that it colored up right after being divided!  
  
I know a few of you out there have this clone, so if you know the history of it or want to share pics, feel free to contribute to this thread!
