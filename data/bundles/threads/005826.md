couple of years ago, this plant flowered, but for a very long time, I had no idea which plant it was, since at the time the plant was growing with a ton of others and wasn't labeled.  Well, finally.  FINALLY!  It flowered again!  The reason it's so hard to tell from the others is because without a flower, the traps are indistiguishable from the regular pink colored flower.  
  
Where did this clone come from?  Hundreds upon hundreds of individuals, here's a thread on the population and pics of the white flowered variant from a few years back: [sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/1488/massive-colony-rosea-liberty-fl?page=1](https://sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/1488/massive-colony-rosea-liberty-fl?page=1)  
  
There are many different shades of pink all the way to red coloration in the petals of S. rosea, but finding white ones is super rare in both nature and in cultivation.  I'm not sure I've seen anyone else with a white flowered rosea in cultivation-if you have pics or have a clone, we would all love to see it!
