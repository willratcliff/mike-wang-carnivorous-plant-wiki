Yup, you guessed it!  MW is running out of ideas on what to call these select divisions!    In case you were wondering what 4520 means, they are random numbers given to a particular population so that these genetics can be distinguished from other populations from the same County.  In other words, every clone that comes from this particular population has a 4520 in it's name somewhere.  
  
I selected out this clone maybe 10 years ago from a large seed batch, grew a few divisions, and then everything rotted out except a tiny side shoot.  Since it didn't have a name at the time and it was the only side shoot left, I called it "only clone" which also made sure my only division wasn't distributed before copies were made!  Well, it finally produced a nice trap years later!   
  
S. leucophylla 4520 'only clone' Washington Co, AL, pics taken 9/18/22.  In retrospect, this clone has a very typical look for the population as a whole, which is pretty dang nice!  However, in more recent years, I've done massive grow outs from Washington Co and have found clones that appear to be much more impressive, so I'll have to post pics of those once they mature:
