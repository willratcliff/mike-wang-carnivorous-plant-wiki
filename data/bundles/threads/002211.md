A little history: this plant was started from seeds on 5/23/1997, and I originally acquired 2 seed batches from the ICPS meeting in May of 1997: one from Ben Hill Co, GA and another from Liberty Co, FL.  Clone A came from the Liberty Co, FL batch.  
  
Some clones of flava look completely different from one year to the next, and S. flava var. rugelii clone A is no exception.  From one year to the next, the traps overall looks similar, but slightly different.   So what's so special about this clone, and why is it that everyone who grows Sarracenia constantly bombards me with requests for this exact plant?  It's hard to find a more horticulturally friendly individual that grows fast and produces beautiful, large pitchers consistently year after year.  It's also an exceptional plant to use for breeding, and I hope to see some incredible hybrids from you guys in the near future.
