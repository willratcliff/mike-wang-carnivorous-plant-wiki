I mean, judging by the photos, you'd think this clone is maybe solid red or deep red, but at the time the clone was named, the traps were DARK RED!  Phenotypic variance (difference in physical appearance) within a single individual can vary year from year.  This exact division was divided the winter of 20-24-2025, and Sarracenia are well known to need a year or two after dividing to reach their fullest color potential.  In any case, these traps lasted a long time: most other alatas are brown to the ground by early January but these were still standing at the end of the month!  This is a great trait to select for if you're going for a hybrid that has long lasting traps!
