Chances are, if you're a Sarracenia G-NURD™ and have been collecting for a while, you'll recognize the name of this clone, as it has been used extensively in breeding!  Despite having this clone in the collection for over 20 years, I haven't personally gotten around to photo-documenting it until now!   
  
One thing that takes time and experience to realize is that naming a clone "best clone" doesn't always withstand the test of time.  There are arguably much better clones in cultivation now (and even back when I named the clone!!!), but to make sure there's no confusion, unlike in the medical field where the same medicine has 5 different synonyms, I try to keep things simple and stick with the original names that the clones are given.
