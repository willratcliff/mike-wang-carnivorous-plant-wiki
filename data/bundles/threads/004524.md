What’s a Carolina shape?

  
Put plainly as I can think of, the S. flava that come from the Carolinas are very different looking in structure from those in the Florida region. In my eyes at least they kind of look "cleaner," more stately and with less wavy floppy lids or broad pouting mouths. More upright, smoother rounded lids, etc.  
It's easier to see when comparing a FL plant against a Carolina plant.
