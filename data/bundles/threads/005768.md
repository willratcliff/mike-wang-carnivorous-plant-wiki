Here's a clone that I've had in the collection for a very long time now and it hasn't been fully photodocumented until now!  It originated from private property in Southern Baldwin Co, AL, where it is native to.  This clone stuck out from the rest because of it's ability to produce side shoots like no other plant from the same population! As a result, over time, these plants can form enormous "tufts" although I haven't really let them get super gigantic.
