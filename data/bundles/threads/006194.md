There are many species in the genus Sabatia and I have run into them many times while bogging in the south.  They can be grown just like a Sarracenia!  I've always seen them but never really paid much attention to them until now.  This is an excellent carnivorous plant companion, there are some that are quite weedy (eg. set seeds and spread) while others, as long as you only have one clone, will only colonize vegetatively.  I've been growing S. dodecandra for a long time now and still haven't quite figured out why it does well and why it doesn't do well, but I'll leave that for another post.
