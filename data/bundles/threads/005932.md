So, this one is technically an okee giant because it can size up, but under my conditions, it's just a large, red minor.  This clone forms beautiful, compact clumps over time and colors up the best during the fall/winter.  Certainly, it could color up earlier in the season if you grow them under LED's or in a greenhouse OR if you live in the deep south where they naturally occur, but in my climate, these usually only color up in the fall.

The traps absolutely need to be "hit up" with full sun in order to color up!  Notice how much green is on this trap below, that's because this side of the trap wasn't exposed to full sun and was also surrounded by neighboring pitchers!  Had it been exposed to full sun, that portion of the trap would also turn red!
