I don't whether to call this an ornata or rubricorpora!  This clone originates from the "outlaw" site in Bay Co, FL, which many of you know is some of this disjunct population that is a bit of a distance from the "main" extant populations of flavas.  County lines are man made and they have little to do with diversity: these are drastically different from the rubricorporas and other flava types in Bay Co, FL!
