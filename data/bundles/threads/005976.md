Figured it's about time to create a thread for this clone!  AFter growing hundreds of rubricorpora clones from Liberty Co, FL, only 4 or 5 total turned out to be dark ones!  This clone in particular is one of the best due to its shape.  They all color up relatively easy and can turn a purple-blackish color as the traps age.   One tell tale sign that you have a dark clone is the ease by which it colors up: even in "bad" years, these are incredible deep to dark red plants, but in great years, they're super dark!
