Yet another gem from Bud Wilkerson's bog, S. leucophylla 'wide lid' may be somewhat typical for this particular bog, but it's certainly unique for a leucophylla.  This clone does seem to have some red genes showing up (slightly), so it'll be interesting to see how that pans out when we end up breeding with it.  Will it get redder as the temps. cool down this fall/early winter?  Only time will tell.  It also has a very wide looking lid, especially while the lids are unfolding...this is something you don't see very often in leucophylla.    This is the first year after many moons that it's produced some nice, large traps, and it'll probably bloom for the first time next year.
