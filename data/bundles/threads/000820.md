The Old Dock  population of S. minors has a bunch of diversity, and clone A is the most "standard" selection from this group. These traps are pretty short, and don't get much taller than a foot (1/3 meter).  They form nice compact clumps over time.  Some clones are red, others have red windows, and there's a lot of differences in shape.  Clone A has symmetrical, fat traps with a great set of "regular"  windows.  The head or lid has a deep copper color.
