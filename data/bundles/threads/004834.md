The Berkeley Co, SC flava genetics are incredible!  Multiple individuals from this population produce enormous traps, and they're incredibly vigorous!  I think there are a lot of giants in this population, but they have been neglected for years, so they haven't yet been grown to their fullest potential.    
  
If you've seen roots on Carolina flavas, they're much different than the root systems of rugelii's from florida and alabama:they're like thick ROPE in comparison!  I think these Carolina genetics are better adapted to wetter conditions, but that still needs to be tested and proven.  
  
In the meantime, check out this stand-out S. flava var. maxima clone#1 Berkeley Co, SC!  I couldn't believe it, but one of the old traps turn GOLDIE yellow!!!!  WOW!  On top of that, this plant is incredibly vigorous, pushes out a ton of pitchers during the spring/summer, and can get very tall/gigantic!  The shape is also highly desirable.
