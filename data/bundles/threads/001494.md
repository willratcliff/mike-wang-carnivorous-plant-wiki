Naturenuts, honestly, I'm not exactly sure, but  here's how I plan on getting this built:  
1) save $100 every month for about 2 years (done)  
2) call in a contractor and brainstorm how to build these beds.  
3) Have a professional install them.  
  
Essentially, I want to build long regular rows of beds, and have each bed partitioned or segregated so if there's a disease outbreak in one part, it doesn't go to the rest.  Each partition will be no more than 6" deep, and will have something built in so they can be drained (ie. one of the four sides can be removed).  This way, during the winter season when it rains like crazy, my plants won't be flooded with water.  
  
No idea how this will be done, but experienced contractors will likely be able to fill in the details.  It ain't worth doing a job unless you do it right!
