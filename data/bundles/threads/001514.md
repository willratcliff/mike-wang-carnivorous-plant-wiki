Here's a really neat-O clone named by Brooks Garcia and found on the Wilkerson's property in Northern Walton Co, FL.  Based on the appearance, this seems to be a moorei, or complex moorei hybrid, and this plant came directly from the wild.  The most outstanding feature of this plant is the throat-when the plant matures, the red throat can be as intense as S. flava 'Killer' (scroll down this post to get an idea: [sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/229/flava-killer-new-pics-added](http://sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/229/flava-killer-new-pics-added) )  This really makes me wonder: how did these "extreme red throats" originate in the wild?   
  
Photos below are of S. x Bud Wilkerson, taken 7/26/14.  I just acquired this plant and it was small when I got it, but hopefully there will be some significant pitchers to look at next year.  These traps are still pretty small and nothing near what this plant will look like at full maturity.  Of course, this will be an ongoing thread:
