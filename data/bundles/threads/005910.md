turns out, I haven't photodocumented this clone, so here it is!  When it was first "discovered" in the collection, the plant was shaded by many neighboring plants and it produced these huge-mungus traps that were mostly green, hence the name of the clone.  
  
Years later, it colored up in full sun but still produces those beautiful and large traps, so...meh, I'll still keep the name just to not create confusion.  If you want this thing to live up to its name, grow it in part shade!
