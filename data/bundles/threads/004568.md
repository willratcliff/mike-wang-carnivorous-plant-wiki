Mike that plant is stunning! I do however submit that this and I believe one other red sibling you have posted about are almost certainly the result of OP with cuprea. The shape of the trap gives it away for me, plus the fact that bright red atros are more or less unknown from Liberty Co, but are close to 100% of any good rubri x cuprea seed batch.  It doesn’t sound like you bag your flowers (?) so this occurrence is to be expected - it happened to me in NorCal. There is not enough of a difference in flowering times between the two varieties to form a rock solid pollination barrier, especially in a collection with so many diverse examples of cuprea.
