#### *almightydolla*
`post #2` · `id=48401` · `2022-06-16 23:06:09 UTC`

Duplin County historically had a very small number of populations of just flava and purpurea. It's barely even represented in herbarium, and very disjunct from other flava populations much like the Iredell populations. While there could still be some pockets holding on in the county still, it's likely extirpated. The most recent herbarium I know of was right after my 0th birthday... so back when bellbottoms were first in style.    
  
The good news is these genetics are getting out there. And very soon there is likely to be a major free offering from my breeding population of greenhouse bagged hand pollinated flower seeds and seedlings later this year to help get them in as many collections as I can get them into!
