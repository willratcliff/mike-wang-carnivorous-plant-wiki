Here's a clone that originated from Charles Brewer, it's widely circulating in the US but still one of those clones that are quite challenging to come by.  This was the  very last plant that Charles was willing to sell me before he stopped offering plants, I was grateful to have the opportunity to get one!  That said, it has since grown into a huge plant that will be divided this winter and hopefully I'll be able to offer some late spring/ early summer 2025!  
  
This clone is extremely vigorous once it gains good growth momentum and forms massive clumps over time!  The traps get large but aren't by any means giant:generally speaking, no matter how well you care for the plant, the traps don't ever exceed the maximum "typical" size for Cephalotus.  
  
Growing-wise, some cephalotus clones are more sensitive and challenging to grow than others.  If you're new to the Genus, this is one of the clones you'll want to try out: it's harder to kill than others, although that's relative because IMO, all cephalotus are very easy to kill LOL    
  
While all clones of Cephalotus can turn red if you give them enough light or give them a fake suntan (eg. LED's with optimized wavelengths), this clone was selected out because of it's shapely traps and exceptional coloration.  Traps can also form those "upward," fully open lids under certain environmental conditions.  The patterns under the lid are very well defined and quite striking in appearance.
