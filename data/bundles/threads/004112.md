Just realized I don't have a thread for this variant, so here goes nothing! Just as a heads up, I haven't been able to propagate any of these recently, but I'm hoping to get divisions within a year or so if all goes to plan.   
  
Anyways, here's the result of more than a decade worth of breeding and several generations of selecting only the best of the best for breeding.  The goal here was to make an easier to grow plant that produced incredible red venation with less pigment between the veins so they appear more pronounced.  As far as I know, no naturally occurring ornata has such crystal clear, heavy, red venation!  Photos taken 4/30/18:
