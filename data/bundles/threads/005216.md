What do you get when you cross the most sought after alata clone in the collection:  

![](../../images/99/99e7c27c4f917bd51ec58571cf5311336a3c50a12fbfd1f1e4de5e69f523c3a2.jpg)

  
  
With the most sought after alba clone in the world?  

![](../../images/7c/7cd2b3bf20c5643f9f2887853c07a805193fe6fda15583b73bde91e9bda98484.jpg)

  
  
You get exactly what I was hoping to get: an areolata look with the shape  from the alata, but with a white top!  Interestingly, some of these traps start of reddish (almost for sure a HCW influence), but as they age, they turn LEMON YELLOw!  This is exciting guys!!!    
  
Of course, areolatas aren't very exciting as tiny seedlings, especially when we're looking at spring traps.  The fall traps are where it's at, and when they size up, they color up and shape up even better!  That said, it's exciting to see small spring pitchers looking this way, it means this cross is a winner.  
  
Very few can make this cross in the first place because the lemon yellow clone is impossibly difficult to keep alive and isn't widely in circulation, but fortunately, these seedlings are vigorous and very easy to grow!  
  
This cross ain't no joke ladies and gentlemen, just wait until they get larger and you'll either regret not getting one or I'll regret giving them up!  Pics taken 5/28/21:
