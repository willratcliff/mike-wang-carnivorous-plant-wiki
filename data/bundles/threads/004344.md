I always knew there was an alba lurking in this community tray, but the plants are usually overgrown by neighboring trays or are over-croweded themselves, so they rarely produce fall pitchers.  When stressed and neglected enough, the community tray doesn't grow so thick, which allows one or two plants to at least attempt to shoot out a nice fall trap.
