Now, I don't know what you's craven...Co, NC, but for me, it's flava var. flava and flava var. maxima!    
  
Here's a new population of flava var. flavas and flava var. maximas that almost remind me of flavas from Cartaret Co, NC except these look slightly different shape + colorwise and are growing rather tall considering how small the rhizomes are!  I have a feeling these are going to be super skinny yet tall plants at maturity.  Also, those who are chasing yellow flavas should keep an eye out on this population, as many individuals are turning quite yellow!
