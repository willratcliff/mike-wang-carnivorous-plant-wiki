Here's a complex "hybrid" that has undergone some very strict selective pressure over 2 generations.  Meet the parents: here's Rod's favorite, a super vigorous hybrid and one of the best individuals from crossing hurricane creek white clone F with Mike King's L42 (an alba that has introgressed with alata).  For the record, that cross was the first documented  alba outcross utilizing outstanding clones from 2 different locations.  Anyways, my homie Bristol (on this forum) selected this exact clone (I didn't even know it was that great until he mentioned it) and it has since become very popular.    By itself, this clone is pretty amazing: it's extremely vigorous, produces a lot of traps, has a crazy tall flower stalk, produces tall traps,  and can color up relatively easily:   

![](../../images/d6/d63de4f34e5e0028350dac1c0f70c9c1f3ef061248a77d3e584bd55189013ce7.jpg)

  
  
The pollen donor for this cross was S. leucophylla var. alba 'the one' Baldwin Co, AL-this clone is shy to color up, rots very easily, is difficult to grow in general, and very difficult to coax out that solid white coloration...but  when it does, it's quite spectacular!  I still don't think I've taken this clone to its fullest potential after over a decade of trying to get it to color up, but knowing some good genes are up in this mix, it seemed logical to experiment with:  

![](../../images/34/3414d00e5cfaeded299ac1a10b2195c357d992cb769ef4cf4da53d912ed8d87c.jpg)

  
  
Here's one seedling from a pretty decent sized batch that so far has stuck out of the crowd!  S. leucophylla var. alba (Rod's favorite x the one), pic taken 8/19/21.  For the record, fall traps don't usually show up until around mid to late September here, but genetics that have alata introgression can produce both a late summer flush of traps as well as fall traps!  Still, the best traps have yet to come:  

![](../../images/be/be9af3c669134a49c02050f1545a7b1d58f91a39a5aa5e2131c228367d095a95.jpg)

  
  
If I get any fall traps on this clone, I'll update this thread!  the plant is jam packed in a community tray, so maybe we have to wait until next fall the see it's fullest potential!
