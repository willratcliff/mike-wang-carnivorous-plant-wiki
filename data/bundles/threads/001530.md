For those of you who are not familar with Hurricane creek white, here's a link to the story and their status in the wild: [sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/921/remains-original-hurricane-creek-white](http://sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/921/remains-original-hurricane-creek-white)   
  
Surprisingly, S. leucophylla Hurricane creek white clone D hasn't had a thread of its own until until now!  In a previous leucophylla thread ( [sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/1391/leucophylla-var-alba-baldwin-clone](http://sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/1391/leucophylla-var-alba-baldwin-clone)  ), it was mentioned that some S. leucophyllas grown outdoors in Northern California this year produced what appears to be fall traps about 1-2 months early!  S. leucophylla hurricane creek white clone D was one of them!     
  
These traps are regular sized, but a few years ago, this clone produced MONSTER sized traps!  Photos taken 8/1/14:
