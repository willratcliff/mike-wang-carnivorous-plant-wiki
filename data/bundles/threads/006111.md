I've had these clones for a very long time but never realized one of these was an ornata until now!  I looked at the mother plants and sure enough, there is one with light veining: trasplanting a bunch of divisions into new soil, letting them grow out for a year undisturbed,  and giving them more space so they weren't shading each other is probably the reason the clone didn't vein up until now!
