I suspect this is the result of crossing S. rubra wherryi 'best clone' with a leucophylla from Southern Baldwin Co, AL, but since this is division from a plant grown in the field (from private property, acquired with permission), I don't know for sure "who" the exact parents are.  The owner was known to collect open pollinated seeds from all his plants, grow them out by the gazillons, and then plant them back into the field.  Anyways, for reference, here's rubra wherryi 'best clone': [sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/341/rubra-wherryi-best-clone](https://sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/341/rubra-wherryi-best-clone)   
  
   Technically speaking, this is quasi-man made because rubra wherryi was introduced into the field where this plant came from.  The only truly native species there were roseas and leucophyllas.   Nevertheless, for all practical purposes, I'm gonna give this clone  location data because from a breeding standpoint, the more information, the better!
