I haven't yet photo-documented this population yet, but there is one interesting clone in the community tray that produced some great looking traps this year!  There are some even more amazing looking clones from this population, but I figured this one is worthy of having its own thread.  
  
These carolina flavas sure can "yellow up" when the right environmental conditions!  That said, I have no clue what to really call this plant, since it has sort of a reddish throat thing going on as well as some external veining.  I guess it's an ornata for now.
