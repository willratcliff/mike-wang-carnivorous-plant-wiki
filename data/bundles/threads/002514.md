From a previous post, most S. x readii's (rubra x leucophylla) primary hybrids produce either a plant that looks like a mix between the 2, like rubra, or like leucophylla with less white.  In very rare circumstances do you get golden colored plants, but it happens!  
  
I'm not sure which plant is "better"(here's S. 'Golden Luek' to compare: [sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/2407/readii-golden-leuk](http://sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/2407/readii-golden-leuk) ) but for certain, they are both outstanding keepers!  "Golden Luek" takes more of the leucophylla shape, whereas the clone below has more of the rubra wherryi shape.  Makes me wonder what breeders like Phil Faulisi could do with this golden plant!  As time progresses and we learn more about Sarracenia, it becomes clear that hybrids that none of us have imagined are possible!  
  
Here's pictures of another golden S. x readii, photos taken 7/13/15.  Check out the red veins and red edging on the trap, how amazing is that?!
