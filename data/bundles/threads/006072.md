Here's an extremely rare clone of S. rosea that comes from the extirpated site in Cook's Bayou, Bay Co, FL.  As far as I know, plants from this original population only exist in cultivation now.  Sadly, the habitat was destroyed several decades ago.  There must have been way more amazing gems in those fields!
