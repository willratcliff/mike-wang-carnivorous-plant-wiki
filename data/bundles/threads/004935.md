Out of a batch of a few hundred leucophyllas that have been ignored for years, so far, I've found 3 winners, and here's another one that was missed last year!  There are probably more "hidden gems" just waiting to be found!  
  
S. leucophylla 'light red' Baldwin Co, AL has been neglected and not repotted for about 6 years now.  Despite a lot of soil missing/decomposed, the plant has figured a way to survive by having the rhizome grow out of the pot!     Certainly, with better care, a larger pot (it's in a 3.5" pot), and some feeding, it'll produce bigger traps that, just like S. leucophylla 'hidden beauty,' will be jaw dropping. That's now on my list of things to do!  
  
S. leucophylla 'light red' Baldwin Co, AL, pics taken 9/1/20.  Most of the other leucos haven't produced their fall traps yet, but a few, like this clone, have just opened up!
