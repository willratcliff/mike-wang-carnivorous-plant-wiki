Here's a really nice species of Utricularia that has some amazing colors!  What's interesting about Utricularias in general is that the plant is so dang small, but the flowers, compared to the miniscule leaf size, are YUUUUGE!    
  
This particular clone was extremely difficult to get going: at first, I almost lost it due to very low vigor, and it took YEARS to finally get some good growth momentum.  This is the first year that it has produced multiple blooms!  Once you get good growth momentum, the plant is quite vigorous and relatively easy to grow.  The quality of the plant you start with plays a huge role in how well it does!  
  
The plant prefers to be grown in bright indirect light.  I like growing them in 100% peat.   It also like warm soil temperatures: I've noticed that when you keep it on the warmer side, they grow faster!  Like most Utricularias, it really needs to fully colonize the soil it's in before it starts to bloom.  To keep this plant going, after it blooms, you'll want to take a "plug" and start colonizing some new soil: this plant really needs to continue expanding to keep its growth momentum going in the long term.  I stand the plant in about 2" of water and let that fully evaporate before filling it up again.
