Q: Dude....Mike Wang Bruh....I'm not sure I believe that this clone is an okee giant....how do you determine if this is a giant anyways?  
  
A: I don't really know, it's just that the clone can get unusually giant.   Giant is relative to the norm: if you know what a regular sized psittacina looks like and you compare it to this monster, you'll notice the heads on the okee giant (okefenokeensis is the more accurate term) as well as the overall size of the traps far exceed that of the regular form.  
  
This particular clone has been in cultivation for quite some time, but I haven't really distributed it because there was no proof that it could size up and if you want a giant, you probably want to see pics of the plant at its fullest size potential.  Initially, the traps were longer than others in the population, but as the plant reached vegetative maturity, they just seem gigantic!
