Here are some interesting genotypes of S. minor var. minor that originate from a large site (on private property) in Brooks Co, GA.  The site is reportedly housing one of the largest flava var. rugelii population in the entire state of Georgia!    
  
I think these plants are somewhat new to cultivation, and from what I can tell, they're not yet circulating in collections just yet.  I don't really know much about these plants just yet, as they are still babies, but I do know that divisions were made, so they're now available.
