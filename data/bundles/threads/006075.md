Here's a new find that I just stumbled upon this morning while checking out the collection!  Right now, pretty much everything is dead and brown except a few hybrids, some alatas, and some rubras.  It's always sad to see everything dormant during the winter, but it's also interesting to see which ones are still persisting while the rest aren't!  
  
IF you're looking to breed pitchers that have longevity throughout the winter and can tolerate below mild freezes without turning brown, those aforementioned species are what you want to work with!  This alata is also quite exceptional, although the traps have aged quite a bit and are starting to senesce (turn brown).  At least you can get an idea of what they look like!
