This is most likely the result of an F-1 hybrid mixed with what I suspect is a complex hybrid (Adrian Slack).  Why is Bouquet thought to be an F1 hybrid?  Probably because when we were in the field, we saw a leucophylla that had the same exact shape as Bouquet, here's that leuco:  

![](../../images/8d/8d94990555bae0c7ca339910f40da3f6ad54a7b79589b9b8ce072ad746233e56.jpg)

  
  
Also, crosses with bouquet result in segregation, but it's not as bad as super complex hybrids, where the offspring are all over the place!    
  
Here's Bouquet for reference, it seems plausible that a flava crossed with the plant above and made this:  

![](../../images/e0/e0c49e567bb2cf74d3dbf71d1bd6bc12d0a9b00e69b009d7ec1c519f5ff9a6b8.jpg)

  
  
  
Why do I think Adrian Slack is a complex hybrid, which is a hybrid x hybrid or possibly the result of multiple generations of species and hybrids crossing with each other?  Because breeding with it always results in a huge range of phenotypes, and whenever that happens, it's more than likely that one or more of the parents are complex hybrids.  More often than not, I get skunked breeding with Adrian Slack, which is why I've focused on other crosses in more recent times.  many other breeders have reported similar results, but if you make enough crosses with Adrian Slack, luck + probability will be in your favor and  eventually  you'll find a looker, so don't give up if you're interested in making these sort of crosses!  
  
  
Anyways, this "new" hybrid  looks as though it's  for sure looking like a looker!  Excessive use of the word look?  probably.
