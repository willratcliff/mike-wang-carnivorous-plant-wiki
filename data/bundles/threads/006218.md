Here's a real standout clone that not only can get solid red from head to toe (mine are sort of greenish near the bottom, this is because there are neigboring plants shading it), which is unusual for a minor okefenokeensis, but also it has great shape/looks!  In my lifetime, I've grown out thousands of minors and this is easily one of the best red clones there is in existence as far as I know!  Even the spring pitchers color up right away under optimal conditions, no waiting until the fall show, which is when they tend to be their biggest and most colorful!  That said, the spring traps right now are not giant but this clone can size up, especially during the fall.
