So this plant displays characteristics of both flava var. flava and flava var. rugelii (eventually).  I have no idea what to call it, so I came up with this cockamamie name for the meantime.   the red throat isn't yet well defined, but this clone can produce much redder throats when the conditions are right (ie. when the traps are a lot bigger).  
  
Rugeliis from North Carolina are quite rare in cultivation and maybe are not as common in the wild?  If you have any comments on that or know more about it, feel free to share your knowledge!
