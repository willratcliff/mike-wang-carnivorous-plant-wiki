Here are some new flava rugeliis from Jeremiah Harris that I think have great potential!  For one, Jeremiah has a lot of experience and knows what to look for in a plant.  Gangsters with thug like mentalities typically would state in such circumstances that "he ain't playin."  Regarding the name of this batch,  while "JH" is super logical and is clearly Jeremiah's initials, I think the "319" was added on just to make this accession sound cool.     
  
 Despite the rhizomes being very small, the traps are relatively tall(rhyming not intentional), suggesting these could very well be giants as they mature.  Another really interesting trait are the well defined red throats: for those of you who have grown S. flava var. rugelii from seed, you'll know that most rugeliis produce this "lame line" at the center of the neck and not really ever color up until the traps get big enough.  You will also know that only a handful of rugelii clones perform well year after year in cultivation.    The fact that these already have well defined red throats as young plants is quite promising!
