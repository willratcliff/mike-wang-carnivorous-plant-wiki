Here's an old clone that I've had for maybe over 20 years that has withstood the test of time, and I haven't gotten the chance to document it until now!  Sheesh!  
  
  I remember a homie Kinjie visiting me many years ago, long before I was married with kids, and saying that he really thought this clone was the S-izzle (well, he thought it was the (explicative), but since I have to be a good role model my pirate level of swearing has been slightly curtailed these days).
