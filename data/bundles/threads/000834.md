Here's a clone of S. oreophila var. ornata 'Sand Mountain' that was imported from Europe.  I'm guessing this plant can become a lot more colorful, and if somehow I can coax it to produce a fall pitcher or two, I bet they will be stunning.  Also, S. oreophila tends to color up even more intensely when grown under twin wall polycarbonate or under greenhouse conditions. The veins on the spring pitchers are certainly more pronounced compared to other clones that I've grown.
