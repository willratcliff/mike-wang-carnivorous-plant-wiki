There's a lot of pink clones in the collection, maybe close to 40 or so, but this one stood out from the rest!  First off, it has the classic leucophylla shape and doesn't look "hybridy"  from it's parental crossings.    It also has some cilia on the body of the trap, making it kinda hairy and weird 

![:)](//storage.proboards.com/forum/images/smiley/smiley.png) [not mirrored]

  
The white on the top of the trap stays white too, so there's some contrast going on.  You can only imagine how that would pan out when you cross this clone and make hybrids with it!  
  
Vigor-wise, it's about average, not too fast and not too slow.  The pink is ELECTRIC to my eyes and pops out of the crowd because the colors are so intense!  The white pigments in the interior and exterior of the trap mixing in with the pink seems to really bring out the color and make it BLING!  While the actual color is difficult to capture in the photos, some of these are pretty close to what you see in person.  It's actually a little bit pinker!    
  
These traps just opened recently, so as the cold fall nights creep in, they will likely become even more intense pink.  Fortunately, this plant is somewhat division friendly, so you'll likely see these in other people's collections in the near future!  
  
S. leucophylla 'Super Pink' Baldwin Co, AL, photos taken 9/8/17:  

![](../../images/49/49a038be0049cfb6b3a93ff0d5b1d2c7d3619bd3d59e32b105e28d18cba98c72.jpg)
