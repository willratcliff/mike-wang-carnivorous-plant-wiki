One of the most unique atropurpurea clones in cultivation: this clone, which can get solid red from head to toe, can produce a BLACK throat!!    
  
  
I've been growing this clone for years, and for the most part, it looks like a flava killer for me year after year: the traps didn't color up very well, but it's still impressive even when it doesn't color up!    Maybe it has been transplanted too frequently, who knows.    
  
In other collections (ie. when grown by Dirtydivisions, formerly AKA naturenuts), growers have been able to get this clone to color up seemingly quite easily, so never judge how good a clone can be just because it doesn't look that great under MW's care, LOL  
  
I've been able to get it to color up all the way in the past, but the spring traps usually emerge greenish with a very well defined red throat.   Maybe right before the trap bites the dust, it'll turn all red for me at the best.  
  
Well, this year, there's some great success with this clone, it's starting off reddish to begin with!  The throat, as the trap ages, can get BLACK, it's almost unbelievable, and I think it'll color up very well this season, so hopefully there'll be an opportunity to capture this clone at its best color potential.  
  
In the meantime, enjoy these pics!  The new ones with the pitchers just starting to bust open were taken 5/11/20:
