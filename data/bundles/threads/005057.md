This clone actually turns much whiter than what is pictured, and could possibly be an alba.  The original mother plant hadn't been divided for close to a decade, but last winter I split the whole thing up, and it produced some nice traps this season!  
  
Vigorous, relatively easy to grow, and with a nice, bulbous shape!  Let's see what this clone looks like next season, maybe it'll get whiter after being left undisturbed for a while.  Pics taken 10/1/20:
