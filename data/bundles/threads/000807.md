Here's another interesting clone of S. montana that I've labeled clone BB to distinguish it from genetically different clones.  Unfortunately, I have no idea what county and state this clone came from, but it's from the same population as this plant: [sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/274/large-clone-montana-pic-update](http://sarracenia.proboards.com/thread/274/large-clone-montana-pic-update)  In fact, I sometimes have a hard time telling the two apart.  At some point last year, they looked exactly the same.  
  
Nice thing is this clone grows extremely fast, and I bet it can color up in the fall just like clone AA.
