Another plant that I have never seen anywhere: a red bodied leucophylla with contrasting white lips!  Pretty darn neat!  It'll be interesting to see how the fall traps develop on this clone.  
  
It's not exactly a vigorous individual, and I have no idea how to recreate it, this is one out of many hundreds of plants, and there isn't anything else like it....so far!   I wonder what would happen if you crossed an ornata black lips with this white lipped leuco clone?  
  
S. leucophylla RED 'White Lips' Franklin Co, FL...I feel like Stewie and Donnie Schnell should have come up with a latin name for red leucos while they were at it, like S. leucophylla var. rubra, would have made the name of this clone sound more official, haha!
