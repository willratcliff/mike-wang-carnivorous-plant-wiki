# Sarracenia montana F1 Transylvania Co, NC

## Origin

same **Transylvania Co, NC** population. Cross made >10 years before
the 2023 thread; seeds were cold-stored when most of Mike's original
mother plants died during a move, then sown ~2017-2018. The resulting
seedlings are now the inheritor lineage for those parental genetics.

performing without that as a barrier.
