# Sarracenia (purpurea var.) montana 'Clone #1' Transylvania Co, NC

## Origin

Transylvania Co, NC montana selection. Mike's most morphologically
variable montana clone — same plant produces taco-folded, bulbous,
pointed-closed-lid, and red-body-with-green-lid expressions across
different seasons.

## Standout traits

- Extreme upward lid-fold ('taco') expression
- Multi-phenotype: taco / bulbous / pointed-closed / red-body
- Demonstrates Mike's broader montana framework: any montana clone
  can produce the red-body expression under right environmental
  conditions

## Cultivation notes

Outdoor Northern California. For red-body expression in montana,
Mike (2026) recommends filtered light or fewer hours of direct
sun rather than blasting full sun.
