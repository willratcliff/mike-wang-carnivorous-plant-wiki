# Sarracenia rubra 'Ancestral' Taylor Co, GA — group page

A cultivar group covering Mike's two *S. rubra* 'Ancestral'
clones from Taylor County, GA, distinguished by habit. See
member-clone pages below for individual entries.

## Background

*S. rubra* 'Ancestral' is found in Georgia and reportedly South
Carolina, and Mike (post 1, 2012-06-04) cites the prevailing view
that this form is "the ancestor of S. rubra ssp. gulfensis." Mike
has two distinct clones from a Taylor Co, GA collection — they
share the cultivar name but express very differently:

- **Clone A** stays small and short, with a *S. rubra* ssp.
  *rubra*-like clumping habit.
- **Clone B** can grow as tall as *S. rubra* ssp. *gulfensis* and
  forms beautiful clumps.

Both clones photograph with similar dark-red coloration on top of
the trap.
