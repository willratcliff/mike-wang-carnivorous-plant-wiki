# Sarracenia rubra ssp. gulfensis 'THC' (Turkey Hen Creek) Okaloosa Co, FL

A multi-individual gulfensis accession from the Turkey Hen
Creek site (Okaloosa Co, FL). High intra-population diversity:
red, copper-topped, long-lidded, fiery-orange, and classic
green forms. Mike (2019) considers gulfensis under-utilized in
breeding for long-lasting trap traits. ~38 photos: ~35 Mike +
3 mahlon (fiery-orange clone). Plus 4 flic.kr photos by
hcarlton (Clones A and B in NV) not mirrored.
