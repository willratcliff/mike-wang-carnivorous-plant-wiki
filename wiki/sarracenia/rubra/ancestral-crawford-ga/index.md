# Sarracenia rubra 'Ancestral' Crawford Co, GA

A tall, chameleon-like *S. rubra* 'Ancestral' from Crawford
County, GA — a separate locality from Mike's better-known Taylor
Co, GA Ancestral accessions. Phenotype shifts dramatically over
years (jonesii-like → rubra/gulfensis-like → with subtle wherryi
vibes per other growers). Documented from 2015 to 2020 (12 Mike
photos).

## Origin

Wild-origin *S. rubra* 'Ancestral' from Crawford Co, GA. Original

*viatorum*; alexis prompted a re-label discussion in 2020.
