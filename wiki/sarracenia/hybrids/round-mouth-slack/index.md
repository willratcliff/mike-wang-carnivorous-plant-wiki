# Sarracenia 'Round Mouth Slack'

## Origin

One of "up to a hundred" Adrian-Slack-progeny seedlings circulating in
cultivation — Mike's selection from this pool, distinguished by three
traits.

## Standout traits

- **Hybrid vigor in cooler climates** — unlike Adrian Slack, which
  prefers warmer temperatures.
- **Easier color development** than Adrian Slack.
- **Distinctly round mouth** — diagnostic.

Otherwise visually very similar to *S.* 'Adrian Slack'.

## Cultivation notes

Vigor under cool conditions makes this a more tractable Adrian Slack
analog for non-warm climates.
