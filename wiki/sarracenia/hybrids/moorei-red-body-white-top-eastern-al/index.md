# Sarracenia × moorei 'red body, white top' Eastern AL

A Mike-bred F1 moorei from an Eastern AL parent cross — a deep
red body with whitening top that Mike compares to a "red
bodied Adrian Slack" while clarifying it has no direct AS
ancestry. Slow-ish but easier than AS to grow; lid whitens late
in spring like AS; expression degrades if divided too often.
30 Mike photos spanning 2018-2025. Sibling 'extreme red throat'
Select (C0411) is from a similar Mike moorei breeding program.
