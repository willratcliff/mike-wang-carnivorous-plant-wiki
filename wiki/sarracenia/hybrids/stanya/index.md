# Sarracenia 'Stanya' (Premysl Otakar I × Adrian Slack)

## Origin

A select hybrid cultivar bred by **Miroslav Srba** in the Czech
Republic. Parentage: *S.* **'Premysl Otakar I'** × *S.* **'Adrian
Slack'**. Imported to the US by **Jeremiah Harris**, who hooked Mike
up with a division in summer 2022.

## Standout traits

- Stunning color when established multi-season.
- Robust through transplant stress — survived its first season after
  shoots died back.
- Mike rates Miroslav Srba's hybrid breeding highly: many growers
  fail to land winners with Adrian Slack crosses; Srba succeeds.

## Cultivation notes

First season: shoots died back, but rooted enough to overwinter.
Subsequent seasons performed well. Divided early 2025; recent
divisions still hold red color.
