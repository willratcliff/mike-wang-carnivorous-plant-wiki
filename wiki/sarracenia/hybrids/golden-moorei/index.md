# Sarracenia 'Golden Moorei' (S. × moorei)

## Origin

Mike's controlled cross — **very yellow flava × leucophylla var.
alba**. The single most-yellow individual from a community-tray
grow-out. Mike had walked past it for years without thinking it was
notable; recognition came only after his wife (whom Mike describes
as a "super critical" observer) called the clone "beautiful" while
on a brief unrelated visit to the collection. Mike's heuristic:
when his wife says something looks nice, there's a chance others
will too.

## Standout traits

- **Solid yellow head-to-toe** under the right light conditions —
  traps need light reaching the soil line.
- Hybrid genetic background: yellow flava × leuco alba, mixing AF
  tendency and saturated yellow.
- Forum-suggested alternative names: "Lemon Meringue" (hcarlton),
  "Frosted Lemonade" (lushgrows).

## Cultivation notes

The featured photos are from a tight community tray — Mike's note is
that you can still get full coloration in this layout if light
penetrates to the soil line. Standard hybrid Sarracenia care
otherwise.
