# Sarracenia × ('Burgundy' × minor Giant) 'pink clone'

## Origin

A Mike-bred F1 hybrid: leucophylla 'Burgundy' (pod) × minor
okefenokeensis Giant (pollen). The seed batch produced mostly
typical S. × excellens-colored offspring; only ONE seedling
expressed the pink color from the Burgundy mother. This entry
covers that single individual.

Mike (post #47, 2019) candidly admits he was "debating tossing
this clone" more than a decade earlier — it has since become one
of his most-distributed and most-praised hybrids.

## Standout traits

- **Lone pink segregant** from seed batch — rare phenotype.

## Cultivation notes

Drier-leaning culture is safer (rmeyer rot recovery). Pink color
is environment-dependent — peak neon expression seems to require
late-summer / fall conditions with bright sun. Tolerates cold
