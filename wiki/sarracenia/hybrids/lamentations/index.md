# Sarracenia 'Lamentations'

A formally-registered Sarracenia cultivar that has remained
unusually obscure since the 1990s. Mike first saw it at
California Carnivores in 1996 (then 'impossible to acquire')
and finally documented it in 2018. Long-lasting pitchers —
holds color into December when most Sarracenia have gone brown.
Color in cultivation far exceeds the original CPN cultivar
photo. 6 Mike photos (2018-12-11).
