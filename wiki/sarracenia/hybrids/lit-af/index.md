# Sarracenia 'Lit AF'

## Origin

Bred by **Rob Co (Rob Coe) of the Pitcher Plant Project**. Mike's
plant came directly from Rob — adaetz100 (forum) confirmed in the
source thread: "Aha, so that's where Rob's spare piece ended up!"

**Parentage** (per adaetz100 in the source thread): *mitchelliana
AF × luteola.* Both parent lines are AF, which produces the glowing
anthocyanin-free phenotype.

## Naming

The cultivar name carries both meanings: (1) the slang superlative
(per Rob Co's "vulgar" original intent, in Mike's playful framing),
and (2) "AF" descriptive of the genuinely anthocyanin-free phenotype.
Mike's running joke in the thread: "we'll just go with that for now
so that we don't get any angry parents wondering why such a peaceful
plant world is filled with vulgar language."

## Standout traits

- Strong AF (anthocyanin-free) expression.
- Glowing yellow/green coloration.
