# Sarracenia x readii 'Golden Leuk'

Mike-bred leuco × wherryi F1 with rare yellow-golden body + red veins +
exact leuco trap shape. Phonetic-spelling cultivar name (golden + 'leuk'
phonetic for leucophylla). Mike later relabeled as S. areolata Golden
Leuk (2022) reflecting taxonomic shift.
