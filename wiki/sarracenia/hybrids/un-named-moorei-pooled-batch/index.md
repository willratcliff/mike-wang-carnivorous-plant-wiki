# Sarracenia × moorei (un-named clone, pooled-batch survivor)

## Origin

A *S. × moorei* (flava × leucophylla) seedling from one of Mike's
**pooled seed batches** — many different flava × leuco crosses, all
seeds combined and sown together to save grow-out space. The
trade-off: parentage information is lost.

The grow-out result was disappointing. Most seedlings were "mutt
looking" — Mike's terms — not colorful, dull, "a waste of time." A
few were colorful but weakly so. This single clone is the one Mike
kept from the entire batch, flagged for further evaluation.

## Standout traits (interim)

- Pretty white lid.
- Body veining (mattmiller observation).
- Most likely a leucophylla × flava rugelii by trap shape; pooled
  origin makes precise parentage unconfirmable.

## Mike's lesson

Pooling seed batches across multiple crosses is a space-saving
shortcut that risks losing meaningful breeding information. The
project was "almost a waste of time" by Mike's own assessment.
