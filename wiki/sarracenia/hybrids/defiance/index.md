# Sarracenia 'Defiance'

A select clone from a Travis-Wyman-bred / Rob-Co-grown three-way hybrid
seed batch ('Reptilian Rose' × (rubricorpora × leucophylla)). Mike's
accession was originally labeled clone #11 — the eleventh of 11+
seedlings — and was officially registered as S. 'Defiance' by 2022.
Sibling of S. 'Crown of Thorns' from the same cross.

## History

Mike introduced the plant in 2014 (post #1) describing it as one of
those clones "that I think is nameable" — a Reptilian Rose that has
"even better shape and more dynamic colors." Cross was made by Dr.
Travis H. Wyman and grown out by Rob Co of the Pitcher Plant Project,
with several worth-naming siblings appearing in the seed batch.

By 2018 (post #7) Mike upgraded the assessment to "jaw dropping," and
by 2022 (post #9) the plant was officially registered as S. 'Defiance'.
Forum member magknite (post #8, 2021) was first to mention the
'Defiance' name in this thread, ~1 year before Mike confirmed the
registration.

## Standout traits

- Reptilian Rose mouth character with improved shape/color over parent
- "Check out them lipz!" — Mike's 2018 trap (post #7) shows the heavy
  lip distinction that drove the cultivar selection
- Vigorous grower
- Phenotypically labile across growing conditions

## Cultivation notes

- Phenotype is dramatically condition-dependent. Rob Co's greenhouse
  plant produced octagonal/round mouths; Mike's outdoor NorCal plant
  produces oval mouths. Mechanism not confirmed (humidity hypothesis
  proposed but not validated).
- Took ~4 years for the plant to reach full potential under Mike's
  outdoor conditions.
- Deep-purple throat color most reliable in fall flush, less so spring/
  summer (magknite 2021 observation).
