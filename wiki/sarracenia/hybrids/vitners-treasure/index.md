# Sarracenia 'Vitner's Treasure'

Phil Faulisi's CPN-registered moorei masterpiece — sibling to
Saurus, 'Hot Lips', 'Super Heavy Veins', 'Purple Sparks',
'Phil Clone 7'. Beautiful both spring and fall (rare for
moorei). Easy to grow; capable of giant traps; holds traps
into January under mild conditions. 19 Mike photos (2020-2025).
