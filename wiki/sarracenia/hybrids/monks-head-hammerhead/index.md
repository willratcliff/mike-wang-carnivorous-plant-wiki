# Sarracenia 'Monks Head Hammerhead'

A Hummer's Hammerhead-related clone with even more exaggerated
floppy lids and outstanding yellow coloration. Vigorous winter
performer (frost-tolerant to 25F). 41 photos from Mike + adaetz100
(2021-2024). Mother plant lost to rot 2024-09; salvaged from a
side-shoot.
