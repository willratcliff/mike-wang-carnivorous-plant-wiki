# Sarracenia × 'Adrian Slack' (Bob Hanrahan original)

## Origin

Originally found growing in the wild on **Bob Hanrahan's property in
Alabama** and collected from there. Most likely a natural
*S. × moorei* (flava × leucophylla). The cultivar name 'Adrian Slack'
honors the British carnivorous-plant authority Adrian Slack.

Mike's 2011 framing: "one of the most amazing hybrids out there."

## Trade history

In 2011, the clone commanded $160+ on eBay (admin observation, post
#2). Mike's network (paulbarden, post #5): well-respected growers
sell specimens for far less than eBay prices — eBay grossly
inflates desirable-plant prices. The same dynamic applied (and
applies) to adult Cephalotus.
