# Sarracenia (white mitch × HCW F)

## Origin

Mike's hybrid cross. **Maternal:** a white-form Mitchelliana ('white
mitch'). **Paternal:** Mike's 'HCW F' clone (HCW-line, F designation).
Cross likely made years before the 2023 thread.

## Standout traits

- Traps go solid white head-to-toe under optimal conditions.
- Colors up *easier* than the original white Mitchelliana parent.
- **Heterozygous for veinless** — per mahlon (Feb 2025). Suggests
  promising follow-up: pair with a homozygous-veinless partner to
  produce an alba-type Mitchelliana.
- Color persistence even immediately post-division.

## Cultivation notes

Solid-white potential requires undisturbed multi-season cultivation.
2024 mid-season post-division traps still colored well — strong
robustness signal.
