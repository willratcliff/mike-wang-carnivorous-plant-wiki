# Cultivation
cultivation_notes: |
  - **Recently divided plants can still color up.** The
    'Select' clone was divided/transplanted in winter 2022-23
    and still colored up nicely the following spring.
  - **Spring/summer traps are less colorful than fall traps.**
    Expect full red-throat development as the trap ages and
    into fall.
  - **Hybrid vigor.** Vigorous; capable of giant first traps in
    favorable years.

# Sarracenia × moorei 'extreme red throat' Select

A Mike-bred F1 flava × leucophylla hybrid selected for extreme
red-throat development; the standout 'Select' individual
emerged from a batch of ~10 retained seedlings. Distinct from
S. × moorei 'Red Throat' Southern Baldwin Co, AL (C0331).
Hybrid vigor; capable of large traps with big lids; throat
color saturates as the trap ages and into fall. ~41 photos
spanning 2018-2025 (40 by Mike + 1 by Phil Long, TX).
