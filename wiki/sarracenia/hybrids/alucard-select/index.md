# Sarracenia 'Alucard' 'select'

A standout 'Alucard' grex selection (red rubricorpora × 'Royal
Ruby'). Sibling of the more famous 'Prince of Darkness' but
performs more easily — pushes 3-4'+ tall traps. Slow to
vegetative maturity, then rapid year-over-year size gains
when undivided + heavy-fed (sprouse58's NC clone hit 4'+
after 5 years undivided). 13 Mike photos (2019-2022) + 9
external links (other growers' year-over-year docs).
