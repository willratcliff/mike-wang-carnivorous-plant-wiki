# Sarracenia 'Golden Catesbaei' (SUPER YELLOW)

## Origin

A Mike-bred F1 cross: yellow *S. flava* var. *flava* × a yellowish
(less-red) *S. purpurea* ssp. *venosa*. Of ~60 seedlings, 6 stood
out as super yellow and were separately tagged + propagated.

## Standout traits

- Upright hood, super yellow.
- All 6 winners separately propagated (mother lines kept independent
  for future "winner" identification).
- Best traps in spring/summer; little late-summer/fall trap output
  observed so far.
- Compared by podsnfins to an Australian cultivar called 'Tina'.

## Cultivation notes

Full sun + undisturbed conditions required for full color. Mike
expected first availability ~July 2024.
