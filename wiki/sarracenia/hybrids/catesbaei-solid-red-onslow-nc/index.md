# Sarracenia × catesbaei 'solid red' Onslow Co, NC

## Origin

A **naturally-occurring wild hybrid** of *S. flava × S. purpurea ssp.
venosa* (= *S. × catesbaei*) from Onslow Co, NC. The locality data
matters: most catesbaeis in cultivation are man-made seedlings, so a
wild-origin clone with verified site information is rare and
breeding-valuable.

This is **not** an *S. × naczii* (rosea × flava) — Mike explicitly
calls out the distinction.

## Significance for Mike's breeding work

Per Mike's broader hybrid-origin hypothesis (thread 4294),
naturally-occurring catesbaeis like this one are plausibly the
ancestors of wild atropurpureas. If correct, you can use the
catesbaei as a starting point to recreate atropurpurea ex-situ:

> has the all red traits, and water it down enough....you can get
> the flava shape....hypothetically!"

Mike has used this clone in crosses in 2025 toward that goal.
