# Sarracenia 'Adrian Slack' × 'Judith Hindle' 'Select'

Mike-bred F1 hybrid retaining Adrian Slack's color/shape on a
Judith-Hindle-sized plant — much higher trap output than AS,
much easier to grow. Mike (2018) considers it cultivar-
registration-worthy. ~26 photos including ~22 Mike + 4 from
other growers (Ps3isawesome, kirkauburn, joossa, cocles); plus
2 photos of adaetz100's secondary cross with an oreophila.
