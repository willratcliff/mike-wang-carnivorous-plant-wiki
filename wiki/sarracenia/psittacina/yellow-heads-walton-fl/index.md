# Sarracenia psittacina 'yellow heads' Walton Co, FL

## Origin

A *S. psittacina* population from **Walton Co, FL** — Mike's third
attempt to keep Walton Co material alive. First attempt (>10 years
before 2024): rotted from over-watering. Second attempt: seeds were
misplaced in Mike's seed library and lost. Third attempt: pandemic-era
seed gift — currently in cultivation, slow-growing.

## Standout traits

- Yellow trap heads — striking, NOT anthocyanin-free.
- Walton Co historically produces unusually large 'wings' on traps
  and includes giant forms — visible in Mike's first batch a decade
  ago.

## Cultivation notes (psittacinas in Mike's NorCal climate)

- **Don't sit Walton-area psittacinas in water permanently** — Mike's
  rot loss came from this.
- Climate is borderline too cold; growth concentrated in fall heat
  waves.
- Late-summer 2023 cold snap burned most of Mike's fall psittacina
  traps — rhizomes/roots survived.
