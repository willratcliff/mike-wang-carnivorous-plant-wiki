# Sarracenia rosea f. luteola Baldwin Co, AL (Hanrahan F2)

Bob Hanrahan's AF rosea breeding line. Original wild AF plant stolen
from Hanrahan's property; he then grew 100,000+ seeds from surrounding
area to recover AF segregants. Mike's plants are F2 sibling crosses;
ABG preserves the lineage. Vigorous (atypical for AF roseas), eerie
green color, yellow-green flower.
