# Sarracenia rosea 'large, vigorous' Liberty Co, FL

A Mike-selected Liberty Co, FL rosea — large traps via vigor
rather than slow-growth genetic-giant pattern. Almost lost
after years of low priority; recovered via babying and divided
for the first time winter 2024-2025. More vibrantly colored
than other Liberty Co roseas Mike has compared. 8 Mike photos
(2019 and 2025).
