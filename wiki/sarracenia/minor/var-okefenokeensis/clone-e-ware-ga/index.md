# Sarracenia minor var. okefenokeensis 'Clone E' Ware Co, GA

## Origin

Mike's letter-designated selection from the Ware Co, GA okee
population. Notable for symmetric trap shape and bronzy-red head
tinge; capable of turning fully red in fall under direct-sun
exposure.

## Standout traits

- Excellent symmetry
- Pencil-thin face-on profile
- Bronzy-red head tinge year-round, full red in fall
- Small for an okee but historically capable of 2' traps
- Medium vigor; limited distribution as of 2013

## Cultivation notes

Outdoor Northern California. Fall red coloration is genetically
enabled but environmentally triggered — direct-sun on the trap
surface drives the expression.
