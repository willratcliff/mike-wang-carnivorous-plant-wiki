# Sarracenia minor var. okefenokeensis 'deep red, compact' Ware Co, GA

## Origin

A red okefenokeensis selection from **Ware Co, Georgia**. Technically
capable of giant okee size, but in Mike's climate it presents as a
"large red minor" with compact clump form.

## Standout traits

- Compact, clump-forming habit.
- Deep red color in full sun.
- Strong light dependency: sun-side red, shaded-side green on the
  same trap.
- Some summer color development possible — atypical (Mike usually
  sees this clone color only in fall/winter).

## Cultivation notes

Direct full sun is required for full color. Cooler conditions
(fall/winter) trigger expression in Mike's NorCal climate; LEDs or
deep-south climates would advance the color window.
