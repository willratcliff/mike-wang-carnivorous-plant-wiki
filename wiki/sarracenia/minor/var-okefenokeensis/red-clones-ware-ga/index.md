# Sarracenia minor var. okefenokeensis (red clones) Ware Co, GA

## Population overview

Ware Co is one of the two major *S. minor* var. *okefenokeensis*
populations Mike documents (the other being Charlton Co, GA).
Per-population trade-off:

- **Charlton Co clones** — size up more easily.
- **Ware Co clones** — color up more impressively.

This thread aggregates Mike's Ware Co red selections without
assigning individual cultivar names. Specific standout individuals
from this locality (e.g., 'Ultimate Red Giant') get their own
threads and entries.

## Standout traits (population-level)

- Strong red coloration, range from deep red to ultimate bronze.
- Smaller frame than Charlton Co kin — a feature, not a flaw, in
  trade.
- Color is strongly sun-dependent at the within-leaf level — shaded
  bands stay green.

## Cultivation notes

Mike grows these outdoors in NorCal. The 2025-12 update photos show
that with Mike's conditions, individual traps can still reach large
sizes despite the population's general size reputation.
