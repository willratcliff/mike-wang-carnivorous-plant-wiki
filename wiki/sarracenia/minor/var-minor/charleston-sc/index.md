# Sarracenia minor var. minor Charleston Co, SC

A standout Charleston Co minor population — most spectacular
windows of any minor in Mike's collection (thousands of clones
known). Thin veins between windows → very white traps.
sunbelle confirms not leucophylla introgression. Possibly only
one extraordinarily-windowed clone in the batch. 22 photos
(2022-2023).
