# Sarracenia minor var. minor Charlton Co, GA (different population)

The "regular size" Charlton Co var. minor population — distinct from
Mike's tall-form Charlton entry. Plants stay only a few inches at
maturity but show notable color-form variance, including red individuals.

## History

Mike posted this in July 2014 (post #1) noting the population had been
torturous to establish — 5+ years of stunted juvenile growth and
repeated botrytis outbreaks before the plants finally moved to adult
form. Once at adult stage the fungal threats subsided.

Updates through 2015, 2021, and 2022 confirm the established population
is robust and producing color-form variants. The 2015 update (post #5)
flagged "a very nice red one" worth tracking.

## Standout traits

- True regular var. minor size (a few inches at maturity)
- Color form variation including red individuals
- Botrytis-resistant once mature

## Cultivation notes

- Long juvenile establishment period (5+ years) is the most important
  cultivation note. Don't write off slow Charlton minor seedlings.
- Botrytis pressure is highest in juvenile stage; mature plants weather
  outdoor conditions reliably.
