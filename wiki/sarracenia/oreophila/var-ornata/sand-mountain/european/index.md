  become more colorful with better growing conditions, particularly
  if coaxed into producing fall pitchers.

## Cultivation notes

This is a 2013-era observation: at the time of introduction, Mike had
only recently acquired the clone and considered its growing-out period
ongoing. *S. oreophila* in general intensifies color under twin-wall
polycarbonate or greenhouse conditions.
