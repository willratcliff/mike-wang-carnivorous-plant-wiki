# Sarracenia oreophila var. ornata purple throat 'orange tinge'

A Mike-bred Sand Mountain ornata selection from the MK017 × UCB
cross, distinguished by heavy veins, a purple-ish throat, and a
characteristic orange tinge that develops as the trap ages. The
late-season orange/red coloration is consistent year over year,
suggesting genetic rather than environmental control. 12 Mike
photos (June and August 2018).
