# Sarracenia alata 'veinless' Harrison Co, MS

A Mike-bred Harrison Co alata — first documented veinless
Harrison Co alata. Multi-year selfing project (years 1 + 2
yielded false-positives; year 3 different mothers flowering →
success). Tall (~3'+), yellow-tinge under full sun, distinctive
green patches. 25 Mike photos (2020-2024).
