# Sarracenia alata 'DARK' Stone Co, MS

A Stone Co, MS alata selected (from grow-out of thousands of
seedlings) for stable dark-pigment expression. Fresh fall
pitchers start reddish and deepen to dark purplish/blackish as
they age; black band develops below the mouth; black veins on
lid. Reliable across multiple years where most "dark"
selections from Mike's grow-out failed to repeat. 20 Mike photos
(2018-2024).
