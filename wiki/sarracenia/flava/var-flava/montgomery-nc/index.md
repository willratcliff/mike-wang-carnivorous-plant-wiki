# Sarracenia flava var. flava (and maxima) Montgomery Co, NC

An inland Piedmont flava population (Uwharrie Mountains, sympatric
with the Montgomery venosa). Hilly habitat unusual for Carolina
flavas. Multiple sub-types (flava-flava + maxima) and consistently
yellow expression. 12 Mike photos (2022-2025).
