# Sarracenia flava var. rubricorpora 'Back Row Giant' Liberty Co, FL

A consistently 3+ ft Liberty Co rubricorpora that breaks the
typical year-on/year-off giant cycle — sizes up consecutively
when kept happy. Slow propagator. Mike's Back Row Giant ×
Burgundy F1 yielded a solid-red giant seedling with dark-black
interior. 18 Mike photos (2020-2024).
