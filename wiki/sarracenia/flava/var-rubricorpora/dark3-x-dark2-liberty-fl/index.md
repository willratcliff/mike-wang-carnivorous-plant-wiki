# Sarracenia flava var. rubricorpora (dark#3 × dark#2) Liberty Co, FL

## Origin

Mike's controlled cross of two dark Liberty Co, FL rubricorpora
parent clones from his collection — internally designated **dark #3**
and **dark #2**. Goal of the cross: produce *darker* offspring than
either parent (which didn't materialize) **or** produce some
*atropurpureas* (which did).

## Standout traits

- High-quality rubricorporas, color up more easily than unselected
  Liberty Co wild types.
- Post-cull batch: only red-tubed offspring retained.

## Liberty Co rubricorpora breeding pattern

Mike's broader observation: rubricorpora crosses out of Liberty Co
populations rarely produce 100% rubricorpora offspring. The
population carries mixed genes for many other variants. Expect to
cull aggressively for red-tubed phenotype.
