# Sarracenia flava var. ornata Iredell Co, NC

The last known surviving clone of the Iredell Co (and 3
adjacent western NC counties) flava lineage — wild populations
all extirpated. Solid bright-red veins, sparse but striking
expression, summer-dormant (oreophila-like). Conservation-
significant; NASC + landowner working on regional restoration.
10 Mike photos (2021-2023).
