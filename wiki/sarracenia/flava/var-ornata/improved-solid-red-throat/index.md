'improved' breeding line (~decade+ of selection for clean,
heavy red venation). One individual emerged with a SOLID red
throat instead of the line's signature heavily-veined throat;
its trap was also unusually red in 2018. 4 Mike photos
(2018-04-16). See parent line at `../improved/`.
