# Breeding history (Mike's 2019 deep-history post #60)
breeding_history: |
  Multi-generational breeding spanning 1997-present:
  
  - **1997**: Mike acquired SELFED seeds from the original mother
    black-veined flava. Art Junier acquired seeds from the same
    batch simultaneously; Mike + Art traded F2 clones. Mike
    eventually acquired all of Art's individuals as well.
  
  - **F2 generation (early-2000s through ~2012)**: extremely slow
    growing, finicky, rarely bloom, incredibly rot prone. Each
    individual slightly different — some more vigorous, some
    bloomed more readily. All had extreme external veining; varied
    in extent. The WORST-LOOKING F2s were the easiest to bloom and
    grow; the BEST-LOOKING F2s were the hardest. Mike used the
    worst-looking F2s as breeding material because they were the
    only ones producing pollen + seed pods.
  
  - **F3 generation = "Improved Black Veins"**: Mike crossed
    (Bulloch Clone B × Gulf Coast 'select') × Bulloch SFL05X.
    First documented 2013-08-17. Released to the general public
    after extensive selection.
  
  - **F4 (planned)**: cross the best F3 individual with F2 Clone E
    (the absolute best-looking but rot-prone individual). Mike has
    identified the F3 winner; Clone E has only flowered TWICE in 22
    years and pollen sacks rotted both times. Project setback by
    Mike's college, marriage, and 2 kids.
  
  - **Goals achieved (F3)**: vigor, slightly increased venation,
    increased disease resistance.
  - **Goal pending (F4)**: heavier venation matching the original
    F2 best-looking individuals.

# Sarracenia flava var. ornata 'Improved Black Veins' (IBV)

Mike's 3-generation breeding line (1997-present) intended to combine
the spectacular crystal-clear black-vein phenotype of the F2
Bulloch-Co originals with vigor sufficient for general cultivation.

## Origin

Wild origin: Bulloch Co, GA — possibly a Kennedy family property or
the powercut site (now destroyed, 2012). Original mother black-
veined flava likely dug from the wild decades ago.

## Breeding history

- **1997**: Mike + Art Junier acquire F2 selfed seeds from the
  original mother plant
- **F2 (1990s-2000s)**: extreme veining, extreme rot susceptibility,
  poor blooming
- **F3 = "Improved Black Veins"** (2013 release): (Bulloch Clone B
  × Gulf Coast 'select') × Bulloch SFL05X. Vigorous + slightly
  improved venation + rot-resistance.
- **F4 (planned)**: IBV F3 × F2 Clone E for heavier venation.
  Bottlenecked by Clone E's rare-and-rot-failed flowering.

## Standout traits

- True BLACK veins
- Crystal-clear veining
- Vigorous (relative to F2)
- Fast growing
- Up to 30" tall in mature select clones
- Phenotypically variable
