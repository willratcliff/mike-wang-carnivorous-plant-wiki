# Sarracenia flava var. ornata 'improved'

Mike Wang's multi-generation, decade+ ornata breeding line
selected for cleaner, heavier red venation, easier cultivation,
and more traps year-round. As of 2018-04, propagation was
slow; updated 2026-04 photos show continued expression of the
selected traits. 8 Mike photos (2018, 2019, 2026). 'improved
solid red throat' is a sub-selection from this line —
`../improved-solid-red-throat/`.
