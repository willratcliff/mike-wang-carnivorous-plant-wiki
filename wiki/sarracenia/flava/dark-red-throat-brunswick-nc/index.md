# Sarracenia flava 'dark red throat' Brunswick Co, NC

An extremely uncommon Brunswick Co flava — red-to-black throat
overlay on flava-flava interior veining. Same population as
'lemon yellow'. Throat trait expresses only on mature traps,
darkens with age. 10 photos (2021, 2024).
