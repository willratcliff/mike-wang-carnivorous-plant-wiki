# Sarracenia flava 'solid red throat' Eastern AL

An Eastern AL flava with unusual shape and a solid-red-throat
expression that takes work to coax out year-over-year.
Inconsistent aesthetic but useful for breeding — Mike confirms
this is one parent of S. × moorei 'red body, white top'
Eastern AL (C0413, the red-bodied Adrian-Slack-look-alike with
no AS genes). 20 Mike photos including 2 of the cross
offspring.
