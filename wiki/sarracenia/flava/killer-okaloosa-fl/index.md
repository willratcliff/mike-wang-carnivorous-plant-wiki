# Sarracenia flava 'Killer' Okaloosa Co, FL

## Origin

Wild origin Okaloosa Co, FL — Mike (post #6, 2012-07-03). calen
(post #15, 2016-07-05) reports learning the clone came from a
now-destroyed roadside site along Interstate 10 in Okaloosa. Mike's
working hypothesis: this is a complex-hybrid plant of likely atropurpurea
descent (post #8, 2012; post #18, 2022).

July. Cooler-climate growers (calen) report it's the latest flava
