# Sarracenia flava var. cuprea 'tendril' Brunswick Co, NC

A standout Brunswick Co, NC cuprea distinguished by a small
spur/tendril on the lid tip and an unusually well-defined red
throat — Mike (2020) considers a genuine Carolina cuprea with
this red throat development "definitely very rare in
cultivation." Coppery color persists vibrantly as traps age,
atypical for cuprea. 10 Mike photos (2019-2025). Acquisition
tag references NASC003.
