# Sarracenia flava var. rugelii 'YELLOW GIANT'

## Origin

Mike-bred F1 cross between two of his proven giant *S. flava* var.
*rugelii* clones. Out of a large seed batch, the smallest seedlings
were culled and the largest 10 were retained — YELLOW GIANT is one of
those 10. Sown ~2020 (3 years before the 2023 thread).

## Standout traits

- Striking yellow trap color.
- Mouth ~3" wide on traps barely a foot tall — classic giant signal.
- Heavy girth in cramped community-tray conditions.
- Mike rates this among the best-performing giant rugeliis in his
  collection — outcrossing yielded F1 vigor without being a true hybrid.

## Cultivation notes

Currently growing cramped; not yet at full size potential. Mike
expects significant size-up once given individual pot space.
