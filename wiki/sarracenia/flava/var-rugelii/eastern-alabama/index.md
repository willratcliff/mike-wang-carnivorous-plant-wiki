# Sarracenia flava var. rugelii Eastern Alabama

A multi-individual population accession from an unspecified
Eastern Alabama flava site — among the last remaining flava
populations in the state east of the central Conecuh National
Forest stands. About half of Mike's founder plants showed
abnormally large lids and heads ("possibly giant"); the
population also includes cuprea-coloration individuals Mike
attributes to deep-time moorei or atropurpurea introgression.
17 Mike photos (2020-2022). Distributed by Mike via division
to multiple growers ~2016-2018.
