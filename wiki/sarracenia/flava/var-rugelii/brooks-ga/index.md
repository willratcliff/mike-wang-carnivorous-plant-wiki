# Sarracenia flava var. rugelii Brooks Co, GA

## Origin

A Mike-held *S. flava* var. *rugelii* population from a large private
parcel in **Brooks Co, Georgia** — reportedly the largest extant
rugelii stand in GA. Mike has sampled from 3 different bogs on the
property. Recently entered cultivation. Same private property
referenced in the Brooks Co minor population entry (C0628).

Per nclarkii: managers burn annually for quail-hunting purposes —
flowers rarely persist to seed set, which complicates ongoing seed
acquisition.
