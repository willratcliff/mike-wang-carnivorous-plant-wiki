# Sarracenia flava var. rugelii 'Bob Hanrahan'

The historic, formally-registered Sarracenia cultivar — Bob
Hanrahan's wild collection from Colquitt Co, GA in the
1980s-1990s. Genetic giant: extraordinarily large lids, big
traps, well-defined red throat under greenhouse conditions.
Mike's outdoor NorCal cultivation: poor performance after 6
years (cool climate hates this clone). 15 photos: 8 Mike + 5
adaetz100 + 1 almightydolla + (CC archive shots by Mike). Mike
is breeding it via cross with rugelii Thomas Co, GA giant for
hybrid vigor in cool climates.
