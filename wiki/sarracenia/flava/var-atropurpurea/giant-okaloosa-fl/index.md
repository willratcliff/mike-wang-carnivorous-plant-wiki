# Sarracenia flava var. atropurpurea 'GIANT' Okaloosa Co, FL (multi-clone)

giant atropurpurea selections from Okaloosa Co, FL. At least 3
giant individuals confirmed in 2019; sub-named clones include
'GIANT Type' (2022) and 'Elegant' (2023). Some clones show
black veins on lids — potential precursor for a black-veined
