# Sarracenia flava var. atropurpurea (Carolina Clone)

## Origin

A rare Carolina-source flava var. atropurpurea individual in Mike's
collection. Mike held the plant for more than a decade by 2012,
making the original acquisition pre-2002 [VERIFY]. Specific wild
locality is not stated in the thread.

This clone is distinct from 'Waccamaw' (which has its own thread,
C0119) — both are Carolina atropurpureas, but Mike treats them as
separate accessions with separate growth and distribution histories.

## Standout traits

- **Solid red interior** under optimal conditions.
- **Year-to-year phenotypic variability** including shape.
- **Slow-growing, low-division-rate** Carolina-form atropurpurea.

## Cultivation notes

Patience required. Division rate is among the lowest in Mike's
collection. Color is environment-dependent; some seasons produce
markedly less red traps even on the same plant.
