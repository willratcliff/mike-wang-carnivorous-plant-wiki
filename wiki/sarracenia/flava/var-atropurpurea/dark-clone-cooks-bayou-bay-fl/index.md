# Sarracenia flava var. atropurpurea 'dark clone' Cook's Bayou, Bay Co, FL

## Origin

A dark red *S. flava* var. *atropurpurea* from **Cook's Bayou,
Bay Co, FL**. Mike reports the site has largely been converted to
slash pine plantation, with only relic patches remaining of what was
once a vast Sarracenia field.

## Suspected introgression

Mike places "atropurpurea" in quotes here because the flowers are
**orangey-red** rather than the more typical pure red — suggestive of
prior introgression rather than a clean atropurpurea. Mike has not
named the suspected pollen donor.

## Standout traits

- 2023 traps reached a depth of red Mike had not previously seen on
