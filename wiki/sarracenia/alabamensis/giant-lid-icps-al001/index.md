  Mike's conservation framing: "a hybrid can be made anytime, and
  takes more or less than 5 years to reach maturity. The genetic
  diversity that exists in the wild took 10's of 1000's of years
  to make, so with the relatively little time we have on this
  earth, the best benefit Sarracenia can get from man kind is to
  preserve its current genetic diversity."
