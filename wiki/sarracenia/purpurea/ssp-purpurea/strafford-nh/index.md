# Sarracenia purpurea ssp. purpurea Strafford Co, NH

## Origin

A small wild-source population of *S. purpurea ssp. purpurea* from
Strafford Co, NH. Mike has had it for several years; ~1/3 of the
original plants survive as of 2026-02-28. Mike noticed in winter
2026 that one of the surviving plants produced near-black old traps
— "Holy Filoli historic house and Gardens!"

## The crash-population problem

Mike's purpurea collection splits into two categories:
- **30-year survivors** — populations he's held for ~3 decades.
- **5-year crashers** — populations that thrive briefly and then
  rot out en masse.

The Strafford NH batch reads as a **5-year-crash** type.

## Conservation strategy

Mike's contingency for crash-prone populations:
- Try to take divisions from surviving mother plants. Strafford NH:
  unsuccessful so far.
- If divisions can't be taken before the population fully crashes,
  cross-pollinate with other dark ssp. purpurea to preserve the
  genetics in hybrid form.

Mike's track record on this strategy: see the AF venosa story
(cluster C0748) — he saved AF venosa alleles by outcrossing before
all his original AF venosas died.
