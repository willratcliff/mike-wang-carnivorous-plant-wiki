# Sarracenia purpurea ssp. venosa 'Brunswick Beauty' Brunswick Co, NC

A famous venosa cultivar with a complex history: Mike's original
clone (slow, difficult, spectacular) lost in his 2014 move; a
current circulating clone (Jeremiah-Harris-sourced) is more
vigorous and likely a sister rather than the original. Mike's
pre-2014 BB × Woolworth-venosa F1 cross resurfaced in 2024 as
'Big Fib' (Mike Barnard's clone, traced via Jonathan Kui).
