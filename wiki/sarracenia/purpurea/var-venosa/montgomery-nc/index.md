# Sarracenia purpurea ssp. venosa Montgomery Co, NC

A genetically extraordinary purpurea population from a protected
inland Piedmont bog (Uwharrie Mountains) — extremely diverse from
a small wild sample. Multiple distinct phenotypes including
catesbaei-dominant, montana-like, yellowish, and solid-red-
bulbous. Possible ancestral connection to S. purpurea var. montana.
Sympatric flava + catesbaei at site explain trait diversity.
Spread to Europe via Kilian. ~70 photos (2021-2024).
