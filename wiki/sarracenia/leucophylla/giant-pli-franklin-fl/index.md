# Sarracenia leucophylla GIANT 'PLI' (Purple Lips Imposter) Franklin Co, FL

A Franklin Co, FL leucophylla originally acquired as 'Purple
Lips' but later identified as an imposter — turned out to be a
worthy plant in its own right. Capable of GIANT traps under
mature growth. Heavy phenotypic variance: same clone can produce
purple-lipped, red-lipped, and leucophylla-RED-looking traps in
the same clump. 9 Mike photos spanning 2017-2020.
