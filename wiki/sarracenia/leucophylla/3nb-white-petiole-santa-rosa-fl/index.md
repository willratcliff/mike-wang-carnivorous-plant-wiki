# Sarracenia leucophylla 3nb 'white Petiole' Santa Rosa Co, FL

A Santa Rosa Co leucophylla with stable extra-white petiole
expression — Mike notes it reminds him of Hurricane Creek White
but isn't quite that. Possibly var. alba (unconfirmed). Notable
2017 fall expression: body went almost neon yellow. 7 Mike
photos (2018, 2024).
