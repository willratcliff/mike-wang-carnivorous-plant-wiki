# Sarracenia leucophylla var. alba '2" stretch' Washington Co, AL

The stretchiest leuco var. alba in Mike's collection — white
pigment extends ~2" down the petiole. Yellow-flowered. Mike
(2018) staked a claim that this clone could be Adrian-Slack-
class for breeding extra-stretchy white leuco hybrids. Fall
traps progressively whiter; vigor decent. 15 Mike photos
(2018-2021).
