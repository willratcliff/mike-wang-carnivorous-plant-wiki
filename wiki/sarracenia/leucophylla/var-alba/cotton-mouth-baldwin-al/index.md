# Sarracenia leucophylla var. alba 'Cotton Mouth' Baldwin Co, AL

A classic chameleon Baldwin Co alba — usually performs as a
'regular leucophylla' but under optimal conditions can produce
ridiculously white traps (arguably whiter than HCW per Mike).
Vigorous; white-interior trait is dominant; exterior whitening
is the breeding-significant recessive trait. 3 Mike photos
(single 2019-09-28 post).
