# Sarracenia leucophylla var. alba 'Relic Hurricane Creek White' Baldwin Co, AL

A genetically distinct sibling-lineage HCW-site relic clone
from the surviving easement of the historical Hurricane Creek
bog (most of which was destroyed for pine plantation). One of
the few new collections of HCW-site genetics introduced to
cultivation in decades. 3 Mike photos (2018-09-23). Includes
Mike's substantial site-history and conservation prose.
