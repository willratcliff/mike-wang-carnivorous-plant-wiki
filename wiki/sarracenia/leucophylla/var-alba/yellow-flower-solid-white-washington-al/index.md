A premier Washington Co, AL alba — among the whitest leucophyllas
in cultivation, rivals HCW clone F. Yellow flower (uncommon in
leucophylla; possibly indicating distant alata introgression).
Strong breeding parent with dominant solid-white trait. 23 Mike
photos spanning 2017-2020.

## Standout traits

- **Among whitest leucos in cultivation.**
- **Yellow flower** — distinctive trait.
- **Abundant solid-white summer traps** — atypical for leucophylla.
- **Solid-white trait DOMINANT in breeding.**
- **Burns under low-humidity heatwaves**; humidity matters.
