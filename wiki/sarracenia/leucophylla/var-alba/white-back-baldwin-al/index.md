# Sarracenia leucophylla var. alba 'white back' Baldwin Co, AL

A Baldwin Co alba with a strikingly asymmetric white expression:
white pigment region dominates the back side of the trap with
minimal front-side white. White builds with trap age. 3 Mike
photos (2018-10-03).
