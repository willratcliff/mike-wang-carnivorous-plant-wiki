# Sarracenia leucophylla var. alba 'Tall, shapely' Baldwin Co, AL

A Baldwin Co, AL alba — chameleon clone with the 'netting' /
vein pattern in white pigments that can disappear under right
conditions to leave solid white. One of Mike's easier-to-color-up
chameleons. Late-season durability beyond typical leucophylla
dormancy. Mike's 2018 speculation: could exceed HCW under
optimal conditions. 30 Mike photos spanning 2017-2020.
