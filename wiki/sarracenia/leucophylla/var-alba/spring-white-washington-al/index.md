# Sarracenia leucophylla var. alba yellow flower 'spring white' Washington Co, AL

A standout Washington Co alba yellow-flower clone Mike calls
'spring white' for its consistent, bright white spring traps —
unusual among leucos, most of which need warmer spring weather
to perform. Cool-climate candidate. Fall traps reach extreme
whiteness once the plant is in fresh medium and undisturbed.
Heavy 2018 seed-set (~2,000 seeds) on a single year. 34 Mike
photos spanning 2018-2023.
