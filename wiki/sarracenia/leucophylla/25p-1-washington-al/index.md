# Sarracenia leucophylla 25P-1 Washington Co, AL

Co, AL leuco population. The mother plant is somewhere in Mike's

## Standout traits

- Whiter than typical for the population, but not alba — uncommon
  combination.
- White intensifies as traps mature (consistent in Mike's experience).
- Some red below the white area — Mike unsure if stable.
- Fast grower, standard population shape.

## Cultivation notes

character is reliably amplified at vegetative maturity, *provided*
growing conditions support full color development.
