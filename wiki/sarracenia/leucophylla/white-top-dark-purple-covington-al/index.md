# Sarracenia leucophylla 'white top dark purple' Covington Co, AL

## Origin

A wild-type *S. leucophylla* selected from thousands within Mike's
Covington Co, AL material. Mike states this is the only Covington
clone with the **bright-white-top + dark-purple-body** combination,
making it stand out at a distance.

## History

Held by Mike for "a very long time" before the 2022 thread; nearly
lost on multiple occasions. Around 2019 a tiny side shoot was salvaged
and re-propagated; by Oct 2022 there were a few divisions in the
collection and Mike posted a division for sale. A second clone with
similar dark traits was flagged in Oct 2022 — its eventual maturity
will determine if it warrants a separate accession.

## Standout traits

- White top + dark purple body, high contrast.
- Color shift: reddish on just-opened traps → dark purple with age.
- Color intensifies as plants reach maturity.

## Cultivation notes

Near-rot history; Mike's process was to keep the salvaged side shoot
as the gating "only division" until copies were made.
