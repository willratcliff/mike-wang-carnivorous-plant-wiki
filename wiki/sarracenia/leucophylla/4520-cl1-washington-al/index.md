# Sarracenia leucophylla #4,520 cl#1 Washington Co, AL

A Wash. Co, AL leucophylla in distribution under Mike's
accession-code naming scheme (a system Mike later regretted).
Tall, with strong vein patterns and asymmetric sun/shade white
expression on aged traps. Visible alata influence per other
growers. 17 Mike photos (2018-2024). Sidorian's Ohio cultivation
photos (2019) are broken external links and not mirrored.
