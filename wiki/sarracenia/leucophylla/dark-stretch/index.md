# Sarracenia leucophylla RED 'DARK STRETCH'

## Origin

Mike-bred selection from a multi-generational breeding program aimed
at *S. leucophylla* clones that produce impressive spring traps —
unusual, since most leucos produce only minor, lackluster spring
pitchers. After many failures and several generations of selection,
DARK STRETCH emerged as a clear winner. Mike has confirmed (post #3)
that it does **not** carry Dark Red Covington Co, AL ancestry.

- Best RED leucophylla in Mike's collection for spring traps —

- Giant — traps reach ~1 meter, Mike's threshold for "giant".
- Aged traps: white-ish interior + reddened lips.
