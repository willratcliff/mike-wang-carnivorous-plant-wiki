selected by Mike from a hundreds-of-plants batch as the only
individual with this exact color combination. Slow grower but
extraordinary phenotype with potential breeding value (per Mike,
the genetics could enable novel black-bodied / white-lipped
hybrids if crossed appropriately). 11 Mike photos (2017-2019).
