## Standout trait

The defining feature: **even small immature traps look exceptional**
on this clone. Most leucophyllas need to reach mature trap size
(typically fall) to look good; this one breaks that pattern,
producing top-notch form on early-season small divisions. Mike has
not seen another leuco clone like it.

Secondary traits:
