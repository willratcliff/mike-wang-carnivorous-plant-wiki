# Sarracenia leucophylla DARK RED (WRR × DARK RED Covington Co, AL) — breeding line

Mike-bred F1 cross of S. leucophylla 'Wilkerson's Red Rocket'
(C0027, mother) × 'DARK RED' Covington Co, AL (C0371, father).
Heavy seedling segregation (DARK RED Covington is recent
hybrid origin); after culling, retained standouts include
'DARK best clone' (likely darkest leuco), a white-lipped
individual (with own thread 4806), and a double-fall-flush
seedling. 16 photos: 15 Mike + 1 drummer230. Goal: more red
than any other leuco in cultivation, AND that red has to be
DARK.
