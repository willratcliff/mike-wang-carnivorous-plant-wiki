# Sarracenia leucophylla 'Super Pink' Baldwin Co, AL

A standout pink leucophylla from Baldwin Co, AL — Mike's best
pink leuco out of ~40 in his collection. Electric pink interior
with crisp white top contrast; cilia on the body. Yellow flowers
(unusual in leucophylla — discovered 2018). Pink-interior trait
dominant in breeding. Cultivar-status candidate per Mike's
framing. 20 Mike photos spanning 2017-2020.
