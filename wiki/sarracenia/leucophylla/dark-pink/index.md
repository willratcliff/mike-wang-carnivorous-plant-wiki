# Sarracenia leucophylla 'Dark Pink' (Mike's seed-grown clone, locality unknown)

## Origin

A leucophylla clone Mike grew from seed years before 2012; the
seed parent and wild source are not recorded. Mike (post #7,
2012) speculates possible alata or rosea introgression in the
distant ancestry based on morphology.

The clone is genetically distinct from all other pink leucophyllas
in cultivation (Mike, post #15, 2024) — Phil Sheridan's pink
clone, Botanique's pink clone, leucophylla 'Burgundy', and Mike's
other pink leucophyllas are all separate. This makes the clone
particularly valuable for breeding new pink leucophyllas with
hybrid vigor.
