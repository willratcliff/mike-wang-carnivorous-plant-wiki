# Sarracenia leucophylla 'white abaxial' Baldwin Co, AL

A Baldwin Co leucophylla originally selected for a very-white
back side; sun-orientation-dependent expression varies year over
year. Mike leans toward calling it var. alba. Vigorous; strong
stretch potential; surprised observers in 2020 with red-lips
expression. 13 Mike photos (2018-2022).
