# Sarracenia leucophylla RED (WRR × Dark Covington Co, AL) — breeding line

Mike-bred F1 batch from S. leucophylla 'Wilkerson's Red
Rocket' × 'DARK RED' Covington Co, AL. ~1000 seeds → ~20
stunning red selections. Notable retained individuals:
'Red and Yellow' (golden body + red venation, suggesting
historic moorei influence per Mike's 2019 hypothesis) and
'select' (the most-vigorous individual, ~3X sibling size).
Mike (2019) considers this his best red leucophylla cross to
date. ~20 photos: 18 Mike + 2 mahlon ('Red and Yellow'
distributed 2025).
