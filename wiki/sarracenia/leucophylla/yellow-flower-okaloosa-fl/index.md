# Sarracenia leucophylla 'yellow flower' Okaloosa Co, FL

The first reported yellow-flowered S. leucophylla from
Okaloosa Co, FL — yellow flowers are extremely rare in this
species. Not anthocyanin-free; produces normal red pigments in
the pitcher. 3 Mike photos (2019-04-20).
