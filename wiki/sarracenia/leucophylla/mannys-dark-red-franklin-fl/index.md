# Sarracenia leucophylla 'Manny's Dark Red' Franklin Co, FL

A red Franklin Co, FL leucophylla — named for Manny H. — that
expresses its strongest color in late fall under cool nights.
Documented from 2014 to 2018 (13 Mike photos).

## Origin

Wild-origin *S. leucophylla* from Franklin County, FL — the
easternmost extent of the species' range. Per Mike (post 1,
2014-09-14), Franklin Co produces some of the best red leucophylla
variants, and they share characteristic growth patterns: moderate
spring/summer traps with peak color in late fall.

The clone is named for "Manny H." — forum user 'mannyh' — who
appears to be the selector/introducer ([VERIFY]; Mike doesn't
state this explicitly, but mannyh's brief reply to the thread
