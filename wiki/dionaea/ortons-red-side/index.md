# Dionaea muscipula 'Orton's Red Side'

A Mike-cultivation Dionaea selection from a 2002-2003 Orton's
Plantation NC seed batch, originally grown by Dr. William Ratcliff and
transferred to Mike Wang in 2005. After 6+ years of severe neglect,
the strongest survivor revealed a distinctive trait: red exterior
trap pigmentation combined with green petiole and somewhat green
interior — distinct from typical 'all-red' VFT cultivars.
